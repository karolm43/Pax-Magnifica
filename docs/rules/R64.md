## 64. Key Game Moments — Public Presentation (Animations for Everyone) (2026-11-16)
📎 tirules: [Leaders](../rules_tirules/R_leaders.md) · [The Fracture](../rules_tirules/R_fracture.md) · [Breakthroughs](../rules_tirules/R_breakthroughs.md)

User decision 2026-11-16: important game moments are shown to ALL players as a short animation
(networked: every browser; local mode: human at the table). This is a presentation layer — it changes
no rules; the engine only exposes a public fact in the Activity feed (`core/activity.py`).

### 64.1 Source: public feed facts
- A moment is triggered by a NEW Activity feed entry (WebSocket push in networked mode). Entries present when opening
  the page are not new; undone entries (redo branch) do not count — same as `ActivityToasts`.
- All facts below are public information (leader state, board, breakthrough lie faceup on the table);
  hand contents never trigger a moment.

### 64.2 Moments
| Moment | Feed fact | Rule source |
|---|---|---|
| Faction hero used | `leader` with `detail = "purged"` or `effect` `leaders:<hero>`, leader type `hero` | R_leaders: "A hero is purged after its abilities are resolved" |
| The Fracture enters play | `fracture_entered` (`player_id` = who brought it in) | R_fracture: "On a result of 1 or 10, The Fracture enters play. Other game effects may also bring The Fracture into play." |
| Faction breakthrough gained | `breakthrough_unlocked` | R_breakthroughs |
| Custodians token removed | `custodians_removed` (+ VP from `vp` fact) | RULES.md 52 |

- God-Mode adjustments (`kind = godmode`) do not trigger the hero/breakthrough/Custodians moment (it is state editing,
  not a play). EXCEPTION: Fracture entry from God-Mode is shown (the operator intentionally brings it into play).
- The Fracture moment after a d10 roll (R_fracture) waits until the physical die lands (3D scene) — roll first,
  then outcome.
- The `adminFastMode` setting (fast tests) skips animations; the feed and notifications keep working.

### 64.3 Phase and round transitions
- Full-screen phase banners (graphics) removed (user decision). Instead, a quick transition
  bar (~1.6 s, `pointer-events: none`): phase name + round number; a new round (entering the strategy
  phase) has a stronger "ROUND N" accent.
- Passing the turn to this browser's player: short "YOUR TURN" bar (same mechanism).

### 64.4 Hero: scene + board effect (user decision 2026-11-16)
- The hero scene uses the FACTION PORTRAIT from the faction selection scene (`FactionPortraitLayer`, same framing as
  the victory screen: character at the left and bottom edges), not the leader graphic (too small, poor quality).
- After the scene, the board "reacts" to the hero's ability (layer inside map content, follows zoom; subtle,
  ~4–5 s). Single queue with moments; "Skip"/Esc in the scene skips to the effect, on the board ends the moment.
- Recipes (`KeyMoments/heroBoardFx.ts`, `HERO_BOARD_FX`) — heroes implemented in the engine:

Not played when a hero swap carried the token with its tile.

- New hero in the engine => entry in `HERO_BOARD_FX` (guards: `heroBoardFx.test.ts` and backend
  `tests/test_hero_board_fx_guard.py`, which compares heroes with engine hooks against the recipes).
  Without an entry, only the scene plays.

### 64.4a Relic board effect: Stellar Converter (user decision 2026-11-26)
- Using *Stellar Converter* (Activity entry with `relic` lost `stellarconverter` + `planet_destroyed`) plays a board
  effect for everyone, WITHOUT a portrait scene: a caption at the top (relic name, destroyed planet, player) and
  `RELIC_BOARD_FX.stellarconverter` (~8 s, skippable, `adminFastMode` skips it).
- Style deliberately differs from Nova Seed (hot supernova of a star): cold energy. The player's war suns /
  dreadnoughts / flagships in systems adjacent to the target charge up, beams lance into the PLANET, a lattice field
  envelops and squeezes it, the crust cracks, then flash + quake, shards, a flat debris ring and an ash belt.
  Sounds from `battleSound` (charge, assault cannon, sunfire, reversed blast, heavy explosion, ground blast).
- The destroyed planet token stays hidden until the flash (`useHeroFx.veiledPlanets`); Skip reveals it at once.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION (visual only): beam sources are picked on the client (war sun > dreadnought > flagship of the player
  in adjacent tiles; else the nearest such ship; else a beam from above) — the engine already validated BOMBARDMENT.
