## 55. Generic technologies as hooks — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules C_technology, assets) / ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Technology](../../../../rules_tirules/R_technology.md) · [Technology notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_technology.md) · [Unit Upgrades](../../../../rules_tirules/R_unit_upgrades.md) · [Component Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_component_action.md) · [Abilities](../../../../rules_tirules/R_abilities.md)

Scope: factionless technologies (base/pok/codex1-4). Text and parameters from assets (`assets/data/technologies/pok.json`), clarifications from `C_technology`. Effect = hook on a base rule hook point (`core/hooks/technologies*.py`), zero `if tech == ...` in base rules. Card versions (X-89, Magen) selected by `technology.generic_pool` (highest errata: codex4 > codex1 > base).

**55.1 Turn action budget (`core/action_budget.py`).** Base 1 action per turn; hook point `turn.actions` (MODIFY). *Fleet Logistics* (`fl`): 2 actions. All "can an action be taken" gates (strategic, tactical, component) and action registration go through `require_free_action` / `register_action`. From C_technology: "start/end of turn" events do NOT fire between actions (TURN END only on `end_turn`); cannot pass in a turn in which an action was performed; the second action is not forced (may end turn after one). The second action requires the first to be settled (no active tactical action or multi-step primary). `took_action_this_turn` remains the overarching flag ("at least one action"), `actions_this_turn` is the counter.

**55.2 Status phase.** Hook points `status.action_cards` (base 1) and `status.command_tokens` (base 2). *Neural Motivator* (`nm`): +1 card, only in the "Draw Action Cards" step of the status phase (not Politics or laws). *Hyper Metabolism* (`hm`): +1 token (capped by reinforcements `cc_reinf`).

**55.3 Space Cannon / Bombardment.** `space_cannon.roll_cannon` is the sole place for Space Cannon rolls (Offense and Defense). *Antimass Deflectors* (`amd`): hook point `space_cannon.roll_modifier` queried for the TARGET's owner, −1 to each die result (both phases: Offense and Defense — C_technology). *Plasma Scoring* (`ps`): hook points `space_cannon.extra_dice` and `bombardment.extra_dice`; ONE extra die per use (not per unit), "may" ability: PLAYER SPECIFIES the unit (`plasma_unit` parameter = `SpaceCannonShooter.key` for Space Cannon Offense/Defense, `BombardAssignment.key` = type|planet for bombardment); without specification, no extra die; in Space Cannon Defense separately per planet; in bombardment the extra die targets the same planet as the unit rolling it. The roll log carries `modifier` (Antimass) and `extraDice` (Plasma Scoring) so UI can show both.

**55.4 Movement and production.** *Light/Wave Deflector* (`lwd`): PERMIT `movement.pass_foreign_ships`. *Sarween Tools* (`st`): −1 to the total cost of the FIRST order in a PRODUCTION use (`production_used == 0`); no discount for "produce N units" grants without unit PRODUCTION (Freelancers, Sling Relay, Integrated Economy); `standalone` grants (Warfare secondary, Minister of Industry) are dock PRODUCTION uses — the discount applies (per 57.3). Cost does not drop below 0. The production window shows the discount with the card name (`production.cost_reduction_sources`).

**55.5 Unit upgrades (`ws`, `cr2`, `dd2`, `cv2`, `pds2`, `dn2`).** Stats and numeric abilities (cost, combat, move, capacity, Space Cannon, Bombardment, Planetary Shield, shield-disabling) come from the unit variant (`combat.resolve_unit_variant`) → covered by data (`rules_hooks.declare_data_driven`). ⚠ Unmodeled text abilities: Direct Hit immunity (dn2), Infantry II revival dice, "fighters outside capacity" (ff2/sd2).

**55.6 Combat and movement (`core/hooks/technologies_combat.py`).**
- *Gravity Drive* (`gd`): +1 to the move value of ONE ship in a tactical action. The bonus is enabled by the PLAYER (`gravity_drive=True`); applies only where movement is illegal without it, and only for a group of 1 ship (`movement.move_fleet`, `GameState.gravity_drive_used` flag, reset on activation and end of action). Range preview (`legal_move_sources`) accounts for the bonus while unused.
- *Duranium Armor* (`da`): mandatory once per combat round (`COMBAT_ROUND` END event, "combat" phase, not AFB step), even if the opponent scored no hits; repairs 1 damaged unit participating in combat that did not use Sustain Damage this round (from damaged units we subtract those in `sustained` for that round).
- *Assault Cannon* (`asc`): mandatory at the start of space combat, when a side has ≥3 non-fighter ships (condition checked at resolution time, so an earlier opponent hook can remove it; order per `resolution_order`) the opponent destroys 1 non-fighter ship; Sustain Damage does not protect. If a side loses its last units, combat ends immediately (`battle.start_combat`).
- *Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ* (`md`, codex4 version): (a) system activation by ANYONE with your structures → 1 infantry per structure, on the same planet; (b) start of ground combat on a planet with your structure → 1 hit to opponent's ground forces (C_technology Ω/ΩΩ: owner chooses the SPECIFIC unit — type and damage state; ONLY that unit may cancel the hit with its Sustain Damage, decided by its owner in the combat-start hit assignment step — `battle.add_start_hits(..., damaged=, chooser=)`; last ground force destroyed = immediate loss). Replaced by errata: `md_base`, `md_c1`.
- *X-89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ* (`x89c4`): hook point `combat.hit_multiplier` ×2 for GROUND combat and bombardment rolls (ground forces fighting as ships in space do not double); every planet against which BOMBARDMENT was used is exhausted. Replaced: `x89`, `x89_base`.

**55.7 Player choices in technologies — no automations (user decision 2026-11-09, section 56).** Assault Cannon victim, Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ target, unit repaired by Duranium Armor, and Infantry II return planet are chosen BY THE PLAYER via a mandatory offer; the engine applies the result automatically only when there is exactly one legal answer. ⚠ Remaining simplifications: (a) X-89 ΩΩ - bombarding a planet with no ground forces: see 57.8; (b) Magen ΩΩ skips a structure in space (Saar); (c) Gravity Drive activates exclusively on player request ("Use Gravity Drive" toggle in movement panel, `gravity_drive` in `/tactical/move` and range preview); (d) hooks of one event check conditions in resolution order, but player decisions are deferred, so two Assault Cannons in the same combat do not see each other's effects (C_technology: ≥3 ships condition checked after the first one's decision).
**55.8 Passive/mandatory technologies (`core/hooks/technologies_passive.py`).**
- *Dark Energy Tap* (`det`): hook point `retreat.anywhere` (PERMIT) - retreat to an adjacent system without any other player's units (ships, ground forces, structures), including empty ones (no entry in state) and without own presence. ⚠ NOT covered: the "explore a frontier token after a tactical action" part requires frontier exploration (RULES.md 46.3, out of scope).
- *Psychoarchaeology* (`pa`): hook point `technology.specialty_without_exhaust` (PERMIT) - specialties work without exhausting planets, even on exhausted ones (C_technology: each planet covers at most 1 symbol). ⚠ NOT covered: action "exhaust a planet with a specialty → 1 TG in the action phase" (player action, requires UI).
- *Self-Assembly Routines* (`sar`): after your mech is destroyed +1 TG per mech (even when card is exhausted). "Place a mech after PRODUCTION (exhaust card)" - "may" offer, see 57.5 and 57.8.
- *Infantry II* (`inf2`): after infantry is destroyed, die roll (physical die on table, `dice.record_table_roll`), 6+ → unit on card (`PlayerState.infantry_ii_on_card`); at the start of your next turn (`TURN START` event and action phase `PHASE START` for the first player) returns to a planet in your home system, or without a controlled planet at home - to reinforcements (C_units: Infantry). Return planet is chosen by the player SEPARATELY for each piece ("place each unit that is on this card on a planet you control in your home system"; identical slots `planet_1..N`, map click; mandatory offer when having ≥2 planets at home). Also applies to Letani Warrior II and Spec Ops II.
- *Space Dock II* (`sd2`), *Dreadnought II* (`dn2`): covered by data (capacity 3 fighters and PRODUCTION +4 from unit variant). ⚠ Dreadnought II "cannot be destroyed by Direct Hit" - Direct Hit card has no effect yet; once coded it must skip `dn2`.
- *Fighter II* (`ff2`): movement and stats from data; "fighters beyond capacity count toward fleet pool" - see 57.8.
