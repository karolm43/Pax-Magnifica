## 12. Planet Control — Single Source of Truth (Option A) — ✅ DESIGN DECISION

📎 tirules: [Control](../../../../rules_tirules/R_control.md) · [Planets](../../../../rules_tirules/R_planets.md) · [Invasion](../../../../rules_tirules/R_invasion.md) · [Coexistence](../../../../rules_tirules/R_coexistence.md)

**Rule:** `SystemState.planet_owners` (planet control within its system) is the SINGLE
source of truth for who controls a planet. A player's planet list in web-data
(`playerData.planets`) and economy are COMPUTED from `planet_owners`
(`web_data._controlled_planets_from_systems`). God-Mode editing of a player's planet list
(`/player/{id}/edit` with `controlled_planets`) is synchronized to systems' `planet_owners`
(`app._sync_controlled_planets`). Editing ownership in a system
(`/system/{pos}/planet` with `owner_id`) is immediately reflected in the panel (because the panel computes
from `planet_owners`).

**Why (Bug 3):** Previously, two independent sources existed — `player.controlled_planets`
(panel) and `SystemState.planet_owners` (map) — and editing one did not update
the other. The panel showed a planet, while the map had no owner (and vice versa). Option A
reflects the game model: a planet physically resides in a system and the system "knows" who
controls it; it also provides a natural foundation for future conquest/invasion logic.
