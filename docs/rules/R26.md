## 26. Unit placement when moving: ship↔space, ground forces↔planet — ✅ VERIFIED

📎 tirules: [Units](../../../../rules_tirules/R_units.md) · [Ships](../../../../rules_tirules/R_ships.md) · [Ground Forces](../../../../rules_tirules/R_ground_forces.md) · [Structures](../../../../rules_tirules/R_structures.md) · [Transport](../../../../rules_tirules/R_transport.md)

TI4 rule (LRR /wiki/Units, /wiki/Movement; consistent with RULES.md 2b classification): a unit
only exists in a zone matching its type.
- **Ships** (`fighter`, `destroyer`, `cruiser`, `carrier`, `dreadnought`, `flagship`,
  `warsun`) exist EXCLUSIVELY in system SPACE (`"space"`) — never on a planet.
- **Ground forces and structures** (`infantry`, `mech`, `pds`, `spacedock`) exist EXCLUSIVELY
  on a PLANET (zone = planet id) — never in space.

**Enforcement (not just UX):** unlike other God-Mode edits (assistant, not referee), we
ENFORCE this rule in `core`, because "a ship on a planet" is physically unrepresentable and
would break rendering/control. `board_edit.move_units` rejects (`ValueError` → HTTP 422) any
move violating placement. Decision function: `unit_catalog.placement_allows_zone(base_type, zone)`
— a pure world rule, called by the UI (drag-and-drop) and the future BOT (same entry point,
so the bot cannot place a ship on a planet either).

**Drag-and-drop UX (presentation, not a rule):** dropping a SHIP onto a planet is not an error
for the player — the frontend targets ORBIT (`"space"`) of that same tile instead (the player
indicated the system). The backend still validates strictly — this is just a convenient gesture
mapping to the allowed zone.

**⚠️ TRANSPORT EXCEPTION (Block B2) — ground forces/fighters IN SPACE during movement.**
The "ground forces only on a planet" rule applies to the RESTING state (setup, God-Mode edits).
In a TACTICAL ACTION, ground forces and fighters ride on a ship as CARGO and remain in SPACE
of the destination system until the **Commit Ground Forces** step (invasion), when they land
on a planet (source: `R_transport.md`, `R_invasion.md` — "Units are considered to be on the
planet for game effects from the Commit Ground Forces step onward"). Therefore, during MOVEMENT
the frontend does NOT block infantry/mechs dropped onto ORBIT of the active system (previously
`resolveTarget valid=false` — that was a bug). The "ground forces not on orbit" block still
applies OUTSIDE a tactical action (God-Mode: `board_edit.move_units` + `placement_allows_zone`).
Transport capacity is still validated by `move_fleet` (RULES.md 32.3).

Implementation: `unit_catalog.placement_allows_zone`/`zone_is_space`, `board_edit.move_units`
(God-Mode validation), frontend `useUnitDrag` (ship→orbit mapping; during tactical action
allows ground cargo in space of the active system).
