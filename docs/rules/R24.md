## 24. Player order: seating order, speaker, strategy card selection — ✅ VERIFIED (LRR + AsyncTI4 bot)

Milestone E1/E2 (turn overhaul). Two DIFFERENT orders apply in different phases:

- **Speaker order (seating order)** — used in the **strategy phase** when picking cards.
  Starts with the **speaker**, then **clockwise** (subsequent players around the table).
  This is seating order, NOT card initiative. Source: LRR "Strategy Phase" (players choose
  cards in speaker order) + AsyncTI4 `StartStrategyPhase` / selection queue.
- **Initiative order** — used in the **action phase** (as well as status/agenda). Determined
  by the **lowest number of the chosen strategy card** (see RULES.md 9).

**Seating order in the model:** `GameState.seating_order: list[str]` = player order around
the table (`player_id`), determined at `/game/new` **by seats** (`home_position`): player 1
first, followed by players in subsequent seats clockwise (`core/home_slots.seating_by_seats`;
2026-11-23 — previously ordered by player number, which diverged from table seating).
`GameState.speaker_id` = speaker; at start = player 1. Speaker order is the rotation of
`seating_order` starting from `speaker_id` (clockwise).

**Card selection in the strategy phase (flow rule):**
- In the strategy phase, `active_player_id` = the player whose turn it is to pick. Start: `speaker_id`.
- After picking a card, the turn passes to the **next player in speaker order** who does not yet
  have a card. Once everyone has picked → `active_player_id = None` (strategy phase ready to
  advance to action; `next_phase` will set the active player by initiative).
- **Assistant, not referee (design decision, memory `engine-is-table-assistant-not-referee`):**
  the engine SUGGESTS who picks (highlighting) and automatically advances to the next player,
  but does NOT strictly block picking for another player — the operator may override the order.
  Picking a card that is already owned/unavailable is still rejected (that is data consistency, not a rule).

**Action phase:** the player with the lowest initiative starts the first turn (`turn_flow.next_phase`
when entering action sets `active_player_id = turn_order[0]`). Order and passing: RULES.md 9.

Implementation: `GameState.seating_order`/`speaker_id` (`game_state.py`); `strategy_phase`
(`speaker_order`, `next_strategy_picker`, `pick_strategy_card` rotating active player);
`turn_flow.next_phase`/`next_round` setting the active speaker at the start of the strategy phase.
