## 42. Fleet Pool: Ship Limit in System and Excess Removal — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules2.com)

📎 tirules: [Fleet Pool](../../../../rules_tirules/R_fleet_pool.md) · [Capacity](../../../../rules_tirules/R_capacity.md) · [Command Sheet](../../../../rules_tirules/R_command_sheet.md)

- **Limit (R_fleet_pool):** number of tokens in the fleet pool (`PlayerState.fleet_cc`) = max number of
  NON-fighter ships a player may have in a single system. Not counted: fighters and ground forces (that is
  capacity, RULES.md 39.6), units on planets, ships being moved through. Structures in
  space (outside ships) are not counted.
- **Allowed to exceed, must remove:** ships MAY move / be produced / be placed above the pool
  (movement may also pass through a full system), but the player chooses and returns the excess to reinforcements.
  Therefore, the engine does NOT block the action; it (1) WARNS before it, (2) forces removal after it.
- **Implementation:** `core/fleet_pool.py` — `fleet_report` (pool, ships, excess),
  `open_fleet_pool_check` (`WindowType.FLEET_POOL` window, queue clockwise from active player),
  `resolve_fleet_pool` (remove EXACTLY the excess; non-fighter ships only), `auto_removal`
  (suggestion: cheapest first — ⚠️ ASSUMPTION, choice belongs to the player).
- **When checked:** in `tactical.next_step` before leaving a step (after movement, production,
  combat, Space Cannon) — right after the capacity check. The step stays open while the window lasts. Ending
  the tactical action clears the window. Endpoints: `GET /tactical/fleet-pool` (window respondent OR
  active player in active system — for warnings), `POST /tactical/fleet-pool/resolve`.
- **UI:** `FleetPoolWarning` ("N / pool" bar, excess warning) in the production modal (also counts
  ships in the cart) and in the move step panel; `FleetPoolModal` — forced choice modal.
- **Status phase (step 5, token redistribution):** reducing the fleet pool may create excess.
  The panel shows `FleetPoolStatusMeter` (largest fleet vs planned pool, list of systems to
  clear; data `status.fleetShips`). After confirmation, `status_phase.advance_fleet_check`
  opens a `FLEET_POOL` window (`phase=status` context) for the first system with excess; after closing
  it — the next system/player. Steps 6–7 (`finalize_status`) wait until no excess remains.

⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS / gaps: (1) God-Mode (edit-units, rule-free drag&drop) bypasses fleet pool — safety valve;
(2) ~~check on step change in the active system~~ — replaced by 42.1 (fleet pool always immediate); OUTSIDE activation, excess is enforced by `core/limits.enforce_limits` (item 42.x below); (3) Fighter II: fighters BEYOND ship capacity count
toward fleet pool instead of being removed (C_units / card text; `capacity.CapacityReport.fleet_fighters`,
RULES.md 57.8); (4) status phase (`status_phase.fleet_excess`) computes excess using THE SAME function
`fleet_pool.fleet_report` (audit 2026-11-11 — previously had its own copy of the rule without Fighter II).

**42.x Continuous limit enforcement (capacity + fleet pool) outside activation.** An effect that places ships or cargo over the limit
(action card, relic, agenda, faction ability, exploration, card-based production) opens a `CAPACITY` / `FLEET_POOL` window IMMEDIATELY,
rather than waiting for the next action step. Single place: `core/limits.enforce_limits(state, registry)`, called by
`StateService.replace_state` after every normal commit (new effects do not need to remember to check). Scope and exceptions:
- checks all systems (sequentially; the next one after closing a window = next commit), capacity before fleet pool;
- skips the active system of an ongoing tactical action (has its own `tactical._check_can_leave` gate), status phase (own
  `status_phase` step), another active window, unresolved exploration, and round 1 strategy phase (starting setup); strategy phase from round 2 onward IS
  checked — end of status phase places units (Sol's Genesis), and F_sol: "After, the Sol player might need to remove an infantry
  or fighter to meet capacity limits" (Sol audit 2026-11-17);
- God-Mode and manual board edits (labels `god-mode*`, `space:`, `planet:`, `edit:`, `move-units`, `cc:`, `capture:`, `setup:`,
  `clock:`) are a safety valve and do not enforce the limit — but the NEXT normal action will open a window if state was left over limit.
Tests: `tests/test_fleet_pool.py` (`enforce_limits`, `StateService`).

**42.1 Fleet pool: ALWAYS and IMMEDIATELY** — ✅ user decision (2026-11-13), R_fleet_pool: "If at any time the number
of a player's non–fighter ships in a system exceeds … they choose and remove excess ships"; F_mentak (Sleeper Cell): "must then
immediately remove a unit". General rule, no exceptions outside God-Mode:
- `limits.enforce_limits` checks fleet pool in EVERY system, including the active system of an ongoing tactical action and during
  combat (movement, production, card effects, ships placed in combat). Phases ACTION/AGENDA; status has its own step (RULES.md 41).
- When another window is active (hit assignment, retreat, player decisions…), the fleet pool window INTERRUPTS it: `timing.interrupt` stashes it into
  `GameState.suspended_window`, and `timing.close_window` restores it after excess removal.
- Actions without their own window wait: `timing.require_no_limit_window` in `movement.move_ships`, `production.produce`,
  `battle.roll_round` / `resolve_afb` / `assign_combat_hits` / `retreat_from_combat`, offers (`triggers._take_offer`), and pass
  (`turn_flow.pass_turn`); end turn, activation, component and strategic actions are already blocked by an open window.
- Passing through a system (ships in transit) does not count — movement in the engine is atomic (ships immediately at destination).
- Capacity unchanged (item 42.x): has its own exceptions (fighters and ground forces in combat do not count toward capacity — R_fleet_pool,
  Notes), so in the active system it is still checked on step change.
Tests: `tests/test_fleet_pool.py` (window interrupt and restore, action blocking), `tests/factions/test_flows_mentak.py`
(Sleeper Cell over pool during combat).
