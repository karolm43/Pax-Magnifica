## 50. Thunder's Edge: expeditions, The Fracture, Thunder's Edge — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules) / ⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Expeditions](../../../../rules_tirules/R_expedition.md) · [The Fracture](../../../../rules_tirules/R_fracture.md) · [Breakthroughs](../../../../rules_tirules/R_breakthroughs.md) · [Neutral Units](../../../../rules_tirules/R_neutral_units.md) · [Synergy](../../../../rules_tirules/R_synergy.md) · [Relics](../../../../rules_tirules/R_relics.md)

Scope: layout and costs (expeditions, Fracture entry, neutral units, ingress/egress, Thunder's Edge
as a planet on the map). **Breakthrough abilities are NOT coded** (separate stage); Thunder's Edge's legendary ability (Jupiter Brain) is in R60. A planet in The Fracture without a controller gives the conqueror a relic (R60.5).

### 50.1. Expeditions (`core/expeditions.py`, state `GameState.expeditions`)
- At the END of their own turn (action phase, active player, after an action or pass, with no open windows/combat/invasion/
  exploration), the player may perform an expedition: chooses an unclaimed token slice, pays the cost, and claims it.
- Slices clockwise from top (`EXPEDITION_SLICES`): `fiveRes` (5 resources), `actionCards` (discard 2 action
  cards), `fiveInf` (5 influence), `secret` (discard 1 unscored secret objective), `techSkip` (exhaust a planet with a
  technology specialty), `tradeGoods` (3 TG). TGs substitute for resources/influence as usual (overpayment is forfeited).
- One slice per turn (`expeditions.performed_by`, cleared in `end_turn`/`next_phase`); once claimed, a slice
  does not return. A player may claim multiple slices (across different turns).
- An expedition grants the faction's breakthrough if the player does not have it (`core/breakthrough.unlock_breakthrough`) — then
  a **roll for The Fracture** occurs (50.2). A player who already has their breakthrough does not roll.
- Sixth (last) slice: Thunder's Edge enters play (50.3). Control tokens on the expedition token do not
  consume command tokens in the engine (they are slice-claim markers).
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: a discarded secret objective returns to the secret objective deck, which is shuffled (like returning excess during
  drawing) — tirules does not specify a secret objective discard pile.

### 50.2. The Fracture (`core/fracture.py`, state `GameState.fracture`)
- d10 roll after gaining a breakthrough: a result of **1 or 10** ("0" face = 10) brings The Fracture into play. The roll is engine-driven
  and seedable (`dice.next_rng`); result in `fracture.last_roll`, UI animates it. When The Fracture is already in play — no roll.
- Entry: 7 tiles `fracture1..7` at positions `frac1..frac7` (arc of adjacent hexes far from the rings;
  location is arbitrary and has no mechanical effects — R_fracture). Tiles are adjacent to each other, not to the map.
  The map (`HexGrid`) is not in game state: `fracture.sync_grid` synchronizes the tiles from state on every
  read, so Undo/Redo reverts them.
- Neutral units placed from the back of the tiles (`NEUTRAL_SETUP`). ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: counts from AsyncTI4
  (`FractureService.spawnFracture`): fracture1 — 2 cruisers + 2 infantry on Cocytus; fracture4 — 2 dreadnoughts +
  1 destroyer + 3 infantry on Styx; fracture7 — 1 carrier + 4 fighters + 1 infantry each on Lethe and Phlegethon.
  The back of the tiles is a physical component not described in tirules — to be confirmed.
- **Ingress** (`SystemState.ingress_token`, max 1 per system): for a breakthrough with synergy — 3 systems for each of the 2
  synergy colors with a planet having a matching specialty; without synergy (effect, Nekro `NONE`) — 1 system for
  each of the 4 colors. A system with two specialty planets is a single candidate. Fewer candidates than needed →
  tokens are placed automatically (loop until stabilized). Additionally, 1 ingress in Thunder's Edge's system (if on the board),
  otherwise on Mecatol Rex. Chooser: the player who brought The Fracture into play (`fracture.entered_by`).
- **Egress** is printed on the tile (`SystemDef.has_egress`: fracture2, fracture6). Map graph: every ingress
  is adjacent to every egress (`portal` edge); ingress–ingress and egress–egress are NOT (`HexGrid.sync_portals`).
- A player who gains control of a planet in The Fracture WITHOUT a previous controller draws 1 relic
  (`control.set_planet_control(..., registry)` → `core/relics.draw_relic`). The relic deck is created lazily from
  the largest `relic`-type deck in active sources. Relic effects are not coded (ownership only).
- "By an effect" entries (Cabal/Vuil'raith/Onyxxa/Firmament…) = breakthrough abilities — out of scope; `fracture.enter_play`
  is a ready entry point (God-Mode: `POST /god-mode/fracture`).

### 50.3. Neutral units (`core/neutral.py`)
- Neutrals are not a player: key `NEUTRAL_PLAYER_ID = "neutral"` in `space_units`/`planet_units`. Combat sees them as
  an opponent (ships / ground forces of another "player"); they NEVER gain control of a planet
  (`control.recompute_from_units` skips them).
- Combat: neutral losses are assigned automatically by the engine — hits go to the cheapest unit, and Sustain
  Damage is always used (`battle.neutral_loss_choice`); neutrals do not retreat. Combat stats =
  UPGRADED units (Destroyer II, Cruiser II, Dreadnought II, Infantry II…) — user decision 2026-11-18
  (replaces earlier "base" assumption); variant selection `neutral.variant_key` (generic upgrades of types from
  the `neutral` faction unit list in assets). Frontend (`lookupUnit`) already showed upgraded versions.
  ⚠️ Neutral rolls are executed by the engine (instead of "any other player" at the table).

### 50.4. Thunder's Edge (`expeditions.place_thunders_edge`)
- After the sixth slice: count slices per player; the player with the MOST slices places infantry equal to
  the number of slices they claimed and gains control of Thunder's Edge. Tie → the player placing Thunder's Edge chooses (may choose
  themselves). The player who claimed the last slice places Thunder's Edge.
- Legal system: no planet, no supernova, no printed wormhole, outside The Fracture. Frontier token
  remains. Planet `thundersedge` is added to the system (`SystemState.added_planets`; system planet list =
  `core/system_planets.planets_of_system`).
- A player without a breakthrough at the moment Thunder's Edge is placed will not gain it via expedition (expeditions end).

### 50.5. UI and API
- Endpoints: `GET /thunders-edge`, `POST /expeditions/perform`, `POST /fracture/ingress`,
  `POST /thunders-edge/place`, `POST /god-mode/fracture` (escape hatch). `actor_id` optional (defaults to active player /
  player entering Fracture / player placing Thunder's Edge).
- web-data: `expeditions` (slice → `completedBy`), tokens `ingress`, `thundersedge`, `relictoken` (Fracture planet
  without controller), `gameState.thundersEdge` (signal for overlays).
