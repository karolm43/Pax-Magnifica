## 57. Action Technologies, Off-Action Abilities, Frontier, and "Free" Production — user decision 2026-11-09

📎 tirules: [Technology notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_technology.md) · [Component Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_component_action.md) · [Exploration](../../../../rules_tirules/R_exploration.md) · [Frontier Tokens](../../../../rules_tirules/R_frontier_tokens.md) · [Production](../../../../rules_tirules/R_production.md)

Same principle as 56: wherever there is a player decision, the player gets a UI (button/form) and the engine computes the options; mandatory effects without choice execute on their own.

**57.1 Component action with effect.** `component_action.play_component_action(..., params)` invokes the effect of a technology with "ACTION:" (`register_effect("technologies:<id>")` + `register_effect_inputs`); choices are validated (`offer_inputs.validate_params`), an error rolls back the action. UI: `TechnologyUseModal` (form from `GET /technology/{id}/form`). *Sling Relay* (`sr`): choose a system with your own space dock, exhaust the card, and open production of 1 ship (paid).

**57.2 Off-action abilities.** `register_ability` / `GET /technology/abilities` / `POST /technology/ability` (with `actor_id`): do not consume the turn's action. *Psychoarchaeology* (`pa`): during the action phase, outside resolving another ability, the player selects planets with a specialty (ready), exhausts them, and gains 1 TG each. Button in the "Play a component action" modal ("Technology abilities" section).

**57.3 "Free" production (`ProductionGrant.free`).** Outside a tactical action and without unit PRODUCTION: limit `units`, no space dock requirement, zones = planet `zone` + space, `max_cost` (combined cost), `ships_only`. Used by Sling Relay (1 ship) and Integrated Economy (NO unit count limit, only combined cost limit; the production modal shows a cost bar instead of slots). Grants from cards say "produce", not "use PRODUCTION" (C_technology: Sling Relay "produce 1 ship", Freelancers), so `production.produce` does NOT fire the `PRODUCTION_USED` event for them (Self-Assembly Routines, Sarween Tools, and other reactions to "use PRODUCTION" do not apply); only `UNITS_PRODUCED` fires. `standalone` grants (Construction/Warfare) are regular uses of PRODUCTION and do fire the event.

**57.4 Frontier exploration (`core/exploration_frontier.py`).** Token in a planetless system; `start_frontier` removes the token and reveals a card from the `frontier` deck in THAT SAME exploration window (`ExploreCurrent.planet == ""`). Handled cards: fragments, Gamma Relay, Entropic Fields (token to a player-chosen pool + TG), Keleres Ship, Lost Crew, Dead World, Derelict Vessel (return objective on excess), Merchant Station; Mirage, Ion Storm, and Enigmatic Device = `manual` (⚠ for manual resolution). *Dark Energy Tap* (`det`): mandatory after a tactical action in a system with a token and your own ship (`TACTICAL_ACTION END` event fired after clearing the action); `end_turn` waits for exploration to close.

**57.5 "May" offers (decision modal, 56).** *Scanlink Drone Network* (`sdn`: upon activation, the player chooses a planet with their own units to explore), *Self-Assembly Routines* (`sar`: after PRODUCTION, exhaust card, place a mech from reinforcements on your planet in the system; ⚠ "remove mech from the board" variant omitted), *Integrated Economy* (`ie`: after gaining control of a planet, production with cost limit = planet resources), *Transit Diodes* (`td`: at the start of turn, up to 4 ground forces → player's planets, 4 "unit + target" slots), *Predictive Intelligence* (`pi`: at end of turn, redistribute command tokens; ⚠ second ability, +3 votes in agenda, unmodeled, so the card is not covered).

**57.6 Player choices as base rule parameters.** *Graviton Laser System* (`gls`): `exhaust_gls` when firing Space Cannon Offense (toggle in panel); hits must go to non-fighter ships if any exist. *AI Development Algorithm* (`aida`, production part): `exhaust_aida` during production, discount = number of unit upgrades; UI also shows hook discounts (Sarween Tools). Marked with `declare_player_option`.

**57.7 Not implemented.** (empty — gaps in *Fighter II*, *Predictive Intelligence*, X-89 ΩΩ, and Self-Assembly closed in 57.8)

**57.8 General technologies review vs C_technology (2026-11-11).** 📎 tirules: [Technology notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_technology.md) · [Unit notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_units.md).
- *Fighter II* (`ff2`): C_units (Fighter): fighters in excess of ship capacity are NOT removed, they count toward fleet pool instead. `capacity.CapacityReport.fighters_overflow_to_fleet` (owning `ff2`): capacity excess then applies only to ground forces over general capacity; `fleet_fighters` = fighters that do not fit in capacity (ground forces have priority for general capacity, fighters first occupy "fighter-only" space dock slots). `fleet_pool.fleet_report(..., registry)` counts them as `ships["fighter"]`; in the fleet pool modal the player can return them. ⚠ ASSUMPTION: capacity allocation between fighters and ground forces is as above (the rules do not specify order). Not modeled: "a ship cannot move through a system containing another player's Fighter II" (movement). During combat in a system (`capacity.combat_in_progress`), excess Fighter II do NOT count toward fleet pool (R_fleet_pool / C_units, Notes: "any number of Fighter II may be in a system during combat, regardless of capacity and fleet pool"); capacity and this excess are checked only after combat, while non-fighter ships count toward the pool at all times (RULES.md 42.1). Movement: Fighter II at the start may fly on its own OR ride as carrier cargo — choice per unit (R_gravity_rift: "for each fighter, they must choose if that fighter is moving itself or being transported"); the route modal shows it on both the ship list and cargo list with a shared limit (`routeTypes.otherRoleCount`), `move_fleet` does not allow taking the same units twice.
- *Predictive Intelligence* (`pi`): second ability — "when you cast votes, you may cast 3 additional votes; if the outcome you voted for is not resolved, exhaust this card". Player decision (toggle in ballot box), `agenda_phase.cast_vote(use_predictive=...)`; requires a ready (unexhausted) card; exhausted in `_resolve` for votes with `AgendaVote.techs == ["pi"]` on a losing outcome. Both `pi` abilities are now covered by hooks (`covers`).
- *X-89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ* (`x89c4`): hook point `bombardment.empty_planets` (PERMIT) — may also bombard a planet controlled by ANOTHER player with no ground forces (C_technology). ✅ VERIFIED (`C_technology.md`): uncontrolled planets and the attacker's own planets are not targets; Planetary Shield still blocks. All dice of a single unit go to the same planet (assignment per unit).
- *Self-Assembly Routines* (`sar`): when mech reinforcements are empty, a mech is removed from any system without your own command token (player choice) and placed undamaged.
- Verified unchanged vs C_technology: Fleet Logistics (pass after action blocked in `turn_flow.pass_turn`), Dark Energy Tap (retreat only to a system without other players' units), Sarween Tools (no discount for Sling Relay / Integrated Economy grants), ship production blocked when enemy ships are present (Integrated Economy), Magen ΩΩ, Duranium Armor, Assault Cannon.
- **UI indicators** (`core/tech_effects.py`, `PlayerData.techEffects`, component `ActiveTechs`): every general technology has a `TECH_CONTEXTS` entry (in which windows it is relevant: production, movement, combat, Space Cannon, invasion, agenda, fleet, status, turn, exploration) and state `active` / `ready` / `exhausted`. The chip shows that the card works even without a "may" choice (e.g., Sarween Tools in production, Gravity Drive in movement, Antimass Deflectors during Space Cannon); card text in tooltip from assets. A test enforces that any new general technology has an entry.
