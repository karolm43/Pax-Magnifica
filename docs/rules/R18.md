## 18. Player Panel: Abilities, Faction Technologies, Breakthroughs — ✅ VERIFIED (data + frontend)

📎 tirules: [Breakthroughs](../../../../rules_tirules/R_breakthroughs.md) · [Synergy](../../../../rules_tirules/R_synergy.md) · [Technology](../../../../rules_tirules/R_technology.md) · [Leaders](../../../../rules_tirules/R_leaders.md)

AsyncTI4 also renders **faction abilities**, **faction technologies**, and **breakthrough**
(a Thunder's Edge mechanic) on the player card, each in its respective state. Sources (not from memory):

- **Data** (`assets/data/`): abilities in `abilities/*.json` (fields `id`, `name`,
  `faction`, `permanentEffect`/`window`/`windowEffect`); technologies in
  `technologies/*.json` (fields `alias`→id, `faction`, `types`); breakthroughs in
  `breakthroughs/*.json` (fields `alias`→id, `faction`, `synergy`). Faction assignment
  goes through the entry's `faction` FIELD (and the `abilities`/`factionTech` lists in
  faction data) — not from memory.
- **Frontend** (`web-data` contract, verified in viewer code): `PlayerData`
  reads `abilities: string[]` (ability IDs → `Ability` component), `factionTechs`
  and `notResearchedFactionTechs` (the frontend GRAYS OUT unresearched ones, `unresearched` prop
  of the `Tech` component), and `breakthrough?: {breakthroughId, unlocked, exhausted,
  tradeGoodsStored}` (`Breakthrough` component; the slot hides when nobody has a
  breakthrough). Types file: `frontend/src/entities/data/types.ts`; rendering:
  `domains/player/components/deck/PlayerDeckRow.tsx` + `usePlayerCardComputedData.ts`.

**State rules (how we emit):**
- Abilities are passively stateful — their window/effect comes from data (`permanentEffect`
  vs `window`), so we only send IDs (`abilities`). No leader-style locked/exhausted
  states.
- A faction technology is **researched** when its ID is in the player's `technologies`
  list → goes into `techs` (tech rack). **Unresearched** → `notResearchedFactionTechs`
  (frontend grays it out). `factionTechs` = full list from faction data.
- Breakthrough (one per faction) — state from `PlayerState`: `breakthrough_unlocked` (after
  meeting synergy), `breakthrough_exhausted` (after action), `breakthrough_tg`
  (stored TGs). Initial state: locked.

Implementation: `FactionDef.abilities`/`faction_tech` + models `AbilityDef`/
`TechnologyDef`/`BreakthroughDef` (the registry loads them from assets by `source`); emission
in `web_data.py` (`_faction_abilities`, `_faction_techs`, `_player_breakthrough`).
