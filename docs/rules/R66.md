## 66. Player Elimination (2026-11-18)
📎 tirules: [Elimination](../rules_tirules/R_elimination.md) · [Relics](../rules_tirules/R_relics.md) · [Exploration cards](../rules_tirules/C_exploration_cards.md)

Code: `core/elimination.py` (called in `turn_flow.end_turn`), tests `tests/test_elimination.py`.

### 66.1 Conditions (R_elimination: Rules Reference 1)
A player is eliminated when SIMULTANEOUSLY: they have no ground forces on the board, have no unit with the
PRODUCTION ability, and control no planets. The Arborec flagship produces, but does not have PRODUCTION —
it does not protect against elimination (Notes).

- **Engine guard:** only a player deployed on the board is a candidate (`home_position` lies
  in `state.systems`). In a real game this applies to everyone; a state without a home system (God-Mode,
  test scenarios) does not mean defeat.
- The last player in the game is never eliminated.

### 66.2 Check Timing
End of every action phase turn (`turn_flow.end_turn`): combats, invasions, and card effects that can
eliminate a player happen during someone's turn. When the active player is eliminated, the turn passes to the
next unpassed player in the pre-elimination order.
⚠️ ASSUMPTION: elimination resulting from an effect in the agenda/status phase is detected only at the end
of the next action phase turn.

### 66.3 Effects (R_elimination: Rules Reference 2–9, Notes)
- The player's units and command tokens are removed from the board (to the box).
- Agendas they own ("elect player" laws on them) go to the discard pile.
- Other players' promissory notes in their hand and play area return to their owners; their notes held by others — to the box.
- Action cards from hand to the discard pile.
- Strategy cards return to the common pool (player disappears from `players`).
- Secret objectives (scored and unscored) return to the deck, the deck is shuffled.
- Relic fragments go to the discard piles of their respective exploration decks; relics are purged (Notes).
- Speaker passes to the player to the left (`strategy_phase.set_speaker`).
- Game started with 5+ players: after eliminations, still one strategy card each (`pick_count`).
- The eliminated player's home system remains a home system (Notes) — `EliminatedPlayer` trace
  in `GameState.eliminated_players` with `home_position`; objectives like Darken the Skies account for it.
- Activity feed: `eliminated` fact.

### 66.4 Out of Scope (status in `tests/rulings_status.json`)
Faction elements: Nekro assimilator, Creuss wormhole tokens, Naalu "0" token, Ul attachments,
Mahact command tokens, captives.
