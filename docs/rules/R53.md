## 53. Playing action cards (ACTION window) + effects as hooks — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules) / ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Action Cards](../../../../rules_tirules/R_action_cards.md) · [Component Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_component_action.md) · [Abilities](../../../../rules_tirules/R_abilities.md)

Code: `core/action_cards.py` (`play_action_card`), effects in `core/hooks/action_cards.py`, registration via
`rules_hooks.register_effect("action_cards:<id>")`, endpoint `POST /action-card/play` (with `actor_id`, RULES.md 44.1).

- ✅ (R_action_cards) A card with the word "Action" in its header resolves as a COMPONENT ACTION in the action phase
  (one action per turn - `component_action` validation, RULES.md 31.8). After resolution, the card goes to the
  discard pile (`card_draw`). Card hand is hidden, card count is public.
- ✅ (R_action_cards, notes) A player cannot target themselves with offensive cards; a card targeting a SPECIFIC number
  of objects cannot be played when no such objects exist; "each / any number / up to" may affect zero objects.
  Engine: an effect requiring a choice (`planet`) raises `ValueError` when the choice is illegal - zero state changes.
- ✅ A component with an effect (`register_effect`) has IMPLEMENTED status in the effect registry (`covered_components`).
- ⚠ ASSUMPTION (RULES.md 43.3): a card with an ACTION window WITHOUT an engine effect is played and discarded, counts as
  an action, and the API returns `implemented=false` - the outcome is resolved by the operator (God-Mode). The alternative (blocking play
  of an unsupported card) awaits user decision.
- ⚠ NOT supported: reaction cards (when/after/before/instead) - require STOP and playability predicates
  (43.4); card cancellation (Sabotage); 7-card hand limit and other cards without an ACTION window.
- ⚠ ASSUMPTION: Refit Troops covers only infantry on planets (not cargo in space); Plague: d10 die
  (6+ threshold from card text); Scuttle: TG = `cost` field of the player's unit variant, fractions truncated; Lucky Shot /
  Insubordination / Spy / Plague cannot target self (R_action_cards, notes).
- ⚠ ASSUMPTION: planet traits (`industrial`/`cultural`/`hazardous`) from planet data (`exploration.planet_traits`),
  excluding traits granted by attachments.

**Play form (UI):** `GET /action-card/{id}/inputs?player_id=` returns the card text, `implemented`, and the list
of player choices (`choice` / `multi` / `number`, options computed by the engine on current state - owned planets,
systems with tokens, targets…). Descriptions are registered by effects via `rules_hooks.register_effect_inputs`
(`core/hooks/action_card_inputs.py`); the frontend (`ActionCardPlayModal`) knows no cards, it only renders the descriptor.
Consistency test: parameters built the same way as in UI pass effect validation (`test_action_cards.py`).

**Drag-to-play from hand (UI):** `GET /action-card/playable?player_id=` returns for each action card in hand
`playable` / `reason` / `implemented` (`action_cards.playability`): ACTION window, turn/phase/window/one-action (same
validations as playing) and - for automated cards - legal targets for every choice (R_action_cards: a card that cannot
be fully resolved cannot be played). The hand dock allows PICKING UP only playable cards; dropping onto the
"Drop to play" zone opens the play form. Unplayable cards are dimmed, and the tooltip gives the reason.

**New hook points (RULES.md 43):** `agenda.vote_modifier` (MODIFY, hooked in `agenda_phase.extra_votes`),
`combat.extra_dice` (MODIFY), `combat.reroll_misses` (PERMIT, one reroll of misses in `battle.roll_round`).
Hook sources (`owned_sources`) now also include laws in play (`decks.agenda_laws`, apply to every player)
and promissory notes in a player's play area. ⚠ Still missing: leaders, relics, breakthrough (see docstring of `owned_sources`).

### 53.5. Action cards played in REACTION to a window (when / after / before / at start / at end) — ⚠ ASSUMPTION (window model)

A card with a window other than "Action" is not a turn action and does not consume an action (R_action_cards: played by a
player when the described event occurs; multiple cards may be played in the same window). The engine does NOT ask players
"do you have a card" (RULES.md 43.4: behavior must not depend on hand). Instead:

- The engine knows **windows open NOW** (`core/reaction_cards.open_windows`): this is a function of game state (combat step,
  agenda step, tactical action step, phase), computed by `@window_provider` providers from
  `core/hooks/reactions_*.py`. Window = (event, moment) from `game_events` + context (`player` = subject).
- A card in hand is **playable** (`action_cards.playability`) when: an open window matches (address from card window
  text, `declare_reaction` can override it), the player's relation to the subject matches ("you" / "another player"),
  the card's condition is met, and (when automated by the engine) legal targets exist.
- The player **drags** the card onto the play zone; `POST /action-card/play` plays it in the window. A reaction is not
  a turn action (`took_action_this_turn` unchanged), and a non-active player may also play it.
- A window lasts as long as the game state stays in that step (e.g., "after activating a system" until the first ship move),
  corresponding to "the next committed action" from 43.4. ⚠ Rewinding a step (STOP) for cards meant to
  CANCEL an already-taken step (Sabotage, Public Disgrace, Coup d'Etat, Political Stability) is a separate stage.
- An effect with duration = `active_effects` + hook with source `SOURCE_EFFECT` on an existing engine point;
  expires via `triggers.EXPIRY` (TACTICAL_ACTION / COMBAT / COMBAT_ROUND / INVASION / AGENDA / TURN / PHASE).

### 53.5.a Agenda ("agenda" group: `core/hooks/reactions_agenda.py`, tests `tests/test_reactions_agenda.py`)

Also applies to Diplomatic Pressure (same window). Discards the agenda to the discard pile and reveals the next one

- `dp1..dp4` Diplomatic Pressure — `AGENDA_REVEALED/WHEN`. Target: another player with a promissory note in hand, not previously targeted by this player on this agenda (ruling).
  The note is chosen by the TARGET (ruling "the target player chooses"): mandatory `decide` offer with `decider` = target (`triggers`, effect hook `action_cards:dp_give`);
  with one note, given immediately. Transferred via `promissory_notes.transfer` (not a transaction — ruling).
- `const_rider`, `diplo_rider`, `imp_rider`, `lead_rider`, `politic_rider`, `sanction`, `tech_rider`, `trade_rider`, `war_rider` — `AGENDA_REVEALED/AFTER`

  ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS: reward choices (planet, system, technology, distribution of 3 tokens) are provided by the player WHEN playing the card (not upon winning the reward);
  a reward unachievable at resolution (lost planet, none in reinforcements) is skipped with a `skipped` entry; Construction Rider does not handle

- `assassin` — `AGENDA_REVEALED/AFTER`: chosen player (any player, including the playing player) does not vote (`block_voter`); a blocked player may still play riders (ruling).
- `hack` (Hack Election Ω — Thunder's Edge text "During this agenda, you vote last"; TI4 wiki "Thunder's Edge Updated", assets) —
  `AGENDA_REVEALED/AFTER`: the playing player votes last, even AFTER the speaker (`vote_last`; Ω ruling "The speaker votes before the player that played

## 53.6 Hold-play and Sabotage

Source: user decision ("HOLD" brief, mechanism A), reaction model 43.4 pt 5 (public filter), Sabotage text from assets (`sabo1`–`sabo4`: "When another player plays an action card other than 'Sabotage': Cancel that action card."). Code: `core/card_hold.py`, `core/hooks/reactions_sabotage.py`, `action_cards.play_action_card(..., now=)`.

- **Hold-play window.** When someone plays an action card (ACTION window or reaction) and another player could respond with Sabotage, the effect does NOT execute immediately. The card leaves hand onto the table (publicly, `GameState.held_play`), the turn action (for an ACTION card) is already spent, and the rest of the game pauses: `End turn`, pass, tactical and strategic action, component action, phase change, and other player actions raise `ValueError` (in API additionally middleware: any `POST` other than `/action-card/*`, `/god-mode/*`, undo/redo/history, `/game/new`, `/map/load`, `/state`, `/pause`, `/resume` => HTTP 422). God-Mode is allowed.
- **Public filter** (43.4 pt 5): the window opens when simultaneously (1) an action card deck is in play (deck or discard pile non-empty), (2) not all 4 Sabotage cards are in the discard pile, (3) someone ELSE has >= 1 action card in hand (hand count is public), (4) the played card is not Sabotage. Hand contents never drive behavior: a player without Sabotage gets the window identically to one with Sabotage. When conditions fail, the card executes immediately as before.
- **Window end.** Eligible = other players with >= 1 action card in hand when played. The window ends when the fixed window duration elapses (`release`; since 2026-11-16 set by host, default 4 s, plus "More time" — RULES.md 54.11; `now` time is passed by the router from the server clock, engine is pure) or someone plays Sabotage. Passes (`pass_hold`) do not shorten the window; the engine auto-passes for players without playable Sabotage, for bots, and for players with "Skip all reactions" (54.11). The playing player does not pass for others. On release, the saved effect executes with saved `params` (dice rolls/random draws happen only then), the card goes to discard, and for ACTION cards "after component action" fires.
- **Upfront validation.** Card parameters are validated on play (trial on a discarded state copy - real state RNG untouched), so release should not raise an error.
- **Sabotage (`sabo1`–`sabo4`).** Playable via drag when another player's card (other than Sabotage) is waiting in `held_play`, the player is eligible, and has not passed yet (`declare_reaction`: `ACTION_CARD_PLAYED`/WHEN, `subject=OTHER`). Effect: `held_play = None`, waiting card goes to discard without executing its effect, a canceled ACTION card returns the turn action (C_action_cards/sabotage: perform a different action or pass); Sabotage goes to discard like any played card. Sabotage does not open a hold window and cannot be canceled by Sabotage. During the window, no card other than Sabotage can be played.
- ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS: (a) "action card deck exists" as a public filter condition - without it (hand-built states/tests) there is nothing to wait on; (b) the "player plays an action card" event (`ACTION_CARD_PLAYED`/WHEN, tech/faction triggers) fires only on release, and a canceled card does not fire it; (c) cancellation does not fire "after component action"; (d) eligible players are those with a card in hand at play time (hand does not change during window); (e) a player who passed can no longer play Sabotage in that window.

## 53.7 Hold-rewind (rewinding to a past window)

Source: user decision ("HOLD" brief, mechanism B), reaction model 53.5 / 43.4, card texts from assets (`disgrace`, `coup`, `stability`, `crashlanding`), rulings `docs/rules_tirules/C_action_cards.md`. Code: `core/hold_rewind.py`, `core/hooks/reactions_stop.py`, `core/reaction_cards.py` (`register_hindsight`, `hindsight_scope`, `declare_rewindable`), `core/event_store.py` (`recent_chain`, `load`, `commit(branch_from=)`), `api/state_service.py` (`replace_state(branch_from=)`), router `api/routers/hold.py`; tests: `tests/test_hold_rewind.py`.

- **Card scope (user decision 2026-11-15).** HOLD covers EVERY reaction card — an action card with a window other than ACTION (when / after / at start / at end…; same classification as `reaction` in `action_cards.playability`), except Sabotage (`sabo1..4`: responds to `held_play`, which waits on its own and blocks rewinding). ACTION cards do not use it (played as an action on your own turn — rewinding would be regular Undo). Code: `reaction_cards.is_rewindable(registry, card)`; `declare_rewindable` remains for explicitly registering a card. The card's window and targets are computed on the snapshot state, and anti-cheating works regardless of card. Previously only the 4 cards below (gap from 2026-11-15 window audit).
- **Mechanism.** The game does not wait for reactions. A player picks from the snapshot stack the step at which they wanted to play the card; the engine in ONE commit rewinds state to that snapshot (later steps are dropped, history branches like Undo + new action) and immediately plays the card in that window (`hold_rewind.play_at` -> `action_cards.play_action_card` on snapshot state; windows computed from that state, form as well). The chess clock does NOT rewind (real time has elapsed).
- **Anti-cheat** (`is_irreversible_step(prev, next)`, computed from the diff of consecutive snapshots). You may only rewind to the furthest snapshot for which on the path to the present there was NO: (1) dice roll (`rng_seed`/`rng_counter`/`table_roll_seq`), (2) change to any deck except the action card discard pile (draw, shuffle, peek: `decks.*`) or drawing a card to hand, (3) phase or round change, (4) change to the action card discard pile (card play by anyone, including an earlier hold-rewind) or commit labeled `hold-rewind:*`, (5) more than `MAX_REWIND_STEPS = 8` steps back; moreover not during `held_play`. The card must be in the player's hand NOW and in the snapshot.
- **Candidates.** `GET /hold/candidates` returns snapshots (newest first) in which the player has a hold-rewind card playable IN THAT state (`stepsBack = 0` = regular window open now), plus `limitReason` (why further back is disallowed). `GET /hold/{card}/inputs?seq=` computes the form on the snapshot; `POST /hold/play` rewinds and plays (label `hold-rewind:<card>:<seq>`, for `stepsBack = 0` regular `action-card:<card>`).
- **"Hindsight".** The effect may need knowledge of the rewound step. `register_hindsight(card, fn(window_state, post_step_state, window) -> dict)` attaches data to `window.ctx` only in `hindsight_scope` (during rewind); `post_step_state` = snapshot right AFTER the window (the rewound step), not the current state - so even several steps past the window we know which choice is being rewound. In the normal path (window NOW), no data is attached.
- **Public Disgrace** (`disgrace`, "When another player chooses a strategy card during the strategy phase"). Window: strategy phase, designated picker has not chosen yet (`strategy_phase.next_strategy_picker`). Effect: `Duration.PHASE` `action_cards:disgrace` with `data.forbidden=[chosen in rewound step]`; `strategy_phase.pick_strategy_card` rejects the forbidden card with a clear message as long as another is free ("if able"), and the ban disappears after their next pick. ⚠ ASSUMPTION: without a known pick (normal path, window NOW), the card is NOT offered - there is nothing to cancel; it is played via HOLD right after the pick. User report 2026-11-13 (card couldn't be dragged): `hold_rewind.just_passed` — a hold-rewind card playable in the snapshot from BEFORE THE LAST step is playable in `GET /action-card/playable` (`reaction`, `hold_seq` = that snapshot); the hand dock picks it up and shows "Play now", and playing goes via `POST /hold/play` with that `seq` (form warns about rewinding).
- **Coup d'Etat** (`coup`, "When another player would perform a strategic action"). Window (user report 2026-11-15): another player ANNOUNCED a strategic action — played a strategy card (`play_strategy_card` exhausts the card and sets `pending_strategy`), and primary has not resolved yet (first step, empty draft; all 8 cards have steps, effect applies at the end). Does not apply to Overrule or Winnu hero primary. Previously the window lasted from the start of another player's turn and the card was played "in advance" even though the player didn't have to take a strategic action. Effect: `pending_strategy` clears, `exhaust.ready_strategy_card`, `action_budget.unregister_action`, `turn_flow.end_turn(forced=True)` ("at the end of your turn" abilities work — ruling). After the player's first choice, the window has passed — HOLD only (rewind to announcement), provided no dice/card draws occurred along the way (e.g., Politics and Imperial draw cards at the end of primary — then Coup is lost; pause from 43.4 pt 4 needed).
- **Political Stability** (`stability`, "When you would return your strategy card(s) during the status phase"). Window: status, step 8 (`StatusStep.COMPLETE`), player has strategy cards. Effect: `Duration.ROUND` `action_cards:stability`; `strategy_phase.reset_strategy_cards` does not clear that player's cards (and `close_round_cards` leaves them untouched and does not accumulate TGs a second time), and `next_strategy_picker` skips them in the next strategy phase automatically (they already have their full set of cards). Effect expires on `ROUND END`. Because returning cards changes phase/round (rule 3), the card is in practice playable in the window NOW.
- **Crash Landing** (`crashlanding`, "When your last ship in the active system is destroyed"). Window (`UNIT_DESTROYED`/WHEN): space combat in the active system, `ASSIGN` step, player's pending hits >= ships + Sustain Damage (will destroy all their ships), has ground forces in space, system has a planet other than Mecatol Rex. Effect: 1 ground force (`unit`) from space to planet (`planet`) via `unit_ops.transfer_units`. ⚠ ASSUMPTION: coexistence = co-presence in `planet_units` (like Exchange Program), planet control unchanged; ground combat erupts in the Invasion step (RULES.md 40.1 I7a), even without Commit; only the space combat scenario is covered (not destruction of the last ship by Space Cannon/card).

- **Who chooses.** The player THEMSELVES chooses the discarded cards (R56) — in UI drags a card from hand onto the "Drop to discard" zone (`POST /action-card/discard-excess`, one card at a time). Discarding goes through `card_draw.discard_chosen_action_cards` ("after discarding an action card" window, Activity feed).
- **Immediately.** While a player has excess cards: they cannot play an action card (`action_cards.playability` / `play_action_card`), cannot give an action card in a transaction (`transaction`), the active player cannot end turn or pass (`turn_flow._require_action_turn`), and the phase cannot advance (`turn_flow.open_decisions_reason`).
- **View.** `GET /action-card/playable` returns `discard` (how many to discard) and `handLimit`; the hand dock automatically opens to action cards and displays the bar "Hand limit 7: drag N action card(s) onto the discard pile".
- **God-Mode (tests).** The host can disable the limit: `GameState.action_card_limit_disabled` (patch `/god-mode/patch`, toggle in the header God-Mode menu). Then `action_card_limit` returns `None` (no limit). User decision, not a TI4 rule.
- ⚠️ Changing the limit via effects (e.g., Yssaril — Crafty) — hook point in `hand_limit.action_card_limit` when that faction is added.

## 53.9 Rulings audit: economy, technology and planet cards

Source: `docs/rules_tirules/C_action_cards.md`. Tests: `tests/rulings_general/test_rulings_ac_eco_planets.py`.

- **Reparations** needs at least one of its two effects: with no exhausted planet of your own AND no readied planet of the taker it cannot be played.
- **Impersonation** is not playable with less than 3 influence available (ready planets + trade goods).
- **Plague** may target any planet controlled by another player, also one without infantry.
- **Mercenary Contract**: returning the planet card purges every card attached to the planet.
- **Refit Troops**: mechs missing from reinforcements are taken from systems without your command token and placed undamaged.
- **Reactor Meltdown / non-home**: an eliminated player's home planets stay home planets.
