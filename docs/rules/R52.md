## 52. Custodians Token: removal during invasion of Mecatol Rex — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules)

📎 tirules: [Custodians Token](../../../../rules_tirules/R_custodians_token.md) · [Mecatol Rex](../../../../rules_tirules/R_mecatol_rex.md) · [Invasion](../../../../rules_tirules/R_invasion.md) · [Influence](../../../../rules_tirules/R_influence.md) · [Victory Points](../../../../rules_tirules/R_victory_points.md) · [Agenda Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_agenda_phase.md)

Code: `core/custodians.py`, `core/invasion.py` (`claim_custodians`, `custodians_block_reason`), `api/routers/invasion.py` (`POST /tactical/invasion/custodians`), UI `frontend/src/domains/invasion/CustodiansClaim.tsx`.

- (C1) The token sits on Mecatol Rex from setup (45). Units may enter the system normally, but **while the token is on it, ground forces cannot be landed on Mecatol Rex** (`commit_ground_forces` → 422).
- (C2) **Before the Commit Ground Forces step**, the active player may remove the token by **spending 6 influence** (planets — exhaust, + trade goods 1:1; overpayment is forfeited). Engine: allowed in the Bombardment and Commit substeps, before anything has landed.
- (C3) After removal, the player **must commit at least one ground force to Mecatol Rex** (`advance` from Commit blocks without this). Without ground forces in orbit, the token cannot be removed.
- (C4) Removal: the player takes the token into their play area and **gains 1 VP** (`objectives.award_points`, entry `scored["custodians"]`).
- (C5) After removal, **the agenda phase is added to all subsequent rounds, including the current one** — flag `GameState.custodians_removed` (read by `status_phase`, RULES.md 41/51).
- (C6) The token is not an attachment.

**⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS:**
1. The player chooses the payment (planets + TG) as with Leadership; once removed, reverting is possible only via Undo (the engine does not refund influence if the player does not land).
2. The commitment requirement is checked when exiting Commit (not upon removal itself).
