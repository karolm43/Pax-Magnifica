## 40. Invasion: Bombardment, Commit, Space Cannon Defense, Ground Combat, Establish Control — ✅ VERIFIED (LRR 2.0)

### 40.1. Rules

- (I1) **Five substeps** in fixed order: Bombardment → Commit Ground Forces → Space Cannon Defense → Ground Combat → Establish Control. Units are "on the planet" (for game effects) starting from Commit.
- (I2) **Bombardment**: active player only. Chooses a planet for each unit with Bombardment and rolls `X (×Y)` dice; hit = result ≥ X. Effects that modify combat rolls do NOT affect bombardment. All dice of a single unit target ONE planet. The player may choose not to bombard at all, but if they bombard, they MUST use Bombardment of EVERY one of their units in the active system.
- (I3) The planet's defender destroys their own ground forces for each hit (excess is lost); a mech may use Sustain Damage to cancel a hit. Bombardment happens OUTSIDE of combat (does not count toward "win a combat" objectives).
- (I4) **Planetary Shield**: planets with a Planetary Shield unit (PDS) cannot be bombarded. War sun (`disablesPlanetaryShield`) ignores shields of other players' units in its system — then all units with Bombardment may bombard. One planet's shield does not protect other planets in the system.
- (I5) **Commit Ground Forces**: the active player may land any number of ground forces from the active system's space area onto any planets in the system (including those with another player's forces). No landing → straight to Production.
- (I6) **Space Cannon Defense**: units with Space Cannon ON a planet onto which ground forces were committed may fire at the committed forces (optional). The active player chooses planet order. Hits are covered by the attacker (Sustain Damage allowed).
- (I7) **Ground Combat**: a planet where the active player and another player both have ground forces → combat (RULES.md 39, kind="ground"). Occurs after Space Cannon Defense on ALL planets, before Establish Control on any planet. The active player chooses combat order. No retreats.
- (I7a) **Combat without Commit** — USER DECISION (2026-11-13): ground combat in the Invasion step erupts on EVERY planet in the active system where the active player has ground forces alongside another player's ground forces — even if they did not commit them in this Commit step (Crash Landing, coexistence, units placed by an effect). Invasion therefore also opens without forces in space when such a contested planet exists; Commit with no landing does not skip combat, and Establish Control covers landed planets AS WELL AS planets where combat took place. tirules (R_coexistence) says "may start a ground combat" — intentional simplification: combat always occurs.
- (I8) **Establish Control**: the active player gains control of each planet they committed forces to, provided they still have at least one ground force there. Other players' structures on the planet are immediately destroyed; the player receives the planet card and EXHAUSTS it. You cannot gain control of a planet you already control. If combat occurred and all units of both sides were destroyed — the defender retains control (places a control token). Gaining control of an uncontrolled planet → exploration.
- (I9) **One invasion per tactical action**: "Invasion is a step of the tactical action" (R_invasion) — the step executes once; all planets of the system in a single pass through the substeps. After invasion closes (Establish Control → close), it does not reopen in that action: `GameState.invasion_resolved` (reset on activation and end of action), `begin_invasion` → `ValueError`, the step counts as empty; the panel shows only a note (test `test_closed_invasion_cannot_be_started_again`). Component invasions (`source`) do not set this flag.

### 40.2. Model and Implementation

- `GameState.invasion: InvasionState | None` (`phase: InvasionPhase`, `committed`, `bombard_pending`, `defense_pending/resolved`, `combat_results`, `results`, `log`). Opened on entering the INVASION step (`invasion.begin_invasion`); does not open if there is neither bombardment nor forces to commit.
- `core/invasion.py`: `bombard` / `assign_bombard_hits`, `commit_ground_forces`, `defense_fire` / `defense_pass` / `assign_defense_hits`, `advance` (skips substeps with no work), `_establish_control`. Values from unit variant (`bombardHitsOn/DieCount`, `planetaryShield`, `disablesPlanetaryShield`, `spaceCannon*`).
- Ground combat = `core/battle.py` (`start_combat(planet)`); `close_combat` records the result in `invasion.combat_results` (draw → defender keeps the planet). Shared loss code: `battle.plan_losses` / `apply_losses`.
- Leaving the INVASION step (`tactical.next_step`) is forbidden until invasion reaches ESTABLISH.
- Invasion starts via BUTTON in the helper panel (`POST /tactical/invasion/begin`), not on entering the step. "Simple invasion" (`simple`: no bombardment target, no defender ground forces, and no Space Cannon on planets) skips steps 1, 3, 4 — landing is done in the helper panel (how many infantry/mechs onto which planet); the full invasion modal opens only when someone defends a planet.
- API: `GET /tactical/invasion`, `POST /tactical/invasion/{bombard, bombard/assign, commit, defense/fire, defense/pass, defense/assign, next}`.
