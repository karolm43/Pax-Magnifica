## 47. Player-to-player transactions + debt tokens — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules2.com) + ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS

Source: `docs/rules_tirules/R_transactions.md`, `R_commodities.md`, `R_promissory_notes.md`,
`R_relics.md`, `R_neighbors.md` (tirules2.com).

### 47.1 Rule (implemented: `core/transaction.py`, `POST /transactions/resolve`)
- During their turn, the active player may resolve **up to one transaction with each neighbor**
  (RULES.md 2 — adjacency from `neighbors_of`), at any time during the turn, including during combat
  → no timing-window block and no effect on `took_action_this_turn` (not an action).
- A player gives any number of **trade goods and commodities** and at most **one promissory note
  from hand**; in return they receive any number of TG, commodities, and **relic fragments**, and
  at most one promissory note. A transaction does not need to be equal (you can give for free).
- **A commodity given to another player becomes a trade good for the recipient** (R_commodities).
- Promissory notes in a player's play area (`promissory_notes_in_play`) cannot be exchanged;
  relics themselves — also not (only fragments).
- Losing and regaining adjacency in the same turn does not grant a second transaction →
  `PlayerState.transacted_with` (list of partners), reset together with `took_action_this_turn`.
- An empty transaction is rejected (design choice — nothing to exchange, does not consume the limit).
- Once resolved, there is no reverting outside engine Undo (per "cannot be undone").

### 47.1a Partner agreement (user report 2026-11-12: "transactions accepted without consent")
Source: `R_transactions` — "The players agree on the terms of the transaction before exchanging any
components"; `F_hacan` — "Either player may initiate negotiations for a transaction".
- The active player SUBMITS a proposal (`POST /transactions/propose`, `transaction.propose_transaction`) —
  same validation as an exchange, but nothing moves yet. At most one proposal waits at a time.
- The partner ACCEPTS or REJECTS (`/transactions/respond`, actor = partner); accepting re-validates
  and executes the exchange (`resolve_transaction`). The proposer may CANCEL (`/transactions/cancel`).
- Rejecting/canceling does not consume the "one transaction per partner per turn" limit.
- A proposal is cleared on a new turn (`action_budget.reset_turn`).
- Proposal contents (notes, action cards) are visible only to its parties (`GET /transactions/state`).
- **The partner's hand is hidden** (`R_promissory_notes`: "Players should keep their hands of promissory
  notes hidden"; user report 2026-11-13: the modal showed the requester's notes as the partner's notes).
  The requester selects the partner's note from a list of POSSIBLE notes (`transaction.requestable_notes`: notes in other players'
  hands excluding their own — a set derived from public info without identifying the holder;
  `GET /transactions/state` → `requestableNotes`). The proposal does not check the partner's hand (refusal
  would leak its contents); ownership is checked on acceptance — a partner without the note rejects, a bot rejects automatically.
  User decision 2026-11-13. Partner's action cards (Arbiters) are not listed (hidden hand) —
  ⚠️ requesting a specific action card from a partner: to be decided separately.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: a bot partner responds immediately (`transaction.bot_accepts`): accepts when receiving at
  least as much as it gives (TG/commodity = 1, note/fragment/card/relic/objective = 2). Placeholder pending AI.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: the active player starts negotiations (UI); proposals from a non-active player — later.

### 47.3 Debt tokens — ⚠️ UNOFFICIAL TABLE RULE (user decision)
Not in the TI4 rules. Players customarily hand each other small control tokens as non-binding
IOUs/debts (3 TG owed = 3 of the issuer's tokens). The engine only **remembers** who holds whose
tokens (`GameState.debt_tokens = {holder: {issuer: n}}`, `core/debt.py`,
`POST /debt/give`, `/debt/return`) — it enforces nothing. Works **without adjacency**,
in any phase and turn. The creditor returns tokens to the issuer when the debt is paid/forgiven.

### 47.1b Transaction table (rework — user decision 2026-11-14) — replaces proposal from 47.1a
Source: `R_transactions` ("The players agree on the terms of the transaction before exchanging any
components"), `F_hacan` ("Either player may initiate negotiations"), `R_promissory_notes` (hidden hand).
- Negotiation is a **table** for a pair of players (`GameState.transaction_tables`, `core/transaction_table.py`).
  **Either party** can open it (the active player or their neighbor — "either player may initiate").
- Each player places **only their own** items on the table (sees their own hand); the partner's items become visible
  only once they place them. There is no "what I can ask for" list (end of `requestable_notes`).
- Both parties click **Ready**; any change to table contents clears both Ready flags. When both are ready,
  the engine validates and executes the exchange (`transaction.resolve_transaction`, rules 47.1 unchanged:
  action phase, one party active, adjacency, one transaction per neighbor per turn, max 1 note).
- **Debt tokens (47.3) are an item on the table** (`debt_tokens` = how many of MY tokens I place). A table
  holding only debt is not a transaction: it works with any player, in any phase, and does not consume the limit.
  Components (TG, commodities, notes, fragments, cards…) may only be placed when a transaction is possible.
- Table contents are visible only to its parties; publicly, it is visible who is sitting at a table with whom.
- The table clears on a new turn (`action_budget.reset_turn`) and after the exchange executes; either party can close it.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: a bot does not place its own items; when the human clicks Ready, the bot evaluates the table (`bot_accepts`).

### 47.4 Trade — negotiating picks and "wash" (user decision 2026-11-14)
Source: `R_trade` (step 3: chosen players perform secondary for free; note: a deal whose part
resolves in a later step is **non-binding**), `R_commodities` (a given commodity becomes a TG).
"Wash" is a table convention (not a rule): after replenishing commodities, a player gives them to the Trade holder
for their TG (both sides get TG); customarily "wash −N" = the holder gives N fewer TG.
- In step 3 (`choose_free_players`), the card holder can sit at a table with ANY other player and place
  **"free Trade secondary"** (`trade_secondary`) on it. The other player places a price: debt tokens,
  components, or a **wash −N promise** (`wash`). Both Ready = deal: the player is chosen (free,
  mandatory secondary), debt tokens transfer immediately (table memory).
- Components and wash from the deal **do not transfer immediately** (they happen after replenishing — non-binding deal):
  once the chosen player replenishes commodities, the engine automatically opens a table **pre-filled** with the deal
  (wash: all their commodities ↔ commodities − N of the holder's TG, capped at how many TG the holder has).
  Both must click Ready — either can back out (non-binding). The exchange is a regular transaction (47.1).
- Components and wash may only be agreed upon with a player with whom a transaction is possible (neighbor / Guild Ships);
  with a non-neighbor, a deal = debt only ("I'll pay you for the refresh later").
- A player chosen without a table (regular checkbox) performs the secondary for free as before (30.6).

### 47.5 Transactions in the agenda phase (user report 2026-11-17) — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules2.com)
Source: `R_transactions` ("While resolving **each** agenda during the agenda phase, a player may perform
one transaction with each other player. Players do not need to be neighbors"; note: vetoed/discarded
and replaced agenda = new transaction), `R_agenda_phase` ("Each pair of players may resolve one transaction
during each agenda"), `C_agendas` (Miscount Disclosed: a transaction during Miscount does not block a transaction
when re-voting on the law).
- Window (`transaction.agenda_window`): AGENDA phase, agenda revealed (`agenda.agenda_id`), step other than
  `COMPLETE` — from reveal until the speaker's "next". Between agendas (reveal step), the table accepts debt only.
- **Any pair** of players (no adjacency required, neither needs to be active) — one transaction per agenda.
  Pair limit: entry in `transacted_with` of BOTH parties; cleared on every voting round opening
  (`agenda_phase._clear_round_of_voting`: new agenda, Veto/discard and replace, Miscount Disclosed).
- Table (47.1b) unchanged: the "initiating" party (`GameEvent.TRANSACTION` subject, actor in feed)
  is the player who opened the table. Commit label: `transaction:<partner>:<player>`.
- UI: **Transaction** button in the Council window bar and in the turn panel (the same table modal as in the action phase).

### 47.6 Chat and whispers (user decision 2026-11-17) — outside TI4 rules
Source: `R_deals` (deals at any time, even with non-neighbors) — chat is the negotiation space.
- `core/chat.py` + `GET /chat`, `POST /chat/send` (actor = sender). Message to all or
  **whisper** to one player: contents are visible only to sender and recipient, **others see that X whispers to Y**
  (user decision). Local mode / unseated host without a `player` parameter sees everything.
- Chat is NOT game state: it lives in the match file metadata (`chat`), Undo does not revert it. Push: public
  signal `{"type": "chat"}` with no content; the client refreshes only the chat log.
- Max 500 characters per message, log keeps the last 500.
- UI: `domains/chat/ChatPanel` — collapsed to a small icon with an unread badge (bottom left),
  expanded draggable panel; whisper recipient is selected via a player chip above the text field.
