## 49. Researching technology + Technology card — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules2.com) + ⚠ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Technology](../../../../rules_tirules/R_technology.md), [Technology (S.C.)](../../../../rules_tirules/R_technology_sc.md), [Technology notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_technology.md)

### 49.1. Researching (`core/technology.py`)

- Player deck = generic technologies + OWN faction technologies (`FactionDef.faction_tech`, with
  TE replacements). Other factions' technologies cannot be researched. A faction unit upgrade (`baseUpgrade`)
  removes from the deck the generic one it replaces (R_technology note).
- Generic = factionless cards from `technology`-type decks in assets, ONE version per card: when
  multiple versions exist (X-89 base/Ω/ΩΩ, Magen base/Ω/ΩΩ), the highest source priority wins (`SOURCE_PRIORITY`,
  Codexes = official errata, RULES.md 14).
- Prerequisites: letter in `requirements` → color (G biotic, Y cybernetic, B propulsion, R warfare;
  data convention, enforced by test). For each symbol, you must OWN a technology of that color; unit upgrades
  have no color and do not satisfy prerequisites.
- Planet specialty: exhausting a readied, owned planet ignores ONE symbol of the matching color
  (C_technology: one planet = one symbol). An exhausted planet cannot be used. Redundant skips
  (covering nothing) are rejected by the engine.
- AI Development Algorithm (text from assets): when researching a UNIT UPGRADE, exhausting the card
  ignores any 1 prerequisite. ⚠ First technology hook written in `core/technology.py` (constant
  `AIDA`) - to be moved to `core/rules_hooks.py` (RULES.md 43).
- "Gain" of a technology ignores prerequisites (`gain_technology`).
- ⚠ Not yet modeled (hooks): Nekro research ban, Jol-Nar Specialized Compounds, cost discounts.
- Breakthrough synergy (📎 [Synergy](../../../../rules_tirules/R_synergy.md), [Breakthroughs](../../../../rules_tirules/R_breakthroughs.md)):
  an UNLOCKED faction breakthrough (`breakthrough_unlocked`, pair from `BreakthroughDef.synergy`) allows treating
  every owned technology and every planet specialty of one color in the pair as either color of the pair -
  when researching (`technology.missing_prerequisites`, `specialty_planets`) and for objectives
  (`objective_progress`: diversify / same colour). Each technology counts as ONE color at a time.
  Works even when the breakthrough is exhausted; when locked, it has no effect. The breakthrough itself is not a technology.
  Without a color pair (Nekro) - no synergy. UI: the technology window always has a slot for the breakthrough
  (grayed out when locked, "not in effect"; unlocked: synergy markers on the pair's columns).

### 49.2. Technology card (`core/strategy_cards/technology.py`)

- Primary: 1 technology for free (optional - "can research"), then optionally a second for 6 resources;
  the second can use the first as a prerequisite (state after the first research).
- Secondary: 1 token from strategy pool + 4 resources → 1 technology.
- A planet exhausted for a specialty cannot also pay resources (it is exhausted - the engine rejects
  a shared planet in `skips` and `planets`). 1 TG = 1 resource; overpayment is allowed (forfeited).
- UI: fullscreen `TechnologyOverlay` (tree: 4 color columns × prerequisite tiers with
  connections, unit upgrades strip; panel: text, prerequisite status, specialty planets,
  payment; automatic selection of skips and payment, manually editable).
