Stage E3. After winning an invasion, the player gains control of the planet. Rule (source: AsyncTI4
`UnitHolder.addControl` = `controlList.clear(); controlList.add(cc)` and `AddPlanetService.addPlanet`
removing the previous owner via `player_.removePlanet`): a planet has EXACTLY
ONE owner — the new owner overwrites the previous one. Model: `SystemState.planet_owners[planet]`.

Implementation: `core/tactical.capture_planet(state, grid, position, planet, faction_key)` —
sets `planet_owners[planet] = player_id` (resolved as in God-Mode). The application does NOT
verify whether the invasion actually succeeded — the operator confirms via the "Capture"
button in the ground combat modal. Planet control is independent of space control (another
player's ships may remain in the system — RULES.md 19).
