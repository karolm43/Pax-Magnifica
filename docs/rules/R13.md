## 13. Strategy Phase (Strategy Card Selection) — ✅ VERIFIED (data + Java bot)

📎 tirules: [Strategy Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_phase.md) · [Strategy Card](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_card.md) · [Strategic Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategic_action.md) · [Trade Goods](../../../../rules_tirules/R_trade_goods.md) · [Initiative Order](../../../../rules_tirules/R_initiative_order.md)

Source (not from memory): card data `assets/data/strategy_cards/pok.json` +
`assets/data/strategy_card_sets/strategyCardSets.json`; logic from AsyncTI4 bot:
`StrategyCardManager.incrementTradeGoods`, `PickStrategyCardButtonHandler` (grant TG on
pick), `PlayStrategyCardService`. Verified 2026-10 against bot code.

**Card set (default PoK, 8 cards):** each has `initiative` (1–8) and `name`:
1 Leadership, 2 Diplomacy, 3 Politics, 4 Construction, 5 Trade, 6 Warfare,
7 Technology, 8 Imperial. Initiative = card number. Data read from registry per
`enabled_sources.json` (set `pok`). Zero hardcoding of names/numbers.

**Pick rule:**
- In the strategy phase, selection proceeds **clockwise starting from the speaker**
  (speaker order — RULES.md 24). Each player picks one card from the pool in turn.
- **Pick_Count (cards per player) — depends on player count:**
  - **2 players → 2 cards/player** — ⚠️ **ASSUMPTION** (official TI4 does not define a
    2-player mode). We adopt a variant consistent with the 3–4 player threshold (2 cards/player, with
    a SECOND pick round), so that a solo vs 1 bot / 1v1 hot-seat engine works in the strategy
    phase. To be confirmed by the user; changeable in a single place
    (`strategy_phase.pick_count`) if another variant is preferred (e.g., 3 cards/player).
  - **3–4 players → 2 cards/player** (the game specifies a SECOND pick round, see below),
  - **5–8 players → 1 card/player**,
  - **player count < 2 or > 8 → UNSUPPORTED (error).** The engine rejects determining
    `Pick_Count` and does not mutate phase state.

  Source: tirules [Strategy Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_phase.md) — Step 1: "When
  playing with three or four players, each player will choose a second strategy card.";
  LRR "Strategy Phase" (card count depends on player count; 3–4 players → two pick
  rounds). Range 3–8 players = officially supported TI4/PoK player counts.
- **SECOND pick round (3–4 players only):** after the last player has received their
  FIRST card, selection **returns to the speaker** and proceeds AGAIN in speaker order, until
  every player has `Pick_Count` (= 2) cards. Mechanically: the "next picker"
  is the first player in speaker order (counting from the speaker) who has fewer cards than `Pick_Count` —
  therefore the second round naturally starts from the speaker, without a separate round counter.
  Source: tirules [Strategy Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_phase.md) — Step 1, last
  bullet: "After the last player has received their first strategy card, each player
  chooses a second strategy card, starting with the speaker and proceeding clockwise.";
  LRR "Strategy Phase".
- **Transition validation to Action Phase:** the strategy phase **cannot end** until
  EVERY player has exactly `Pick_Count` cards. As long as any player is below
  `Pick_Count`, the engine indicates the next picker instead of allowing the transition;
  an attempt to transition with incomplete picks is rejected (error), and the phase remains
  unchanged. Action phase turn order is determined by initiative (lowest number of picked card —
  RULES.md 9). Source: tirules [Strategy Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_phase.md)
  ("Then, players proceed to the action phase." — after completing Step 1/Step 2, i.e., after
  picks are completed) + [Initiative Order](../../../../rules_tirules/R_initiative_order.md); LRR
  "Strategy Phase".
- **Order consistency on STRATEGY→ACTION transition (engine check):** the transition
  requires a non-empty `seating_order` to match the set of players
  (`set(seating_order) == set(players)`); a mismatch (a real God-Mode edge case: player
  added without updating the seat list) is rejected with a clear engine error
  (`ensure_strategy_complete`) BEFORE initiative is determined — this is a
  referee-engine check, not a TI4 rule. An empty `seating_order` is OK (order then follows player
  IDs).
- **Trade goods on cards:** each UNPICKED card holds a trade goods pool (starts at 0).
  When a player picks a card, they **receive all TGs from that card**, and the card's counter resets
  to zero (`PickStrategyCardButtonHandler`: `player.setTg(tg + tgCount)`, then
  `setScTradeGood(scPicked, 0)`).
- **TG accumulation:** at the end of the strategy phase (when advancing to the next round), +1 TG is
  added to every card that NOBODY picked (`StrategyCardManager.incrementTradeGoods`:
  for each `scNumber` not among picked → `tgCount+1`). This causes unwanted cards to grow in TGs.

**Initiative for Action Phase:** turn order is determined by the lowest picked card number
(already implemented in `turn_flow.turn_order`, RULES.md 9).

**Primary/secondary (ability text):** `primaryTexts`/`secondaryTexts` from card data —
full effect resolution (e.g., Leadership: +3 CC; Trade: +3 TG) belongs to later stages (E4/strategic
actions). In E2 we implement the skeleton: card pool, selection, TG accumulation/transfer,
marking a card as PLAYED (exhausted). Primary/secondary effects as a separate step.

Implementation: `src/core/strategy_phase.py` (pure functions) + model
`GameState.strategy_card_tg`, `GameState.speaker_id`, `PlayerState.exhausted_strategy_cards`;
endpoints `POST /strategy/pick`, `/strategy/play`, `/strategy/reset`.
