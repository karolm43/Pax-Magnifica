## 56. Player Decisions and Technology Offers (Never Automatic) — user decision 2026-11-09

📎 tirules: [Abilities](../../../../rules_tirules/R_abilities.md) · [Technology notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_technology.md) · [Timing](../../../../rules_tirules/R_timing.md)

**56.1 Rule.** Whenever a technology/card ability contains a player choice or "may", the PLAYER decides whether to use it and makes all choices via the UI; the engine never picks the "best" target automatically. Technical exception: when there is exactly one legal response (`offer_inputs.needs_player` = False), there is nothing to decide and the engine applies it immediately.

**56.1a "Auto" ONLY for spending and combat (user decision 2026-11-27, replaces "choice + Auto ALWAYS").** An "Auto" option (`offer_inputs.choice/number(..., auto=)`, UI `AutoButton`) is allowed ONLY where it makes economic sense — spending resources / influence / trade goods, payments (planets to exhaust, TG; cost of production, technology, objectives, command-token purchase) — and in combat (hit assignment and cancelling, combat dice sources, Skilled Retreat pool, ship picked for a combat bonus). EVERY other decision (target system/planet on the map, card, unit, player, token, order, action, yes/no) is an explicit manual choice with NO "Auto": an omitted choice is refused (`ValueError`), an omitted number = its minimum (where the UI stepper starts), an omitted optional field (`choice(..., optional=True)`, e.g. the planet for a 2nd Mercer ground force when only one is moved) is skipped. Removed in this pass: every faction "Auto" of RULES.md 62.19-62.23 except combat ones, card fields of 53.9 / 53.10 / 53.11 except Bribery, Shields Holding and Skilled Retreat, the removal window of 39 / 42 (starts empty), command-token distribution (status phase, Leadership, Warfare, Predictive Intelligence) and the Hil Colish "before / after" order (62.19).

**56.2 Mechanism.** An event hook (`rules_hooks.on_event`) has options: `optional=True` ("may" offer — can be declined), `decide=True` (MANDATORY decision: no right to decline), `inputs` (choice description computed by the engine, same format as the action card form), and `decider` (when someone other than the source owner decides, e.g., the victim of Assault Cannon). `triggers.fire` records the offer (`PendingOffer`: decider, `owner_id`, `mandatory`) and opens the REACTION window for the decider; the response validates via `offer_inputs.validate_params` against the legal options computed NOW. Endpoints: `GET /offers`, `POST /offers/accept|decline` (with `actor_id`, RULES.md 44.1); web-data: `pendingOffers`; UI: `OffersModal` (non-dismissible for mandatory decisions).

**56.3 Flows waiting for decisions.** Combat: start-of-round and end-of-round hooks may leave offers, in which case `ActiveCombat.step = TRIGGERS` (`resume` = what to resume from) and combat proceeds only after a response (`battle.resume_after_triggers`, called from `offers.accept|decline`). A tactical action cannot be ended/left while unresolved offers remain in the REACTION window (`tactical._check_can_leave`), and `end_turn` blocks on any open window.

**56.4 Technologies converted to decisions.** Assault Cannon (`asc`, victim decides), Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ (`md`, hit target), Duranium Armor (`da`), Infantry II (`inf2`), "may" offers: Dacxive Animators (`dxa`: place infantry after winning ground combat), Bio-Stims (`bs`: ready a planet with a specialty or a technology at the end of turn). Gravity Drive: player toggle (`appStore.gravityDrive`). Continued in section 57.

**56.5 Board location selection, not a list (user decision 2026-11-16).** A "where" choice (planet / system) is never a long list of "X on Y #n": targets glow on the map (`useMapPick`), clicking a target opens the choices of what can be done THERE, and identical units at one location use a −/+ counter (`GroupedMapField`, `optionKinds.stacksOf`). The engine describes a unit option at a location via `offer_inputs.unit_at` (unit + `planet`/`position`), and planet → planet transfers via `number(..., meta=route_meta(...))` (UI: source, then target = +1, `RouteNumbersField`). Targetless options ("Do not place", "Decline", at most 2) remain as tiles next to the map. The field value stays the same as before (option id), so engine validation does not change.

**56.5a ZERO planet and technology lists (user decision 2026-11-16, VERY IMPORTANT).** Every decision field whose options are planets or systems (including with planet `meta.ref` and without grouping) is selected EXCLUSIVELY on the map (`GroupedMapField`): the modal does not show a target list, only selected items (chip with ×) and the prompt "Click a glowing planet on the map". Technology options (including with tech `meta.ref`) are selected in the technology tree (`TechPickField` → `TechnologyOverlay` in "gain" mode, without payment) — never a list. This also applies to Bioplasmosis (`RouteNumbersField`), Transit Diodes, and Pride of Kenara in a transaction. Exception: exhausting YOUR OWN planet cards (payment, voting, Diplomacy) — those are planet cards (`PlanetCard`), just like in the physical game. The target pool for "any planet" = the entire board (`system_planets.board_planets`), not just systems with an entry in state.

**56.5 Assault Cannon is its own stage of the combat start** (C_technology assault-cannon 3.1). `battle.start_combat` first fires `COMBAT/START` for every hook EXCEPT Assault Cannon (`triggers.fire(hook_filter=...)`); once all resulting offers are answered (`resume_after_triggers`, resume `"start"`) the Assault Cannon stage fires (`battle._assault_cannon_stage`, resume `"start_assault"`). Ships added by start-of-combat abilities therefore count toward the 3-ship requirement and every other player has had their start-of-combat abilities before it resolves. ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: "each other player may use one ability" = their whole start-of-combat offer window precedes Assault Cannon (more than one ability is possible).
