## 60. Legendary Planets — Ability Cards, Readiness, and Abilities
📎 tirules: [Legendary Planets](../../../../rules_tirules/R_legendary_planets.md) · [Legendary planet notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_legendary_planets.md) · [Muaat](../../../../rules_tirules/F_muaat.md) · [Keleres](../../../../rules_tirules/F_keleres.md) · [Exhausted](../../../../rules_tirules/R_exhausted.md) · [The Fracture](../../../../rules_tirules/R_fracture.md)
Ability texts come exclusively from assets (`assets/planets/*.json`: `legendaryAbilityName/Text`, active sources). The player decides themselves (R56): "you may" is an offer in the decision modal, options are computed by the engine.

**60.1 Ability card = separate component.** A player who gains control of a legendary planet also gains its ability card (R_legendary_planets). Hook source is `SOURCE_PLANET` (`rules_hooks.owned_sources`: every controlled planet with `legendaryAbilityName`). The card exhausts and readies **independently of the planet card** (`PlayerState.exhausted_legendaries` via `core/exhaust.py`: `exhaust_legendary` / `ready_legendary` / `ready_all_legendaries`); "ready a planet" effects do not touch it, it readies in status phase (step 6). When control changes (`control.set_planet_control`), the card moves with the planet: from the deck (no controller) it arrives ready, from a player it preserves its exhausted state. Action cards placed on the card (Garbozia) stay with it (`GameState.legendary_cards`).
**60.2 Windowed abilities (offers).** "At the end of your turn" = `TURN/END`, "when you pass" = `PLAYER_PASSED/WHEN`, "after you activate a system" = `SYSTEM_ACTIVATED/AFTER`. An offer appears only when the card is ready and every choice has a legal answer (`core/hooks/planets_legendary.py`, `_register_timed`). Multiple cards can be used at the end of a single turn; "end your turn" does not take away the ability.
**60.3 ACTION.** An ability with an `ACTION:` header is played as a component action: `component_action.play_component_action(component_id="planet:<id>")`; effect `register_effect("planets:<id>")` + `register_effect_inputs`, condition `planet_abilities.blocker`. API: `GET /planet-ability/{id}/form` (form), played via `POST /tactical/component-action`. UI: "Play a component action" list (kind Legendary planet) + `LegendaryUseModal`.
**60.4 Implemented.**
- *Exterrix Headquarters* (`mallice`, `lockedmallice`): end of turn, 2 TG or convert all commodities to TG.
- *Flight Academy* (`illusion`, `mirage`, `phantasm`): end of turn, up to 2 fighters from reinforcements to a system with your own ship.
- *Imperial Arms Vault* (`hopesend`): end of turn, a mech from reinforcements onto your own planet or an action card. Empty mech reinforcements: a mech is removed from the board (a system without your own command token) and placed undamaged (R_legendary_planets).
- *The Atrament* (`primor`): end of turn, up to 2 infantry onto ONE of your own planets (not onto Custodia Vigilia).
- *The Acropolis* (`emelpar`): end of turn, readies another exhausted component that is not a strategy card: planet, technology, relic, leader, breakthrough, or legendary ability card; including one belonging to another player.
- *The Galactic Council* (`mrte`): end of turn, discards an unscored secret objective (returns to deck, shuffled), then draws (may draw the same one); without an unscored secret objective it cannot be exhausted.
- *Maxis Central Control* (`faunus`): pass, gains control of a non-home, non-legendary planet without units (including neutral) and attachments; an uncontrolled planet is explored.
- *Dok 'N Pic's Salvage Yard* (`garbozia`): pass, places an action card from the discard pile faceup ON the card (`card_draw.place_on_legendary_card`).
- *Aeurex Mechanica* (`industrex`): pass, 1 ship from reinforcements matching a unit upgrade the player owns, to a system with their ships (type resolved via `resolve_unit_variant`).
- *4X41D "Hyperion" VI / Barren Husk* (`ordinian`, `ordinianc4`): pass, draws an action card and takes 1 command token from reinforcements into a chosen pool.
- *A Song Like Marrow* (`styx`): +1 VP when gained (`PLANET_CONTROL_GAINED/AFTER`), −1 VP when lost (`PLANET_CONTROL_LOST/WHEN`, card is still with the player).
- *Ionian Fuel Refinery* (`tempesta`): offer after activating a system; exhausting grants an effect for the tactical action (`Duration.TACTICAL_ACTION`) with a MODIFY hook on `movement.move_value`. Own movement toggle (`tempesta` in `/tactical/move` and `/tactical/move-range`, `GameState.tempesta_used`, `web-data extraMoveBoostActive/tempestaUsed`), independent of Gravity Drive; for a ship short on movement, the engine tries a single bonus first, then both at once.
- *Jupiter Brain* (`thundersedge`): when gaining the card, faction breakthrough (like any gain: unlock + roll for The Fracture; `expeditions.gain_breakthrough`); end of turn: bonus action via `turn_flow.begin_bonus_action` (like Puppets on a String; after the action, the `passed` flag returns to its pre-bonus state).
- *The Nucleus* (`avernus`): ACTION, exhaust, STAR FORGE without token: 2 fighters or 1 destroyer from reinforcements into a system with your own war sun (F_muaat: cannot be used without a war sun on the board).
- *Custodian's Favour* (`custodiavigilia`): controlled Mecatol Rex (`mr`, `mrte`) has SPACE CANNON (threshold and dice from the planet's `spaceCannonHitsOn/DieCount`) and PRODUCTION (value from ability text): extra shooter in `space_cannon._system_shooters` and producer in `production.producers` (`legendary_planets.mecatol_grants_planet`). Second clause: event `IMPERIAL_VICTORY_POINT` (VP from Mecatol control in Imperial) gives another player with the card 2 tokens from reinforcements, pools chosen by the player (mandatory decision).
**60.5 Fracture and relics.** A player who gains control of a planet in The Fracture (positions `frac1..7`) that was not controlled by another player draws a relic (R_fracture; `control.set_planet_control`, deck registry from `rules_hooks.bound_registry` when the caller did not supply it: landing, production, manual takeover).
**60.6 Salvage Yard play.** ✅ Dok 'N Pic's Salvage Yard (C_legendary_planets /2): the controller of Garbozia plays a card lying on it "as if in hand" (`card_draw.salvage_yard_cards` / `borrow_salvage_yard_card`; `action_cards.play_action_card`, `playability` rows carry `from_planet`, `card_inputs`). The ability card need NOT be readied; the played card is PURGED (`GameState.salvage_yard_borrowed`, not the discard pile); the new controller plays the cards that stayed (/5). ✅ User decision (2026-11-28): "purge" = the card leaves the game when it leaves the hand (also if Sabotage cancels it). Frontend: no tray for the yard cards yet (API `/action-card/playable` already lists them). Not implemented: ⚠ Regular Muaat STAR FORGE (faction ability with a token from strategy pool). ⚠ Custodia Vigilia: enters play exhausted, cannot hold units, and is not adjacent (F_keleres) only in Keleres data/hook. ⚠ Nano-Forge does not create an ability card (per C_relics).
