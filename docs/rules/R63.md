## 63. Empty Reinforcements → Unit from the Board; C_/F_ Rulings Audit (2026-11-15)
📎 tirules: [Component Limitations](../rules_tirules/R_component_limitations.md) · [Agendas](../rules_tirules/C_agendas.md) · [Action Cards](../rules_tirules/C_action_cards.md) · [Exploration](../rules_tirules/C_exploration_cards.md) · [Relics](../rules_tirules/C_relics.md) · [Mentak](../rules_tirules/F_mentak.md) · [Sardakk N'orr](../rules_tirules/F_norr.md) · [Winnu](../rules_tirules/F_winnu.md) · [Hacan](../rules_tirules/F_hacan.md)

### 63.1 General Rule (R_component_limitations)
"When a player would place a unit, if there are none of that type left in their reinforcements, that player can
remove a unit from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and place that unit (…) Abilities
cannot force a player to remove and place a unit in this manner." Notes: a removed unit is placed
UNDAMAGED; "replace" works the same way.

- ONE place: `core/board_reinforcements.py` (`needs_board_source`, `board_sources`, `take_from_board`,
  `place_unit`, `source_options`). Source = `"<position>|<zone>"`. Structures from strategy cards
  (`strategy_cards.common.board_structure_sources`) delegate here.
- Always the player's decision: the form shows sources (and "do not place" when an ability places mandatorily).
- Production (`production.produce`): `ProductionOrder.sources`; missing units beyond reinforcements require specifying
  sources. View `/tactical/production/view` → `units[].boardSources`; the production modal shows "take from
  the board" chips when reinforcements run out. Applies to every production (including Salvage Operations, Freelancers, Duha
  Menaimon, Dirzuga Rophal).
- Usages: Rearmament Agreement "For" (C_agendas), Infiltrate (C_action_cards: separate choice for each structure,
  `sources:<type>`), Local Fabricators (C_exploration_cards, option `params`), Sleeper Cell and Salvage Operations
  (F_mentak), Reclamation/Reclaimer (F_winnu), Letani Ospha (F_arborec, own version).

### 63.2 Fixes from C_agendas Audit
1. Classified Document Leaks rejected: the objective remains in `objectives.revealed`, but `objectives.retire` disables it
   from scoring ("no longer scorable … It remains in play").
2. Wormhole Research: structures in space (Saar Floating Factory) remain; ships are destroyed, ground forces
   are removed without "destroyed".
3. Search Warrant / Archived Secret: cards enter the hand FACEUP, excess over the limit is returned by the player afterward
   (`secret_objectives.draw_to_hand` + "return … to the deck" decision). 3 scored + drawn = immediately to deck.
4. Colonial Redistribution: the chosen player receives an OFFER to place infantry ("may").
5. Homeland Defense Act rejected: PDS beyond 2 on a planet are removed (R_structures: max 2).
6. Ixthian Artifact / Wormhole Research / Technology Rider: allowed to exhaust a planet with a specialty or AI
   Development Algorithm to ignore a prerequisite (`technology.plan_with_skips`, `skips` input).

### 63.3 Fixes from F_* Audit
- F_norr (G'hom Sek'kus): no forces through a wormhole under Enforced Travel Ban / Nexus Sovereignty
  (`movement.wormhole_link_blocked`).
- F_winnu (Reclaimer): one structure per EACH Reclaimer on the planet (planet structure limit).
- F_hacan (Pride of Kenara): units moved from a planet in a gravity rift roll for removal
  (`movement.roll_rift_exit`).

### 63.4 Exploration and Relics
- Demilitarized Zone: after returning ground forces to orbit, the CAPACITY window opens (C_exploration_cards).
- Book of Latvinia: the engine accepts only technologies WITHOUT prerequisites (C_relics: no specialty skips
  nor Analytical).

### 63.5 Open (⚠️ pending user decision)
- Ion Storm (`core/ion_storm.py`, assumption 2): when a system with the token has its own wormhole of the same type, the engine
  assumes using the token. Ruling C_exploration_cards: the player CHOOSES the wormhole, the token flips only when choosing
  the token — requires a choice in movement (movement UI), not implemented.
- JR-XS455-O (`relics_actions.py`): the chosen player's response is provided in the owner's form, not by the chosen player
  themselves (networked game) — not contradicting the ruling, but contrary to RULES.md 56.
