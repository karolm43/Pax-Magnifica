## 59. Relics — Ownership, "ACTION:" Actions, Passive and Event-Driven Effects
📎 tirules: [Relics](../../../../rules_tirules/R_relics.md) · [Relic notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_relics.md) · [Component Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_component_action.md) · [Exhausted](../../../../rules_tirules/R_exhausted.md) · [Purge](../../../../rules_tirules/R_purge.md)
Relic texts come exclusively from assets (`assets/data/relics`, active sources). The player decides themselves (R56): options are computed by the engine.

**59.1 Ownership.** A relic lies faceup in front of the player (`PlayerState.relics`), not in hand. A player gains one from the relic deck (`core/relics.draw_relic`: The Fracture planets, frontier "Draw 1 relic", fragments). Relics cannot be traded (exceptions: Silver Flame, Black Market Dealings). An eliminated player purges their relics. Exhaustion: `PlayerState.exhausted_relics` via `core/exhaust.py` (`exhaust_relic` / `ready_relic` / `ready_all_relics`; readied in status phase, step 6).
**59.2 Loss and purge.** `relics.transfer` (giver loses, receiver gains; card arrives READY), `relics.detach` (leaves play area without purge, e.g. Nano-Forge), `relics.purge_relic` (card goes to `DecksState.purged`, does not return to deck). "When you lose this card" effects register via `relics.on_lost(<id>)` (fires on transfer, detach, and purge). Gaining fires `COMPONENT_GAINED/WHEN` with `component="relics:<id>"` (`where` filter in hook, because all of the player's relics see the event).
**59.3 Relic ACTION = component action.** A relic with an `ACTION:` header in its text (`relic_actions.is_action_relic`) is played via `component_action.play_component_action(component_id=<relic>, params)` (consumes the turn's action) and **stays** with the player (exhausts or purges via its own effect). Effect: `register_effect("relics:<id>")` + `register_effect_inputs`; playability condition: `relic_actions.blocker(<id>)` (e.g. relic exhausted). API: `GET /relic/playable`, `GET /relic/{id}/inputs`, `POST /relic/play` (with `actor_id`). `run_effect=False` in `play_component_action` = dry run (UI playability). A relic with ACTION without a coded effect is played as an action, returning `implemented=false` (effect applied manually, God-Mode).
**59.4 UI.** **Relics** tab in the bottom hand dock: all of the player's relics (from `playerData.relics`). ACTION relics can be dragged onto the "Drop to use" zone (form `ActionCardPlayModal kind="relic"`), only legal ones right now can be picked up; passive ones are marked "Passive" and view-only. Relics also appear in the player panel (like technologies, dimmed when exhausted) and in the "Play a component action" list.
**59.5 Implemented (engine).**
- *The Codex* (`codex`): choose up to 3 action cards from the discard pile (copies counted separately), purge. Empty action card deck = discard pile immediately becomes a new deck (C_relics: "as soon as the action card deck has zero cards", `card_draw.reshuffle_action_deck_if_empty`), so Codex has nothing to take then.
- *Dynamis Core* (`dynamiscore`): passively commodity value +2 (hook point `economy.commodity_value`, MODIFY hook); ACTION: TG equal to commodity value (computed BEFORE purge), purge; upon loss, excess commodities return to supply (`economy.cap_commodities`).
- *Book of Latvinia* (`bookoflatvinia`): when gained, research up to 2 technologies without prerequisites (mandatory "up to 2" offer); ACTION: purge, 4 specialty types → 1 VP, otherwise speaker token (mandatory, no choice). ⚠ ASSUMPTION: Nekro exception (6 tokens instead of technologies) omitted.
- *Circlet of the Void* (`circletofthevoid`): passively ignores anomalies in movement and enters asteroid fields (PERMIT hooks, even when exhausted); gravity rift still grants +1 movement, just without rolling (C_relics; hook point `movement.ignore_anomalies_keep_rift` — unlike Nav Suite, which grants no bonus, C_action_cards); ACTION: exhaust, explore frontier in a system with no other players' ships (neutral units do not count).
- *Nano-Forge* (`nanoforge`): ACTION: attaches to your own non-legendary, non-home planet (`exploration.attach_to_planet`, id `nanoforge` = attachment id: +2/+2, `planet_stats.is_legendary`). The planet is legendary for objectives (*Make History*, *Become a Legend*, `objective_progress`), Shard of the Throne, and Stellar Converter, but has no legendary ability card (C_relics).
- *Stellar Converter* (`stellarconverter`): ACTION: target = non-home, non-legendary planet (not Mecatol Rex, not yours) in a system ADJACENT to a system with a unit having BOMBARDMENT; units are destroyed, planet loses controller and attachments (purged), goes to `SystemState.destroyed_planets` (disappears from `planets_of_system`).
- *The Silver Flame* (`thesilverflame`): ACTION: d10 roll (`record_table_roll`, physical die), purge; 10 → 1 VP; otherwise units in home system disappear, its planets lose controller and leave the game (player does not satisfy public objective condition: `controls_home_planets`), Fracture enters play (`fracture.put_into_play`). ⚠ ASSUMPTION: "purge home system" = removal of units and planets, tile stays; trading in a transaction not handled.
- *Shard of the Throne* (`shard`): +1 VP when gained, −1 VP when lost (`on_lost`); transfers to the player who gains control of the owner's legendary planet (including via Nano-Forge) or their home system planet (`PLANET_CONTROL_GAINED/AFTER`, immediately).
- *The Obsidian* (`obsidian`): when gained, draws a secret objective (on excess, player chooses what to return); secret objective limit +1 (hook point `objectives.secret_limit`, `secret_objectives.secret_limit`).
- *Maw of Worlds* (`mawofworlds`): "may" offer at the start of the agenda phase: purge, exhaust all planets, any technology (without prerequisites).
- *The Quantumcore* (`quantumcore`): when gained, gains faction breakthrough; SYNERGY for all types = PERMIT hook `technology.synergy_all` (`technology.synergy` then returns all four colors; `missing_prerequisites` and `objective_progress` count excess of any number of synergy colors). The Fracture ingress from breakthrough synergy does not change (the breakthrough that triggered it counts, not the relic).
- *JR-XS455-O* (`titanprototype`): ACTION, exhausts the relic (agent; readies in status). Form: chosen player (including owner), their response (structure on their planet or decline) and payment of 3 resources; decline = 1 TG. ⚠ ASSUMPTION: the chosen player's response is provided in the form (local mode / table agreement); the engine validates that the payment and planet belong to the chosen player. Structures per `strategy_cards/common.placeable_structures`.
- *Scepter of Emelpar* (`emelpar`): on a strategy card secondary, the player may exhaust the relic and spend a token from REINFORCEMENTS instead of the strategy pool (`scepter` parameter, toggle in secondary modal; `command_tokens.spend_strategy_token`). If reinforcements are empty, refused (C_relics). ⚠ Other places spending from the pool (token objectives, Diplomacy, retreat) do not yet have this option.
- *The Crown of Emphidia* (`emphidia`): offer after a tactical action (exhaust, explore 1 controlled planet with a trait); offer at the end of the status phase when you control a planet with the "Tomb of Emphidia" card (`toe`): purge for 1 VP.
- *Dominus Orb* (`dominusorb`): offer after activating a system (before movement): purge and tactical-action effect that lifts the ban on moving/picking up from systems with your own command token (R3/T4, hook point `movement.ignore_own_command_token`).
- *The Prophet's Tears* (`prophetstears`): skip `relic:prophetstears` during research ignores 1 prerequisite (`technology.plan_research`, `researchable_techs`, tree form), or offer "draw 1 action card"; both exhaust the relic.
- *Neuraloop* (`neuraloop`): offer after revealing a public objective (`OBJECTIVE_REVEALED`, now emitted from `objectives.reveal_next_public`): purge your own relic (or attached Nano-Forge), discarded objective returns to its deck (shuffled), a new random one from any objective deck (including secret) takes its place and fires "when revealed" again. The purged relic's own purge ability does NOT resolve; a loss effect (Shard −1 VP) does. ⚠ Purging The Obsidian does not yet force discarding a secret objective when holding four.
- *The Triad* (`thetriad`): pseudo-planet `relic:thetriad` (`core/relic_planets.py`) in payments (`payment.ready_planet_values`) and voting (`agenda_phase.voting_planets`); value 3 + number of DIFFERENT fragment types; exhaust state in `exhausted_relics`, readied by planet-readying effects (`exhaust.ready_planets/ready_all_planets`). Outside `planet_owners`, so it is not a planet for other effects.
- *Lightrail Ordnance* (`lightrailordnance`): hook `space_cannon.granted` grants space dock SPACE CANNON 5 (x2) with range to adjacent systems (Offense and Defense).
- *Metali Void Shielding* (`metalivoidshielding`): hook `combat.granted_sustain`; `CombatUnit.granted` = 1 for a non-fighter ship without its own Sustain, `plan_losses` allows using it at most once per assignment, `required_hits` counts +1 absorbed hit.
- *Metali Void Armaments* (`metalivoidarmaments`): offer at start of space combat (accept → `ActiveCombat.afb_extra`); `battle.resolve_afb` adds an AFB 6 (x3) roll and the AFB step enters combat even if no one has AFB.
- *The Crown of Thalnos* (`thalnos`) and *Heart of Ixth* (`heartofixth`): tools above round dice in the assign-hits modal before anyone assigns (`battle.reroll_with_relic`, `battle.adjust_die_with_relic`; API `POST /tactical/battle/relic-reroll|relic-adjust`, panel `BattleRelicTools`). Thalnos: chosen dice rerolled with +1, a unit with no hit after reroll is destroyed; Heart: exhaust, ±1 to one die of either side. Hits recounted, assignment queue rebuilt. ⚠ Heart currently works on combat dice; Space Cannon, bombardment, and "on the table" rolls have no window to modify the result.
**59.6 Not implemented.** *Silver Flame*: purge home system TILE (decision 2026-11-11: effect will be added later; today units and planets disappear). Trading Silver Flame in a transaction. Faction exceptions (including Nekro with Book of Latvinia) intentionally omitted.
**59.7 Fragments → relic (fragment ACTION).** 📎 [Exploration](../../../../rules_tirules/R_exploration.md) · [Relics](../../../../rules_tirules/R_relics.md) · [Exploration card notes](../../../../rules_tirules/C_exploration_cards.md). Card text (asset `explores.json`): "ACTION: Purge 3 of your cultural|hazardous|industrial relic fragments to gain 1 relic." — count, type, and reward read from card text. *Unknown Relic Fragment* ("counts as a relic fragment of any type") is a wildcard, but three Unknown cannot be purged — min. 1 cultural/hazardous/industrial fragment (C_exploration_cards). Empty relic deck: the player MAY purge fragments as an action and gains no relic (R_relics, Notes). This is a component action (consumes the turn's action, `component_action.play_component_action(component_id="fragments:<type>")`). Engine: `core/relic_forge.py` (`options` = legal sets with automatic selection: fragments of that type first, wildcards last; `forge` = validation + purge `relic_fragments.purge_chosen` + `relics.draw_relic`). API: `GET /relic/forge/options`, `POST /relic/forge`. UI: Relics tab in the hand dock, "Forge relic" button.
**59.8 Relic reveal (spectacle for everyone).** Every draw from the relic deck (`relics.draw_relic`) records a public `GameState.relic_reveal` (`seq` increments, like `table_roll`): who, which relic, source (`fragments` + purged fragments, `fracture` + planet, `explore` = e.g., Dead World). A relic lies faceup, so this reveals no secrets. UI (`domains/relics/RelicRevealWatcher`) plays the scene for all players; front graphic = relic back + name and text from assets (to be swapped for card art).
