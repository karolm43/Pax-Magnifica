## 30. Strategic action: primary + secondary window — ✅ VERIFIED (LRR 2.0)

Milestone E8-T3. Source: local snapshot of official LRR 2.0 — `docs/rules_tirules/R_strategic_action.md`
and `R_leadership.md` (content © Fantasy Flight Games; see `docs/rules_tirules/SOURCE.md`).
Card parameters/texts from assets: `assets/data/strategy_cards/pok.json` (zero from memory).

### 30.1. Strategic action flow (R_strategic_action) — ✅ VERIFIED

- During the action phase, the active player may perform a STRATEGIC ACTION: they resolve the
  PRIMARY ability of their strategy card.
- After resolving the primary, EACH OTHER player, starting WITH THE PLAYER TO THE LEFT of the
  active player and proceeding CLOCKWISE, MAY resolve that card's SECONDARY ability (optional — pass).
- After each player has had an opportunity to resolve the secondary, the active player EXHAUSTS
  the card (flips it — cannot use it again this round; may pass thereafter).
- Primary and secondary effects are resolved TOP TO BOTTOM (order of lines on the card text).
- 3-4 player game: a player must resolve the strategic action of EACH of their cards before
  passing (applies to the 2-cards/player variant — currently we support 1 card/player, see RULES.md 13).

**Mapping to the timing window model (RULES.md 29.2):** playing a card = resolving primary
(active player) + OPENING a `STRATEGY_SECONDARY` window with a queue of the other players (from
the player after the active player, clockwise — this is EXACTLY the queue from
`timing.build_response_queue`, initiator skipped). Each window respondent resolves the secondary
or passes. Once the window closes, the card is exhausted.

### 30.2. Leadership — primary and secondary (R_leadership + data) — ✅ VERIFIED

- **Primary:** the active player gains 3 command tokens. Then they MAY spend any amount of
  influence, gaining 1 command token for every 3 influence spent.
- **Secondary:** each other player (in the window) MAY spend any amount of influence, gaining
  1 command token for every 3 influence spent. **Leadership Secondary does NOT require a
  strategy token** (exception — LRR note; most other cards require a token from the strategy pool).
- Gained tokens are placed into ANY pool (tactic/fleet/strategy) — player's choice.
- Excess influence (1-2 above a multiple of 3) is lost.

**⚠️ SCOPE OF T3 (intentional, "make it exist first"):** we implement the secondary window
SKELETON on a real card:
- Primary Leadership: +3 command tokens (into the tactic pool by default — ⚠️ ASSUMPTION:
  eventually the player chooses the pool; room for expansion).
- Secondary: window mechanics (queue, execute/pass) work end-to-end; the "execute" option does
  NOT yet buy tokens with influence (deferred — requires an influence-spending / planet-exhausting
  action model). Marked TODO in code. Buying tokens with influence (primary excess and secondary)
  will be added once the influence model exists (tie into `exhausted_planets` + planet influence values).

**Architecture for expansion:** resolving primary/secondary is a separate, extensible dispatch
by card (`core/strategic_action.py`), not an `if` in the endpoint. Each subsequent card = a new
dispatch branch + rule entry here with source. The window model carries context (initiative number),
so respond can resolve the proper secondary without changing the window shape.

### 30.5. Multi-step primary and secondary cost skeleton (E8-T5) — ✅ implemented

📎 tirules: [Strategic Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategic_action.md), [Strategy Card](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategy_card.md), [Action Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_action_phase.md)

- Rule: "A player can only resolve the primary of their own cards / secondary of cards chosen by
  other players". The server enforces: primary only `owner_id`, secondary only current window respondent.
- Primary may require choices → `GameState.pending_strategy` (step + draft), endpoint
  `POST /strategy/primary-step`; effect applied atomically after the last step, then secondary window.
  Until resolved, `End turn` is blocked.
- Secondary "follow" spends 1 token from the strategy pool (returns to reinforcements), except
  Leadership (LRR note) and players chosen by the active player (Trade — secondary is free and
  MANDATORY). No token → follow rejected (pass allowed). Passed players may follow (R_action_phase).
- Cards without a `CardSpec` entry default to no effect and no cost (as before) — we do not
  charge a token for an empty effect. A card gets its cost together with its effect implementation.
- ⚠️ SIMPLIFICATION: the card is exhausted upon play, not after the window closes (during the
  window nobody can use it again anyway; the outcome for the player is identical).

### 30.6. Trade — ✅ VERIFIED (R_trade)

Primary: +3 trade goods → replenish commodities up to faction sheet value → choose any other
players for a free, mandatory secondary (`choose_free_players` step). Secondary: replenish
commodities (cost: 1 token from strategy pool, unless chosen). Step order and the fact that
deals involving later steps are non-binding — R_trade note, outside engine scope. "When
replenishes commodities" effects (e.g., Trade Agreement) will arrive via hooks (E7).
User DECISION (2026-11): players chosen by the active player resolve the secondary AUTOMATICALLY
in their window order (no prompt and no token) — there is no choice in it, and a "follow"
window displaying a token cost was only confusing (`strategic_action._auto_resolve_free`).
Primary ORDER (F_vuilraith the-stillness-of-stars 4): +3 TG and the replenish happen right when the card is
played (`CardSpec.on_begin`, `strategic_action._begin_steps`), so "after replenishes" abilities (The Stillness of
Stars) get their REACTION window BEFORE the active player chooses the free-secondary players
(`choose_free_players`); `submit_primary_step` is blocked until that window closes.
Negotiating the price of being chosen (table, wash, debt) — RULES.md 47.4.

### 30.7. Leadership — spending influence — ✅ VERIFIED (R_leadership, R_influence, R_trade_goods)

📎 tirules: [Leadership](../../../../rules_tirules/R_leadership.md), [Influence](../../../../rules_tirules/R_influence.md), [Trade Goods](../../../../rules_tirules/R_trade_goods.md)

- Primary: 3 tokens + optional influence spend (1 token / 3 influence). Steps `spend_influence` →
  `distribute` (player chooses distribution across pools). Secondary: spend influence → tokens,
  WITHOUT strategy token; follow carries `payload` (planets, TG, distribution).
- Influence is spent by exhausting planet cards (`core/payment.py`); 1 TG = 1 influence. Excess
  (1–2) is lost. Tokens come from REINFORCEMENTS — at most as many are gained as are in
  reinforcements (⚠️ ASSUMPTION: tirules does not describe empty reinforcements, we apply the
  general "tokens come from reinforcements" rule).
- UI shape: `GET /strategy/options?player_id=` returns step options; `needs_input` in secondary
  indicates whether "follow" requires a selection view.
- User DECISION (UI, 2026-11-23, replaces earlier block on redundant spending): the window does
  NOT enforce multiples of 3 — payment can be any amount; any remainder not divisible by 3 is
  lost, and the UI merely informs about it. Payment = the same planet tiles as in offers
  (`PaymentPicker` → `Tile`).
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION ("Auto", primary and secondary): buys the MAXIMUM tokens the player can afford
  and that fit in reinforcements (`reinforcements − base_gain`), using the cheapest payment
  (largest planets without extras, then TG). The player can always pick manually or click
  "Continue without spending".
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION ("Auto" token distribution after purchase, Leadership; UI): all gained tokens
  go to the Tactic pool. "Auto" Warfare = unchanged (current distribution). The player can
  always distribute manually.

- UI (engine hint, not validation): secondary options carry `follow_blocked` = reason when
  "follow" would accomplish nothing (ready planet influence + TG < 3 or empty reinforcements).
  The "Follow" button is then disabled with that reason (dead end, plan U5/Z7). The engine
  still accepts follow with zero spend. A "free" respondent (must resolve) is not blocked.

### 30.8. Diplomacy (Codex I errata) — ✅ VERIFIED (R_diplomacy)

📎 tirules: [Diplomacy](../../../../rules_tirules/R_diplomacy.md)

Primary: system other than Mecatol with own planet → each other player (from active's left,
clockwise) places a token there from THEIR REINFORCEMENTS (skipped if they already have a token
there) → ready up to 2 exhausted planets. No legal system: action still allowed, nobody places
anything. Secondary (1 token strategy pool): ready up to 2 planets. ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: when a
player's reinforcements are empty, a token is taken from their sheet AUTOMATICALLY (tactic →
fleet → strategy), instead of prompting the player for a pool (tirules: "places one command token
of their choice from their command sheet") — to be optionally refined with a prompt for other players.

### 30.9. Politics — ✅ VERIFIED (R_politics)

📎 tirules: [Politics](../../../../rules_tirules/R_politics.md)

Primary in order: new speaker (player without speaker token, including active) → 2 action cards →
peek at top 2 agendas, each to top/bottom of deck (both on the same side → any order). PRIVATE
peek: step options returned only to the owner, draft does not contain the cards. Secondary (1 token):
2 action cards. Agenda deck is created lazily (`core/card_draw.ensure_agenda_deck`,
`DecksState.agenda_deck`). Speaker and 2 action cards are resolved immediately in the
`choose_speaker` step (card effect order), so the player sees drawn cards before ordering agendas;
the list of drawn cards sits in the private draft key `_drawn` (keys starting with `_` do not
go into public `pendingStrategy` in web-data).
⚠️ The 7-card hand limit is not modeled (as in the status phase, 41.5). ⚠️ `/state` exposes deck
contents (like the action deck) — to be hidden in the networking module.

### 30.10. Imperial — ✅ VERIFIED (R_imperial)

📎 tirules: [Imperial](../../../../rules_tirules/R_imperial.md)

Primary: optionally score 1 public objective (requires controlling ALL home system planets and
meeting requirements; objective may be from any stage — `score_public(ignore_phase=True)`), then:
control Mecatol Rex → +1 VP (mandatory), otherwise draw a secret objective. Limit of 3 secrets
(hand + scored): excess → return one UNSCORED to deck + shuffle; may also return the freshly
drawn one (`return: "drawn"`). Secondary (1 token): secret objective (with the same limit).

### 30.11. Warfare (Thunder's Edge `te6warfare`) — ⚠️ local tirules adaptation (R_warfare.md)

📎 tirules (rewritten locally for TE): [Warfare](../../../../rules_tirules/R_warfare.md)

Primary: redistribute tokens (optional) → tactical action in ANY system without a token
(`tactical.activate_system(free=True)`: no token is placed or spent, even when own token is in
the system) → full normal tactical action flow → redistribute after the action. Primary waits in
the `tactical_action` step; `tactical.end_tactical_action` resumes it from `redistribute_after`.
The whole thing is ONE strategic action (not an extra tactical action). Secondary (1 token
strategy pool): PRODUCTION of all units in home system — normal `standalone` production
(`ProductionGrant.standalone`), token does NOT land in the system; window queue waits for
"Finish production" (`await_production`).
⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS: tactical action from Warfare proceeds normally except for activation (moving OUT
of systems with own token is still forbidden); the BGA ruling on Nullification Field / Minister
of Peace (ends only the tactical action, Warfare resolves to completion) is not yet handled
because those cards are not in the engine. Additionally, `activate_system` now rejects activation
after another action in the same turn (one action per turn).

### 30.12. Construction (Thunder's Edge `te4construction`) — ⚠️ local tirules adaptation (R_construction.md)

📎 tirules (rewritten locally for TE): [Construction](../../../../rules_tirules/R_construction.md)

Primary: structure (PDS / space dock) OR PRODUCTION of one space dock → then a second structure.
Space dock production is normal production (`standalone`, restricted to the producer on the
chosen planet: `ProductionGrant.zone`); primary waits in the `production` step until
"Finish production" (`after_standalone_production`). Secondary (1 token): one structure on an
owned planet (no system choice and no token in system). Limits: 1 space dock and 2 PDS per
planet, none on a planet with Demilitarized Zone, structure from reinforcements.
✅ R_component_limitations (user decision 2026-11-17): when reinforcements are empty, the player
may remove that structure from a system without their own command token and immediately place
it (like Reclamation/Reclaimer, `core.board_reinforcements`). Step options: `placeable` (with
`allow_board=True`) + `board_sources` (structure → source planets); payload `source` = source
planet, selected by CLICKING on the map.

### 30.3. Strategic action is ONE of the turn actions — ✅ VERIFIED (R_action_phase)

📎 tirules: [Action Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_action_phase.md), [Strategic Action](../../../../rules_tirules/R_strategic_action.md)

On their turn, a player performs a SINGLE action: strategic, tactical, or component — or passes.
The engine (`strategic_action.play_strategy_card`) rejects playing a card when: phase ≠ ACTION,
it is not the player's turn, the player has passed, has already taken an action this turn, the
card is already exhausted, or another window is active. Passing requires playing EACH of the
player's cards (3–4 players: both). Expansion design for all cards: `docs/PROJEKT_KARTY_STRATEGII.md`.

**Implementation status:**
- ⬜→✅ T3: Leadership primary (+3 tokens) + secondary window (skeleton, execute/pass).
- ⬜ Remaining cards (Diplomacy, Politics, Construction, Trade, Warfare, Technology, Imperial) —
  primary/secondary added sequentially, each with a note here.
- ⬜ Influence model (spending / exhausting planets) — will unlock real token buying and other secondaries.

### 30.13. Queued secondary decisions (parallel queueing) — ⚠️ USER DECISION (2026-11-27)

- Source of the order: R_strategic_action ("beginning with the player to the left of the active player and proceeding clockwise, each other player may resolve the secondary ability"). The ORDER of resolution is kept; only the DECISION may be made earlier.
- While the primary is being resolved (`pending_strategy`) or the secondary window is open, every player still to be asked (clockwise queue, Mathis Mathinus limit) may queue Follow / Don't follow (`strategic_action.queue_secondary`; `POST /strategy/secondary/queue`, `/unqueue`; options `GET /strategy/queue-options`). The decision is applied when the window reaches the player (`_auto_resolve_free`) and validated only then: if it is no longer legal (no token, the payload no longer fits) it is dropped and the player is asked normally. Queueing again replaces the decision.
- Privacy: THAT a player queued is public (the UI shows who is ready), WHAT they queued is private until it resolves (`visibility.redact_reaction_windows`, web-data `secondaryQueue`). A new strategic action and the end of the window clear the queue.
- UI: `SecondaryQueueChip` (only for a player still waiting in that queue). Tests: `tests/test_secondary_queue.py`.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS: (a) the UI queues only decisions without extra input (Don't follow; Follow when the card needs no choices); (b) a queued Follow on a "free" secondary (Trade's chosen players) resolves like the automatic free resolution.
