## 51. Agenda phase: reveal, voting, outcome, planets — ✅ VERIFIED (tirules) / ⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Agenda Phase](../../../../rules_tirules/R_agenda_phase.md) · [Agenda Card](../../../../rules_tirules/R_agenda_card.md) · [Speaker](../../../../rules_tirules/R_speaker.md) · [Custodians Token](../../../../rules_tirules/R_custodians_token.md) · [Influence](../../../../rules_tirules/R_influence.md) · [Transactions](../../../../rules_tirules/R_transactions.md)

Stage E6. Code: `core/agenda_phase.py`, `api/routers/agenda.py`, `api/web_agenda.py` (web-data key `agendaPhase`),
frontend `domains/agenda/AgendaOverlay` (background `assets/extra/agenda2.jpg`). Card effects: section **51.6** (official deck
`agendas_pok`, 50/50 cards); laws in `decks.agenda_laws`, rest in `agenda_discard`.

**Rules (R_agenda_phase / R_agenda_card):**
- The phase is added to the round after the Custodians token is removed (`custodians_removed`). Two agendas: the speaker reveals
  the top card (reads all outcomes), players vote, the outcome is resolved. Step 3: all players ready their planets;
  new round starts from the strategy phase.
- Voting: starting with the player to the LEFT of the speaker clockwise, speaker LAST (`agenda_phase.vote_order`). Votes = sum of influence
  of exhausted planets (full planet value); all of a player's votes go to ONE outcome; TGs do not grant votes; zero votes =
  abstain; voting is not a "turn" for game effects.
- Outcomes: most votes; tie or no votes → SPEAKER chooses (not a vote, `decide_tie`, only among tied outcomes).
- A law with a "For" or "Elect" outcome stays in play; directives and "Against" → discard pile.
- Transactions: one per pair of players per agenda — `transacted_with` is reset on each new agenda.
- When counting planet influence, `planet_stats.influence_of` (attachments) is used; exhausting exclusively via `core/exhaust`.
- Active player in the phase: speaker (reveal/outcome/end), during voting — current voter (31.4).

**⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS (to be confirmed):**
1. Outcome type is computed from the card's text `target` field (`classify_target`): For/Against, Elect Player/Planet
   (+ non-home, excluding Mecatol Rex), strategy card, law, scored secret, public objective, tech group, command pool,
   planet trait. Everything else (`manual`) — the operator enters the outcome, the engine tallies votes.
2. An empty agenda deck shuffles the discard pile (like action cards); laws in play do not return to the deck.
3. An agenda with no valid outcomes whose text does NOT say to discard and reveal another — the speaker discards it manually
   (agenda number unchanged). Cards with "…discard this card and reveal another agenda" do it automatically (51.6).
4. Space stations/The Triad/Oceans (Dane ruling) — work only if they are controlled planets with influence.
5. Planets remain exhausted between the two agendas of the same phase (they do not ready before step 3).

### 51.6 Agenda card effects (official deck `agendas_pok`) — ✅ C_agendas / R_agenda_card / ⚠️ ASSUMPTIONS

📎 tirules: [Agendas (rulings)](../../../../rules_tirules/C_agendas.md) · [Action Cards (riders)](../../../../rules_tirules/C_action_cards.md)

Card texts from assets (`assets/data/agendas/base.json`, `pok.json`). Absol (fan variant) — later, separately.

**Model (code: `core/agenda_phase.py`, `core/hooks/agendas_common.py`, `agendas_directives.py`, `agendas_laws.py`):**
- OUTCOME effect = hook `AGENDA_OUTCOME_RESOLVED`/DURING with source `agenda:<id>`; EVERY player has the source when the card is a
  law in play or is currently resolving (`agenda.resolving`, including directives and "Against"). An "each player" effect
  runs on each player starting from the speaker, a one-time effect — once (on the speaker).
- Riders resolve AFTER the agenda effect (AFTER) — C_action_cards. Predictions are public: payload `predictions`
  (who, card, on which outcome), `rider` fact in the Activity feed, chips next to outcomes in the Council window.
- Player choices (which units, planets, technologies, pools) = mandatory decisions / "may" offers (RULES.md 56).
  Voting waits for decisions from agenda cards (`require_no_agenda_decisions`), speaker's "next" and phase end — for all decisions.
- Deferred effects ("at the start of the next strategy phase…") = persistent effects `agendas:<id>:<variant>` per player,
  removed by their own hook (`PHASE`/START strategy).
- A law is removed by ONE function `agenda_phase.remove_law` (`LAW_DISCARDED` WHEN/AFTER) — Political Censure loses its VP.
- Cards with "When this agenda is revealed, if there are no laws/scored secrets, discard this card and reveal another"
  discard themselves automatically (Judicial Abolishment, Miscount Disclosed, New Constitution, Classified Document Leaks).
- Covert Legislation: the speaker draws a hidden agenda (`agenda.covert_card`, visible only to the speaker), players vote on ITS
  outcomes, ITS effect resolves; Veto/discard removes both cards (C_agendas).
- Miscount Disclosed: after resolution (and riders), the speaker's "next" opens a re-vote on the law
  (`revote_law`); the law stays in play meanwhile; "Against" removes it, Classified cannot elect the same target.
- Representative Government: 1 vote without planets and without extra votes (`agenda.single_vote`).
- Public Execution: the elected player does not vote until the end of the phase (`phase_blocked`); the speaker token passes to the player to the left.
- Committee Formation: owner offer after revealing an "Elect Player" agenda (before votes) — outcome without voting.
- Checks and Balances "For": `strategy_phase.pick_strategy_card(recipient=…)`, pick queue counts the player's PICKS
  (`strategy_choices`), the chooser gets the TGs from the card; "Against": in step 3 the player chooses 3 planets to ready.
- Imperial Arbiter: offer after the last strategy card pick (the phase does not advance until the owner decides).
- Galactic Crisis Pact: secondary window of the elected card for all players (from speaker), without a token.
- Ministers: Commerce (after replenishing commodities +1 TG/neighbor), Exploration (+1 TG when gaining control of a planet),
  Industry (after placing a dock — unit production in the system, `ProductionGrant` standalone), Peace (after activating
  a system with foreign units — ends the active player's tactical action), Policy (end of status: +1 action card),
  Sciences (Technology card without resources), War (after an action: token from board to reinforcements + 1 extra action).
- Laws in base rules (`rules_hooks` points): hand limit (Executive Sanctions; not Yssaril), fleet pool limit
  (Fleet Regulations), unlimited PDS (Homeland Defense Act), no bombardment of cultural planets (Conventions of War),
  wormholes have no effect during movement and retreat (Enforced Travel Ban; Nexus Sovereignty — Nexus only; not Creuss; Wormhole
  Reconstruction lifts the ban), alpha↔beta adjacent during movement (Wormhole Reconstruction), moving through nebulae
  (Shared Research), mechs without faction abilities (Articles of War), 1 fighter/infantry per cost (Regulated
  Conscription), +1 to fighter combat rolls and card discard when producing <2 fighters (Prophecy of Ixth), war sun without
  SUSTAIN and without prerequisites (Publicize Weapon Schematics), destroying a ship after researching (Anti-Intellectual
  Revolution), ban on playing action cards (Political Censure), face-up secret objectives (Search Warrant).
