## 11. Player Identity Key in web-data (panel↔map desync) — ✅ VERIFIED (frontend code)

📎 tirules: — (frontend contract, not a TI4 rule; conceptually [Units](../../../../rules_tirules/R_units.md))

Source: `frontend/src/entities/lookup/units.ts` (`lookupUnit` matches units by
`playerData[].faction`) + `tileUnitData`/`statTilePositions` keyed by the same string.

**Rule:** the key grouping units/hexes/control across the ENTIRE web-data document must
be the same as `playerData[].faction` and must UNIQUELY identify the player. When the faction
is unique in the game — we use it; on faction collision or missing faction — we use the
stable `player_id`. Implementation: `web_data._entity_key_map` (single map for
`_entities_from_counts`, `_planet_controller`, `_build_stat_tile_positions`,
`_build_player_data`).

**Why (Bug 1/2):** previously key = `faction or player_id` computed locally in
multiple places. Two players sharing a faction (or `None`) got the same key →
their units merged into a single `tileUnitData` entry and vanished from the map, even though
`playerData` showed both. Editing (`_resolve_player_id`) with condition `faction==key OR
pid==key` hit the wrong player. Now `_resolve_player_id` prefers exact
`player_id`, and accepts faction only when unambiguous.
