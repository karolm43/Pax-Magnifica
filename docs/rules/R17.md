Source (not from memory): AsyncTI4 bot, `PlayerSetupService` ("GHOST AND CRIMSON EXTRA
HS TILES" block). Both factions have a home system physically OFF the board, connected to it via a
wormhole:

- **Ghosts of Creuss**: on the home slot sits the **gate** — tile `17` "Creuss Gate"
  (empty, DELTA wormhole). The real home system is tile `51` "Creuss" (planet
  `creuss`, DELTA), placed OFF the board. Starting fleet and planet control are on `51`.
- **Crimson Rebellion**: the gate is tile `94` "The Sorrow" (empty, EPSILON wormhole);
  the real home is tile `118` (planet `ahkcreuxx`, EPSILON) off the board.

**Off-board position (corner of the play area)** — according to the player's home slot (rule from the bot):
- slots `301`, `304` → `tr` (top-right corner),
- slots `307`, `310` → `br` (bottom-right),
- slots `313`, `316` → `bl` (bottom-left),
- collision (corner occupied) → swap `tr`↔`br`.

Positions `tr/br/bl/tl` are special corner slots of the play area (not rings). The frontend
viewer knows their pixels (`frontend/.../tileCoordinates.ts`, `tilePositioning.ts`), so it
suffices to emit the tile at that position. The gate→home mapping (`17→51`, `94→118`) and
corners come from AsyncTI4 `PlayerSetupService` — explicitly encoded as a rule table
(`faction_setup.OFF_BOARD_HOME`), not guessed.
