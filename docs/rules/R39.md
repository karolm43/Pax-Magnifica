### 39.1. Sides and Participants

- **(W1) Attacker = active player** (`R_attacker`); defender = their opponent in combat.
- **(W2) Space combat**: occurs when two players have ships in the active system (`R_space_combat`). Ships
  (space) with a `combat` value participate; ground forces in space do NOT fight.
- **(W3) Ground combat**: on a planet of the active system when the active player and another player have
  ground forces there (`R_ground_combat`). Ground forces with a `combat` value participate (structures without combat do not).
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: when there is more than one opponent of the active player at the combat site — the engine rejects
  (422) and it is resolved via God-Mode (edge case, no unambiguous rule in the dump).

### 39.2. Round Sequence

- **(W4) AFB** — 1st round of SPACE combat only: both players roll simultaneously for units with
  `Anti–Fighter Barrage` (hit = result ≥ `afbHitsOn`, dice = `afbDieCount`). Each destroys their own
  FIGHTERS for opponent's hits; excess is lost; Sustain Damage does NOT cancel AFB hits.
  ✅ User decision 2026-11-23: AFB hits (min(hits, fighters)) are assigned by the fighters' owner
  in the ASSIGN step (`afb_assign`, required target `hit_targets` = fighters, selection + "Auto") —
  before assignment they can be canceled (Shields Holding — C_action_cards 4.1, Tellurian — 62.18).
  Raid Formation and Strike Wing Alpha II count hits from the roll (before cancellation).
  This step occurs even without fighters. If after AFB either side has no ships — combat ends.
- **(W5) Rolls** — each unit rolls `combatDieCount` dice (missing = 1); hit = result ≥ combat
  value. Attacker rolls first, then defender. "0" on d10 = 10.
- **(W6) Nebula** — in SPACE combat in a nebula, the defender adds +1 to every combat roll of their
  ships (does not apply to AFB, space cannon, bombardment, or ground combat) — `R_nebula`.
- **(W7) Assign hits** — each player destroys THEIR units in combat for each opponent's hit.
  Order in the `COMBAT_ASSIGN_HITS` timing window (RULES.md 29.2): defender, then attacker.
  Number of hits to cover = min(hits, units + undamaged units with Sustain Damage).
- **(W8) Sustain Damage** — before assigning hits, a player may use Sustain Damage on any of their
  UNDAMAGED units with that ability: each cancels one hit and becomes DAMAGED.
  A damaged unit operates normally, but cannot use Sustain Damage again until repaired.
- **(W9) End of combat** — when after a round at most one player has units in combat. Winner = whoever
  has units remaining; neither has units = draw. Otherwise next round (without AFB).
- **(W10) Repair** — in the status phase (step 7 "Repair Units") all damaged units are
  repaired (`R_status_phase`). The engine clears damage state upon entering STATUS.

### 39.3. Model and Implementation

- `SystemState.damaged_units` = `{zone: {player_id: {base_type: count}}}` (zone `"space"` or planet
  id). Damaged count ≤ unit count (clamped on read). Web-data exposes
  `EntityData.sustained` (AsyncTI4 frontend draws the damage marker).
- `GameState.active_combat` (`ActiveCombat`: kind, location, attacker/defender, round, step
  `AFB/ROLL/ASSIGN/DONE`, hits, round rolls, log, winner).
- `core/battle.py` — `start_combat`/`resolve_afb`/`roll_round`/`assign_combat_hits`/`close_combat`.
  Rolls via `core/dice.py` (seedable, stored in state).
- The SPACE_COMBAT step cannot be left while two players have ships in the active system or
  combat is in progress (422). Ending the tactical action clears combat (safety valve).
- Movement (`move_fleet`) carries damage along with ships: undamaged ships move first
  (⚠️ ASSUMPTION — selection of specific individual units is not modeled).

### 39.5. Retreats (Announce Retreats / Retreat) — ✅ VERIFIED (LRR 2.0)

📎 tirules: [Space Combat](../../../../rules_tirules/R_space_combat.md) · [Ground Combat](../../../../rules_tirules/R_ground_combat.md) · [Movement](../../../../rules_tirules/R_movement.md) · [Nebula](../../../../rules_tirules/R_nebula.md) · [Gravity Rift](../../../../rules_tirules/R_gravity_rift.md) · [Neutral Units](../../../../rules_tirules/R_neutral_units.md) · [Command Tokens](../../../../rules_tirules/R_command_tokens.md)

Step order of a SPACE combat round: AFB (1st round only) → **Announce Retreats** → Roll → Assign
Hits → **Retreat** → (next round from Announce Retreats).

- **(O1) Space combat only.** There is no retreat from ground combat (`R_ground_combat`). Neutral
  units do not retreat (`R_neutral_units`).
- **(O2) Announcement** — each player MAY announce a retreat, starting with the defender. Retreat does not happen
  immediately, only in the Retreat step. If the defender announces a retreat, the attacker CANNOT announce one that round.
  A player cannot announce a retreat without at least one eligible system (O4).
  Model: timing window `COMBAT_RETREAT` (RULES.md 29.2), queue = [defender, attacker] filtered
  to players with an eligible target; when nobody has a target — the step is skipped.
- **(O3) Execution** — in the Retreat step, a player who announced a retreat MUST retreat, provided they still have
  an eligible system. If the opponent has no ships remaining — combat ends immediately, retreat does not
  occur. If no eligible system remains — retreat does not occur, combat continues.
- **(O4) Eligible system** — ADJACENT to the active system; contains the player's units or a planet they control
  (or both); does NOT contain another player's ships. Anomalies: nebula ALLOWED (`R_nebula` notes/3, user
  decision 2026-11-24 — RULES.md 1.2), not into a supernova, into an asteroid field only with Antimass Deflectors. Gravity rift is allowed.
- **(O5) Movement** — ALL of the player's ships with a move value (player variant: `moveValue`) retreat
  together to a SINGLE system. Move value itself does not matter (adjacent only). Ships may transport
  fighters and ground forces from the active system's space area (up to `capacityValue`) as well as ground forces
  from planets the player controls in the active system (`R_space_combat` Notes). A player CANNOT leave
  fighters/ground forces in space behind if they have available capacity. Fighters and ground forces in space
  that cannot be transported are REMOVED. Transport from the active system is allowed (`R_movement`).
- **(O6) Command token** — when any units actually retreated, the player places a token from
  REINFORCEMENTS (`cc_reinf`) in the destination system; if they already have a token there — no second token is placed. Empty reinforcements →
  token from command sheet (pool choice). Only Fighter I (no move value) → removed, no token placed.
- **(O7) After Retreat step** — if both players still have ships in the active system, next round
  from Announce Retreats; otherwise combat ends (winner = player with ships).
- **(O7a) "After a round of combat" = after the Retreat step** (R_space_combat: Retreat is step 5 of a round; F_yin, F_norr:
  "If a player retreats, the Yin/N'orr player will be unable to use Devotion / Exotrireme II"). End-of-round
  events (`COMBAT_ROUND/END`: Devotion, Exotrireme II, Duranium Armor) fire ONCE — after retreat,
  when retreat occurs; round-loss decisions (e.g., Brother Milor) are resolved BEFORE retreat.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: when reinforcements are empty and the player did not specify a pool, the engine takes a token
  in order from tactic, fleet, strategy pool (the rule lets the player choose — UI may supply the pool explicitly).
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: default cargo (when the player does not specify it) = mechs first, then infantry, then fighters
  from space, up to capacity; without ground forces from planets. Explicit choice is validated against
  rule O5.
- ⚠️ TODO: gravity rift rolls when retreating out of a gravity rift (`R_gravity_rift`), faction/technology exceptions
  (Magmus Reactor — supernova, Empyrean — nebula, Dark Energy Tap, Skilled Retreat) — E7 hooks.

### 39.6. Capacity Limit in System Space — ✅ VERIFIED (LRR 2.0)

📎 tirules: [Capacity](../../../../rules_tirules/R_capacity.md) · [Space Combat](../../../../rules_tirules/R_space_combat.md) · [Space Dock](../../../../rules_tirules/R_space_dock.md)

- **(C1) What counts** — total `capacityValue` of a player's ships in a system determines how many fighters and ground
  forces (infantry, mech) the player may have in the SPACE area of that system. Ground forces on planets do
  not count. Each cargo unit uses `capacityUsed` from its variant (missing = 1).
- **(C2) Excess** — when cargo in space exceeds capacity, the player MUST remove the excess
  (returned to reinforcements) and CHOOSES which units themselves. They remove only as many as needed.
- **(C3) When** — during combat, the limit does not apply. It is checked after determining the winner of
  space combat (you can win with fighters alone, then remove them) and before Space Cannon Offense / space
  combat if a carrier was destroyed or movement delivered cargo (`R_capacity` Notes).
  Engine: `capacity.combat_in_progress` — during combat in the system `players_with_excess` is empty (also for
  `limits.enforce_limits`), and excess Fighter II do not count toward fleet pool (RULES.md 57.8).
  **Space Cannon Offense** (F_xxcha, Indomitus: "their ships are removed by another player's space cannon roll, they must
  immediately remove units to meet their capacity limit. They will not resolve the Space Cannon abilities of any of their
  removed mechs"): after assigning hits (`space_cannon.assign_hits`), a target with excess in the active system immediately gets
  a `CAPACITY` window (`timing.interrupt` suspends the Space Cannon window); removed mechs do not fire.
- **(C4) Space Dock** — dock data (`assets/.../units`: `capacityValue` + ability "Up to N fighters in this
  system do not count against your ships' capacity") → a player's dock on a planet in the system provides capacity
  ONLY for fighters, equal to the variant's `capacityValue`. A dock-type unit in space (Saar,
  "Floating Factory") provides normal ship capacity.
- **(C5) Forced choice** — when there is EXACTLY one legal removal per C2 (e.g., only fighters
  left without a ship with capacity), the engine removes the excess immediately without a window (`capacity.forced_removal`;
  user decision 2026-11-18). Movement that leaves cargo without capacity is legal
  (`R_capacity` Notes: "may pick up any units… even if doing so would cause units to be removed") —
  including effect-based movement (Helix Protocol, Wing Transfer).
- Model: timing window `CAPACITY` (RULES.md 29.2), queue = players with excess in the active system, clockwise
  from the active player. While active, cannot advance to the next action step.
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: Mahact/Cabal dock (Dimensional Tear) has "6 fighters" in its description in data, but NO
  `capacityValue` — the engine counts 0 then (data is authoritative; to be supplemented via E7 faction hook).
- ⚠️ ASSUMPTION: the "Auto" button in UI removes the cheapest units first (`cost` from data). This is only
  a suggestion — the player's choice is validated against rule C2.
- ⚠️ TODO: limit on production (fighters/infantry in space) and after Commit Ground Forces does not yet open
  a window by itself — on the next step transition the engine will still detect excess.
