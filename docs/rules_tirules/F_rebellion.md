## Sundered

- `Sundered` only prevents the Rebellion player’s ships from moving through wormholes other than epsilon wormholes. Other wormholes still make systems adjacent for the Rebellion player for all other purposes, such as `Space Cannon`, neighbors, objectives, action cards, and the ability of the Exile.
- The Rebellion player may use the [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player’s [*Creuss IFF*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Creuss%20IFF) promissory note, but their ships still cannot move through Creuss wormholes.
- The Rebellion player may use the Creuss player’s agent, [Emissary Taivra](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Leaders:~:text=Emissary%20Taivra), and the *Lost Star Chart* action card.
- The *Wormhole Reconstruction* agenda does not override `Sundered`. However, the systems it makes adjacent are adjacent for movement, and the Rebellion player’s ships do not need to use the wormholes to move between them.
- `Sundered` is a state–based effect, and resolves before another player would gain control of Ahk Creuxx, even if that player is the active player.

## Incursion

- Breaches are not wormholes.*
- If a player has a ship in a system containing multiple breaches at the end of the status phase, they may remove any or all of them.
- If the Rebellion player has no more breach tokens in their reinforcements, and an ability instructs them to place a breach, they may remove an inactive breach from the game board and place that.
    - Active breaches cannot be removed in this manner.
- If the Rebellion player is eliminated, breach tokens remain in play, and active breaches remain adjacent to each other. Inactive breaches may be removed from the game board.

## The Sorrow

- If the Rebellion player is eliminated, their home system still destroys units that are placed or moved into it, and their faction sheet remains in play.

## Sever (Promissory Note)

- *Sever* only prevents movement through the wormhole. Adjacency, `Space Cannon`, the [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player’s [`Quantum Entanglement`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Quantum%20Entanglement) and [`Slipstream`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Slipstream) faction abilities, the *Ghost Ship* action card and the *Become the Gatekeeper* objective are unaffected.
- *Sever* affects all wormholes in the system, not just the one the token is placed on.
- If the Rebellion player is eliminated, the *Sever* token and promissory note remain in play until the note expires at the end of the status phase, when they are removed from the game.

## Subatomic Splicer (Technology)

- The Rebellion player cannot use *Subatomic Splicer* to produce ships while another player’s ships are in their home system.
- The produced ships must still be paid for.

## Exile (Unit)

- The destroyer must be on the game board when the combat ends in order to place the breach. If the destroyer was in the active system, and was destroyed in the space combat, it cannot be used to place a breach.
- A breach may be placed after a space combat and/or a ground combat.
- The Rebellion player may place a breach with the ability of an Exile at the end of a combat against neutral units.

## Quietus (Flagship)

- The *Quietus* removes only the unit abilities from other players’ units; any text abilities are unaffected.*
    - Text abilities that rely on unit abilities, such as the space dock II text ability that defines “X” in that unit’s `Production X` ability, will have no effect.*
    - If a unit loses its `Deploy` ability, then the text that describes how that unit’s `Deploy` ability is used will have no effect.*

## Ahk Ravin (Agent)

- Ahk Ravin is a swap, not a move. This means units will not trigger gravity rift rolls and the ability cannot be used to swap with a [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player’s space dock.
- If the Rebellion player transports ground forces during the swap, those ground forces will be placed in the space area of their destination system. They cannot be committed to planets in that system until and unless a tactical action is resolved in that system.
- Either of the swapped ships may be a fighter.*

## Ahk Siever (Commander)

- Ahk Siever triggers off of combats with neutral units.
- If Ahk Siever is unlocked by placing a breach token into a system containing another player’s unit at the end of a combat with the ability of an Exile, the Rebellion player will resolve the commodity ability of Ahk Siever from that same combat.
- The Rebellion player does not have to be a participant in the combat to gain or convert commodities.*
- If there are multiple combats in one turn, the Rebellion player may gain or convert a commodity for each. This includes for each ground combat on a separate planet.*

## Homesick Phantom — Fragment Reality (Hero)

- The Rebellion player may only start placing ships on Homesick Phantom once the hero has been unlocked.*
- *Fragment Reality* cannot be used during a combat between two other players.
- Ships placed by *Fragment Reality* count against the Rebellion player’s fleet pool immediately, but capacity is not checked until the end of the combat.

## Revenant (Mech)

- The Rebellion player may only `Deploy` one mech per tactical action, regardless of the number of planets in the active system.*

## Resonance Generator (Breakthrough)

- The passive, movement ability of *Resonance Generator* is still in effect when the card is exhausted.
