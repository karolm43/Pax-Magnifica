## Fragile

- The `Fragile` ability does not affect anti–fighter barrage, bombardment or space cannon rolls.
- The effect of the `Fragile` ability is mandatory.*

## Brilliant

- When using Brilliant, the Jol–Nar player spends only the command token from their strategy pool, not the four resources. They then resolve the primary ability of the *Technology* strategy card, and may research an additional technology by spending six resources.
- When the Jol–Nar player uses `Brilliant`, they may research a second technology by spending six resources, without spending an additional command token.

## Analytical

## Research Agreement (Promissory Note)

- If the Jol–Nar player will research several technologies in a row, then after each, the player holding *Research Agreement* may play it. The Jol–Nar player does not decide the next technology they will research until after the player holding the *Research Agreement* plays it or declines to.*
- If a player receives *Research Agreement* in a transaction immediately after the Jol–Nar player researched a technology, that player may play it immediately to gain that technology.
    - Once a player has used *Research Agreement* after the Jol–Nar player researches a technology, no other player may use *Research Agreement* for that same Jol–Nar research instance to gain the same technology.
    - If the active player received the *Research Agreement* on a previous turn, they may play it and immediately perform a transaction with the Jol–Nar player to regain it. They may then play it when the Jol–Nar player researches their next technology.*
- Any deal between the Jol–Nar player and another player regarding when *Research Agreement* will be played, if made before *Research Agreement* is exchanged, is non–binding.
- If a faction has a faction–specific unit upgrade as one of their faction technologies, they cannot gain that unit upgrade from *Research Agreement* when the Jol–Nar player researches the generic version of that unit upgrade.*
- *Research Agreement* may be triggered when the Jol–Nar player researches a technology outside of the *Technology* strategy card. For example, it may be triggered by the *Divert Funding* action card, the *Focused Research* action card, the *Reveal Prototype* action card, the *Technology Rider* action card, the *Ixthian Artifact* agenda, the *Wormhole Research* agenda, or the *Enigmatic Device* exploration card.
    - *Research Agreement* cannot be triggered by a game effect that instructs the Jol–Nar player to directly gain a technology, without researching. For example, it cannot be triggered by the [*Fires of the Gashlai*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Embers_of_Muaat#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Fires%20of%20the%20Gashlai) [Muaat](https://www.tirules2.com/F_muaat) faction promissory note, the *Plagiarize* action card, the *Research Grant Reallocation* agenda, or the *Maw of Worlds* relic.
    - *Research Agreement* cannot be triggered when the Jol–Nar player resolves [*Genetic Memory*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Universities_of_Jol-Nar#Leaders:~:text=Genetic%20Memory).*

## E–Res Siphons (Technology)

- *E–Res Siphons* ability is triggered when the system is activated, even if the active player will perform no hostile acts toward the Jol–Nar player this turn.*
- If an effect other than activating a system during a tactical action places a command token in a system with the Jol–Nar player’s ships (for example, the primary ability of the *Diplomacy* strategy card), this will not cause *E–Res Siphons* ability to trigger.*

## Spatial Conduit Cylinder (Technology)

- *Spatial Conduit Cylinder* is exhausted after the system is activated, before movement; the active system must already contain one of the Jol–Nar player’s units.
- The Jol–Nar player’s PDS II units in systems made adjacent by *Spatial Conduit Cylinder* may use their `Space Cannon` ability against the active system.
- Other players may use the `Deep Space Cannon` ability of units in the same system as a Jol–Nar player’s unit to produce hits during the **Space Cannon Offense** step.*

## J.N.S. Hylarim (Flagship)

- Each result of 9 or 10 produces three hits in total: its normal hit and two additional hits.

## Doctor Sucaban (Agent)

- If the Jol–Nar player has the Infantry II unit upgrade, any of their infantry removed with Doctor Sucaban will not roll for resurrection.

## Ta Zern / / Agnlan Oln (Commander)

- Ta Zern and Agnlan Oln work identically. Thunder’s Edge replaces Ta Zern with Agnlan Oln because the [Deepwrought](https://www.tirules2.com/F_deepwrought) player’s hero is also named Ta Zern.
- The unit abilities that roll dice, and whose dice may be rerolled with this ability, are `Anti–Fighter Barrage`, `Bombardment` and `Space Cannon`.
- “Combat” is a unit attribute, not a unit ability; this commander cannot reroll combat dice.
- This commander cannot be used to reroll dice for an Infantry II resurrection ability.*

## Rin, The Master’s Legacy — Genetic Memory (Hero)

- The Jol–Nar player does not have to meet the prerequisites of the technologies they acquire.*
- All replacements happen simultaneously. If this ability returns a specific technology, it cannot then be regained via this ability.
- If the Jol–Nar player later gains a technology they returned, they gain it readied.*
- If the Jol–Nar player has the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player’s *Alliance* promissory note in their play area, they will not draw any action cards from the ability of the [Nekro Acidos](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Leaders:~:text=Nekro%20Acidos) commander when they resolve *Genetic Memory*.*
- If this ability is used to replace a faction technology which is currently being copied by the Nekro player’s [*Valefar Assimilator*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Valefar%20Assimilator) technology, the Valefar Assimilator token stays on that technology while it is in the technology deck, and *Valefar Assimilator* still has that technology’s text.

## Shield Paling (Mech)

- The Shield Paling itself is still affected by the [`Fragile`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Universities_of_Jol-Nar#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Fragile) ability.*

## Specialized Compounds (Breakthrough)

- Due to the synergy of *Specialized Compounds*, the Jol–Nar player may exhaust a planet with a yellow technology specialty to research a green technology, and vice versa.
- A technology with zero or completely ignored prerequisites must still match the color of the planet’s technology specialty to use *Specialized Compounds*.*
- The Jol–Nar player cannot use *Specialized Compounds* when researching a unit upgrade.
    - Unit upgrades do not have a color.
- The *Psychoarchaeology* technology cannot be used to avoid exhausting the planet with the technology specialty.
- The Jol–Nar player cannot use a technology specialty planet to both pay for the research with *Specialized Compounds* and to ignore a prerequisite of the researched technology simultaneously.*
