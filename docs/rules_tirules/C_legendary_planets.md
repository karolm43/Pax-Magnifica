## The Acropolis — Emelpar

- The owner of *The Acropolis* may ready a component in another player’s play area.*

## Aeurex Mechanica — Industrex

## The Atrament — Primor

- Both infantry must be placed on the same planet.

## Dok ’N Pic’s Salvage Yard — Garbozia

- The player chooses which discarded action card to take.
- *Dok ’N Pic’s Salvage Yard* need not be readied for a player to play the action cards on it.*
- Cards on *Dok ’N Pic’s Salvage Yard* do not count toward a player’s action card hand limit.*
- The player with *The Codex* cannot take cards that are on *Dok ’N Pic’s Salvage Yard*.*
- If another player gains control of Garbozia, any action cards on *Dok ’N Pic’s Salvage Yard* will remain there when the legendary planet ability card is transferred; the new controller of Garbozia may play those action cards in the future.*

## Exterrix Headquarters — Mallice

## The Galactic Council — Mecatol Rex

- When using *The Galactic Council*, the active player first discards a secret objective, shuffling it into the secret objective deck. Then, they draw a secret objective. This may result in them drawing the secret objective they discarded.*
- *The Galactic Council* cannot be exhausted by a player that has no unscored secret objective cards in their hand.*
- A player cannot discard a scored secret objective to resolve this ability; only secret objectives in their hand may be discarded.

## Imperial Arms Vault — Hope’s End

- If a player wishes to resolve the *Imperial Arms Vault* ability of Hope’s End, but they have no mechs left in their reinforcements, they may remove a mech from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and place that instead. The mech will be placed undamaged.

## Jupiter Brain — Thunder’s Edge
