## Terragenesis

- A planet cannot contain more than one sleeper token.
- The Ul player may only move a sleeper token with `Terragenesis` once all of their sleeper tokens are on the game board.

## Awaken

- Only sleeper tokens present in the system when it is activated may be replaced with PDS.
- A sleeper token that is placed during the **Activation** step, most commonly by using the *Scanlink Drone Network* technology and the [`Terragenesis`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Terragenesis) ability, cannot be replaced via the `Awaken` ability during the same tactical action.
- The Ul player may place a PDS on a planet that contains two of another player’s PDS. They cannot place a PDS on a planet that already contains two of their own PDS.
- If the Ul player places a PDS on a planet controlled by another player, and that player has ground forces on that planet, then a ground combat will be resolved during the **Invasion** step of the tactical action.*
- If the Ul player activates a system containing their sleeper tokens and the [Xxcha](https://www.tirules2.com/F_xxcha) player’s ships, the Ul player, as the active player, may resolve `Awaken` before the Xxcha player resolves the [*Nullification Field*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Xxcha_Kingdom#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Nullification%20Field) technology. Any resulting ground combat still occurs.
- A sleeper token is not removed when another player gains control of the planet it is on. The Ul player may still replace it using `Awaken`.
- If the Ul player retreats into a system containing their sleeper token, `Awaken` is not triggered, as the system is not activated.
- If another player plays the Ul player’s *Ceasefire* promissory note, the Ul player may still place units into the active system using their `Awaken` ability.
- If the Ul player wishes to place a PDS, but there are none left in their reinforcements, they may remove a PDS from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and place that instead. The PDS will be placed undamaged.

## Coalescence

- Units placed on planets via the [`Awaken`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Awaken) ability are not committed. They cannot be assigned hits during the **Space Cannon Defense** step.*
- If the Ul player’s units exist in the same location as another player’s units, effects that end the Ul player’s turn cannot prevent the combat from occurring.
- Only the active player may use the `Bombardment` ability of their units during an invasion.
    - This prevents the [L1Z1X](https://www.tirules2.com/F_lizix) player from using their [`Harrow`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_L1Z1X_Mindnet#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Harrow) ability.
- If the Ul player’s turn is ended by another game effect, the combat still occurs. No other steps of the tactical action occur, and abilities may only be used within that combat.

## Terraform (Promissory Note)

- Once played, *Terraform* is never returned to the Ul player.
- Once played, *Terraform* counts as being in the play area of the player who controls the planet.
    - If the Ul player gains control of a planet with *Terraform* attached, their own *Terraform* will be in their play area.
- If a planet has multiple traits, the player exploring the planet chooses which of the corresponding exploration decks to draw from.
- If *Terraform* is attached to a planet in The Fracture, that planet may be explored.
- *Terraform* may be attached to [Thunder’s Edge](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Thunder's_Edge).

## Hel–Titan (Unit)

- When multiple units use their `Production` ability, the `Production` value is pooled. As such, if the Ul player has two Hel–Titans in a system, they may produce two infantry or two fighters for one resource, rather than two resources.
- Effects that remove ground forces, and then place them on the game board without transporting them, such as the *Decoy Operation* action card or the *Transit Diodes* technology, may be used on a Hel–Titan.
- Effects that move ground forces, such as the *Ghost Squad* action card or the ability of the [N’orr](https://www.tirules2.com/F_norr) player’s commander, [G’hom Sek’kus](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Sardakk_N'orr#:~:text=G%27hom%20Sek%27kus), (via the *Alliance* promissory note), cannot be used to move a Hel–Titan to another planet.
- A Hel–Titan is a structure, and so cannot be targeted by the [Vuil’raith](https://www.tirules2.com/F_vuilraith) player’s [*Vortex*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Vuil'Raith_Cabal#:~:text=Vortex) faction technology.
- If a player is able to ignore `Planetary Shield`, then Hel–Titan are eligible units to assign any hits produced by a bombardment roll.*

## Ouranos (Flagship)

- Only PDS present in the system when it is activated may be replaced with the *Ouranos*.
- A PDS that is placed during the activation step, most commonly by using the [`Awaken`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Awaken) ability, cannot be replaced with the *Ouranos* during the same tactical action.

## Tellurian (Agent)

- The timing of Tellurian is changed to “before you assign a hit”, the same as the *Shields Holding* action card and the `Sustain Damage` ability. As such, if Tellurian cancels a hit produced by a player who owns the [N’orr](https://www.tirules2.com/F_norr) player’s [*Valkyrie Particle Weave*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Sardakk_N'orr#:~:text=Valkyrie%20Particle%20Weave) technology, *Valkyrie Particle Weave* still triggers before the hit is canceled.

## Tungstantus (Commander)

- The trade good may be spent on the units produced by the `Production` ability that triggered Tungstantus.
    - The trade good may be spent as it is gained. The number of trade goods the Ul player has after producing units is used to determine if the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player may use their [`Pillage`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Pillage) ability.
    - A trade good will not be gained unless at least one unit is produced.*
- Other effects that produce units without a unit using its `Production` ability will not trigger Tungstantus.

## Ul the Progenitor — Geoform (Hero)

- The `Space Cannon` ability is controlled by whichever player controls Elysium.*
- Elysium counts as a planet with an attachment for objectives such as *Reclaim Ancient Monuments*.*

## Hecatoncheires (Mech)

- When the Ul player would place multiple PDS during a single step, they may resolve a `Deploy` ability to place one Hecatoncheires for any or all of those PDS.*
- The Ul player must have at least one PDS in their reinforcements to place in order to use the `Deploy` ability of a Hecatoncheires. However, when they would place a PDS, they may remove a PDS from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and put it in their reinforcements. They may then use the `Deploy` ability of a Hecatoncheires.
- The Ul player cannot place a PDS on a planet that already has two of their PDS on it. As such, they are unable to place a Hecatoncheires on a planet with two of their PDS using its `Deploy` ability.
- A `Deploy` ability may only be resolved for units in a player’s reinforcements. If all four of the Hecatoncheires are already on the board, no Hecatoncheires may be deployed.
- The Ul player may use the `Deploy` ability of a Hecatoncheires to place units into coexistence, most often through the use of [*Slumberstate Computing*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Breakthrough:~:text=Slumberstate%20Computing).
- The Ul player may use the `Deploy` ability of a Hecatoncheires when placing a PDS with the *Construction* strategy card, including for each PDS placed with the primary ability.

## Slumberstate Computing (Breakthrough)

- A planet controlled by another player may contain at most one sleeper token.
- The Ul player does not have to be neighbors with the player that agrees to have a sleeper token placed on their planet.
- There is no timing window or other requirement for another player allowing the Ul player to place a sleeper token on a planet they control; it may be included as part of a transaction or deal.
    - As such, allowing the Ul player to place a sleeper token can be part of a binding deal.
- When another player agrees to allow the Ul player to place a sleeper token, the agreement includes which planet the token will be placed on.
- The additional action cards are only drawn during the **Draw Action Cards** step of the status phase. Other effects that draw action cards during the status phase, such as the *Minister of Policy* agenda or the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player’s agent, [Suffi An](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Leaders:~:text=Suffi%20An), do not draw additional action cards.
