## Star Forge

- The Muaat player cannot use `Star Forge` if they do not have a war sun on the game board.*
- Ships placed with `Star Forge` are not produced, and do not trigger effects that occur when units use `Production`, such as the [*Magmus Reactor*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Embers_of_Muaat#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Magmus%20Reactor) technology.

## Fires of the Gashlai (Promissory Note)

- If a player already owns their war sun unit upgrade technology, they cannot play *Fires of the Gashlai*.
- If the Muaat player has no command tokens in their fleet pool, *Fires of the Gashlai* cannot be played.

- This card is replaced by the Ω version.
- The war sun must be in the system prior to the use of `Production` to gain the trade good.*
- The trade good is gained after the use of `Production`; it cannot be used to pay for the produced units.*

## Magmus Reactor Ω (Technology)

- If the Muaat player has a unit in a system containing a supernova, they cannot be eliminated.*

## Umbat (Agent)

- The produced units must be paid for.
- No unit is using its `Production` ability. The *Sarween Tools* technology and similar effects will not allow a more expensive ship to be produced.
- If ground forces are produced, they may be placed in the space area or on a planet the player controls in the system with their war sun or flagship.*
- If the [Naalu](https://www.tirules2.com/F_naalu) or [Yin](https://www.tirules2.com/F_yin) players are chosen, and choose to produce fighters or infantry, they will only be able to produce two of those units; their commander will have no effect.*
    - If the *Regulated Conscription* law is in play, then that player may use their commander to produce two units for one resource when resolving Umbat.*

## Magmus (Commander)

- When a command token is spent to resolve the secondary ability of a strategy card, the trade good is gained after resolving the secondary ability. As such, it cannot be spent to pay for costs of the secondary ability.*
- If the Muaat player spends multiple tokens from their strategy pool (e.g. for the *Lead From the Front* objective), they gain that many trade goods, one at a time.*
    - Each trade good gained may trigger the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player’s [`Pillage`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Pillage) faction ability.
    - If the Muaat player wishes to score *Lead From the Front* and *Form a Spy Network* during the one status phase, they must do so simultaneously, and both happen before the Mentak player may use their `Pillage` faction ability. If the Mentak player subsequently uses their agent, [Suffi An](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Leaders:~:text=Suffi%20An), when they use their `Pillage` faction ability on the trade goods the Muaat player gained from Magmus when spending the tokens for *Lead From the Front*, then the Muaat player cannot discard the drawn action card for *Form a Spy Network*.*
- If the Muaat player uses the ability of the *Scepter of Emelpar* relic, they will be unable to benefit from the ability of Magmus.*
- The Muaat player must have Magmus unlocked before spending the strategy token in order to gain the trade good.*
    - For example, if the Muaat player spends a strategy token to follow the secondary ability of the *Warfare* strategy card, and in doing so produces the war sun that unlocks Magmus, they will not gain the trade good from spending that strategy token.*

## Adjudicator Ba’al — Nova Seed (Hero)

- Any method of movement is sufficient to trigger this ability, not only tactical action movement. For example, the Muaat player may trigger *Nova Seed* when they retreat.*
    - If the [Mahact](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mahact) player moves the Muaat player’s war sun via the use of their [*Benediction*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mahact_Gene-Sorcerers#Leaders:~:text=Benediction) hero ability, the Muaat player cannot trigger *Nova Seed*.*
- If the war sun is in the target system when it is activated, it must move out of the system, and back in, for *Nova Seed* to be able to be triggered.*
- *Nova Seed* purges all tokens on the replaced system tile other than command tokens and frontier tokens. Any faction–specific tokens, such as [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) wormhole tokens or [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) sleeper tokens, are returned to the player that owns them instead.
    - The Avernus token is purged, not returned to the Muaat player. This also purges the Avernus planet card, *The Nucleus* legendary planet ability card, and any attachments on Avernus.
- The Muaat player’s units may exist in and move out of the supernova system, even if the Muaat player does not own [*Magmus Reactor*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Embers_of_Muaat#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Magmus%20Reactor).
- If the Muaat player owns *Magmus Reactor Ω*, and resolves this ability during a tactical action, they will be able to produce units during the **Production** step, if the Muaat player still has units in the system.*
- *Nova Seed* cannot target the home system of an eliminated player.
- *Nova Seed* may target the [Creuss Gate](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Creuss%20Gate) (tile 17), the [Rebellion](https://www.tirules2.com/F_rebellion)’s [Sorrow](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crimson_Rebellion#Faction_Abilities:~:text=The%20Sorrow) (tile 94), or the Wormhole Nexus (tile 82).*
- The [Empyrean](https://www.tirules2.com/F_empyrean) player cannot use the ability of their agent, Acamar, on the Muaat player after they use *Nova Seed* if the original system tile contained planets.*
- *Nova Seed* cannot replace a system tile in the Fracture, such as the system containing Styx.
- If the Muaat player moves a war sun into a system containing the [Naalu](https://www.tirules2.com/F_naalu) player’s ships, the Muaat player, as the active player, may resolve *Nova Seed* before the Naalu player resolves their [`Foresight`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Naalu_Collective#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Foresight) faction ability.

## Ember Colossus (Mech)

- If multiple Ember Colossi are in or adjacent to the system, each places one infantry.

## Stellar Genesis (Breakthrough)

- The Muaat player explores Avernus when they gain it.*
- The Muaat player may move Avernus even if another player controls it.
- Avernus may be moved into a system containing another player’s units.*
- Structures may be placed on Avernus.*
    - Any PDS (or other relevant unit) on Avernus may use its `Space Cannon` ability during the movement step of the same tactical action the planet is moved.*
    - Any of the Muaat player’s space docks (or other relevant units) on Avernus may use its `Production` ability during the production step of the same tactical action the planet is moved.*
- If the Muaat player moves Avernus into a supernova while another player has units on it, those units are unaffected.
- Avernus is not a ship. It does not roll for removal when it moves out of or through a gravity rift.
- Avernus may be moved out of a system that contains the Muaat player’s command token during a tactical action, even if there are ground forces on it, as it is not a ship.

## Avernus — The Nucleus (Legendary Planet)

- If the Muaat player uses *The Nucleus*, they will not gain a trade good from the ability of their commander, Magmus.*
- If the Muaat player uses *The Nucleus*, they will still place infantry with their adjacent mechs.*
- If another player controls Avernus, they may use *The Nucleus* only if they have a war sun on the game board.*
- If the Muaat player is eliminated, Avernus, its token, its planet card, and its legendary planet ability card remain in play.
