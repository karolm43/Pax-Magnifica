## Notes

- Certain abilities, primarily breakthroughs, grant synergy between two colors of technology, allowing components of one color to be treated as the other for some game effects.
- When researching a technology, a player may treat each technology they own and/or each technology specialty on a planet they control that matches one color of their synergy as any color of that synergy.
- When scoring an objective, a player may treat each technology they own that matches one color of their synergy as any color of that synergy.
- These choices are made individually for each technology and/or each technology specialty.
- Each technology and/or each technology specialty can only be one color of technology at any given point in time.
- A technology and/or each technology specialty may be treated as different colors of technologies at different points in time.
    - For example, say a player currently has just one biotic technology, and they are resolving the primary ability of the *Technology* strategy card to research two technologies; they have a propulsion/biotic synergy ability. They may first treat the biotic technology as a propulsion technology in order to research a technology with one propulsion prerequisite. Then, they may treat both the original biotic technology and the new propulsion technology as biotic technologies in order to research a technology with two biotic prerequisites.*
