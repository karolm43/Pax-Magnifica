## Research Team

- `Research Team` may be used both when the Deepwrought player commits ground forces to a planet another player controls and when another player commits ground forces to a planet the Deepwrought player controls.
- If the Deepwrought player uses `Research Team` when another player commits ground forces to a planet they control, the invading player cannot engage in ground combat with the Deepwrought player on that planet during the same activation in which the Deepwrought player enters coexistence.
- If the Deepwrought player uses `Research Team` when another player commits ground forces to a planet the Deepwrought player controls, the ground combat is skipped and the invading player gains control of the planet during the **Establish Control** step. If another player was already coexisting on that planet, the invading player may instead choose to end that player’s coexistence, resolving a ground combat against them; the winner gains control during the **Establish Control** step.

## Oceanbound

- Ocean cards are spent as though they were planet cards. However, oceans are not planets, and do not count as planets for the purpose of scoring objectives, such as *Push Boundaries*.
    - When the Deepwrought player scores objectives, planets they are coexisting on do count as planets they control. This is a function of coexistence, not of oceans, and other players do not count those planets as controlled by the Deepwrought player.
- If all of the Deepwrought player’s ocean cards are already in play, they cannot resolve `Oceanbound` to ready one of their oceans.
- Ocean cards are readied at the end of the agenda phase, like planets.
- Attachments such as the *Nano–Forge* relic and the [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) player’s [*Terraform*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Terraform) promissory note cannot be attached to an ocean card.

## Share Knowledge (Promissory Note)

- If a player gains the *Antimass Deflectors* technology through *Share Knowledge* and later loses it, their units may remain in and move out of any asteroid fields they are currently in, but cannot move additional units into or through.
- If the Deepwrought player is eliminated, *Share Knowledge* and its technology remain in play until the status phase, when the promissory note is returned to the game box.

## Radical Advancement (Technology)

- If the Deepwrought player replaces the *Antimass Deflectors* technology, their units may remain in and move out of any asteroid fields they are currently in, but cannot move additional units into or through.
- The Deepwrought player may replace *Radical Advancement* itself for a biotic technology with two prerequisites.*
- If the Deepwrought player has the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player’s *Alliance* promissory note in their play area, they will not draw an action card from the ability of the [Nekro Acidos](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Leaders:~:text=Nekro%20Acidos) commander when they resolve *Radical Advancement*.*

## D.W.S. Luminous (Flagship)

- The *D.W.S. Luminous* gains +1 for each system it moves through that contains the Deepwrought player’s units, regardless of any other units present.
- The [Argent](https://www.tirules2.com/F_argent) player’s [*Aerie Hololattice*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Argent_Flight#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Aerie%20Hololattice) technology prevents the *D.W.S. Luminous* from moving through systems containing the Argent player’s structures.

## Doctor Carrina (Agent)

- Doctor Carrina may be used even if no prerequisite is skipped, but the coexisting infantry is not placed. This includes when the player chooses not to ignore any prerequisites, or researches a technology with no prerequisites. The player may ignore prerequisites they already fulfill on a technology that has at least one prerequisite.
- The Deepwrought player cannot use Doctor Carrina to start coexisting with the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player, as the Nekro player cannot skip a prerequisite.
- Doctor Carrina cannot place the infantry on a planet that contains none of that player’s units, as there are no units to coexist with.*

## Aello (Commander)

- The Deepwrought player does not have the capability to prevent other players from using the ability of Aello.
- Aello can only reduce a cost paid with resources, including trade goods spent in place of resources. It cannot reduce a cost paid with influence, trade goods spent in place of influence, or any other currency.*
- If a player has the Deepwrought player’s *Alliance* promissory note, then other players may use the ability of either or both (or neither):
    - Another player may use the ability from both for a two resource discount, triggering the commodity ability for both the Deepwrought player and the player with their *Alliance* promissory note.
    - Another player may use the ability from only the Deepwrought player for a one resource discount, triggering the commodity ability for just the Deepwrought player.
    - Another player may use the ability from only the player with the *Alliance* promissory note for a one resource discount, triggering the commodity ability for just the player with the *Alliance* promissory note.
    - Another player may use the ability from neither player, and receive no discount; the commodity ability will trigger for neither the Deepwrought player nor the player with their *Alliance* promissory note.
    - The Deepwrought player and the player with their *Alliance* promissory note may each use the ability of the other for a one resource discount, triggering the commodity ability for that other player.*
    - If the [Mahact](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mahact) player has the Deepwrought player’s command token in their fleet pool, then this gives each other player the option of another discount. The Deepwrought player, the player with their *Alliance* promissory note, and the Mahact player may choose up to two of the other players with the ability, triggering their commodity ability, and resulting in a corresponding discount of up to two resources. All other players may choose up to three of the players with the ability, triggering their commodity ability, and resulting in a corresponding discount of up to three resources.*

## Ta Zern — Wave Function Collapse (Hero)

- If they also owned the purged technology, the Deepwrought player too will research another technology.
- If another player has gained the purged technology through *Share Knowledge*, they own it, so they purge it and research another technology, which they keep after *Share Knowledge* is returned.
- If the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player purges a technology, they will gain three command tokens instead of researching another technology.

## Visionaria Select (Breakthrough)

- When *Visionaria Select* is exhausted, all other players have the opportunity to participate; the Deepwrought player does not choose which players will participate.*
- The researching player chooses which promissory note they give to the Deepwrought player.*
- Giving a promissory note because of *Visionaria Select* is not resolving a transaction.*
- If the Deepwrought player already owns a technology that a participating player researches, then the Deepwrought player will not gain a technology.*
- If the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player participates, they will gain three command tokens. The Deepwrought player will not gain a technology this way.*
