## Notes

- A breakthrough is a faction specific card.
- A breakthrough is commonly gained through an expedition. There are other game effects that can gain a player their faction’s breakthrough.
- When a player gains their faction’s breakthrough, they perform a breakthrough roll, which may cause The Fracture to enter play.
- A breakthrough has 1 or more abilities, and a synergy (other than for the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player).
    - If a breakthrough has a passive ability and an exhaust ability, the passive ability always remains in effect, even when the breakthrough is exhausted.
- A breakthrough itself is not a technology; it alone cannot be used to meet a prerequisite when researching a technology, nor can it alone be used to meet the technology requirement of an objective.
- The [Rebellion](https://www.tirules2.com/F_rebellion) player gains their breakthrough as a starting component, which triggers a breakthrough roll during setup; The Fracture may be in play from the start of the game.
