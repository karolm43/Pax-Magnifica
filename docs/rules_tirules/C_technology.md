## Antimass Deflectors

- The −1 applies to space cannon rolls during both the **Space Cannon Offense** and **Space Cannon Defense** steps.
- If a player gains then loses the *Antimass Deflectors* technology, their units may remain in and move out of any asteroid fields they are currently in, but cannot move additional units into or through.

## Dark Energy Tap

- A ship may be moved into the system containing the frontier token during the same action that it is explored.
- Exploring the frontier token happens at the end of the tactical action. Notably, this is after the **Space Cannon Offense** and/or **Space Combat** steps.
- Exploring the frontier token is mandatory if the player has a ship in the active system at the end of the tactical action.
- When a player gains *Dark Energy Tap*, they do not immediately explore any frontier tokens in systems containing their ships. They must activate those systems to explore those tokens.
    - If a player moves or places a ship into a system outside of a tactical action, they cannot explore a frontier token in that system.
- The [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player cannot explore a frontier token with a [Floating Factory](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Technology#Faction_Technology:~:text=Floating%20Factory). However, if they produce a ship during the **Production** step, they will be able to use that ship to explore the token.*
- A player cannot use *Dark Energy Tap* to retreat into a system that contains another player’s units, including ground forces and structures on planets.

## Gravity Drive

- The +1 from *Gravity Drive* may be combined with the +1 from the *Flank Speed* action card, applying +2 to the move value of that ship.

## Sling Relay

- The produced unit must be paid for.
- *Sling Relay* cannot be used without producing a unit.

## Fleet Logistics

- A player may use the ability of *Fleet Logistics* the same turn they gain it.
- If a player’s turn is ended by a game effect, they cannot use *Fleet Logistics* to perform an additional action.
- A player cannot use *Fleet Logistics* to pass on the same turn they performed an action.
- If a player’s second action would be to use a component action to play an action card, and that card is canceled, they are not required to perform an alternate second action on that turn.
- If a player’s first action is a strategic action, the other players resolve the secondary ability of that strategy card before the player performs their second action.
- Effects that trigger at the start or end of a player’s turn will not trigger between each of those player’s actions.*

## Light/Wave Deflector

- A ship that uses *Light/Wave Deflector* to move through a system containing another player’s ships may pick up and transport units from that system.
- *Light/Wave Deflector* does not allow a player to produce ships in a system that contains another player’s ships.

## Neural Motivator

- *Neural Motivator* only applies to the **Draw Action Card** step of the status phase. It does not apply to other effects that draw action cards, including but not limited to:
    - The *Politics* strategy card.
    - The *Minister of Policy* law.
    - The *Functioning Base* exploration card.
    - The *Prophet’s Tears* relic.

## Psychoarchaeology

- Exhausting planets to gain trade goods can be done at any time during action phase, except when another ability is being resolved. For example, if a player has one of their planets with a technology specialty targeted with the *Uprising* action card, they cannot use *Psychoarchaeology* in response to gain a trade good.
- If multiple planets are exhausted, the trade goods are gained one at a time.

## X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω (replaced by ΩΩ)

- This card is replaced by the ΩΩ version.
- *X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω* may be used against each planet in a system, if the conditions are met.*
- *X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω* will trigger only after all bombardment hits have been assigned.*
- The [L1Z1X](https://www.tirules2.com/F_lizix) may use *X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω* after they use their [`Harrow`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_L1Z1X_Mindnet#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Harrow) faction ability.*
- *X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω* cannot be used unless the player’s `Bombardment` destroys at least one infantry; if all hits are assigned to mechs, it has no effect.

## X–89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ

- A player with *X–89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ* must mandatorily double the number of hits they produce.
- Effects that cancel hits will only cancel hits based on the doubled number of hits.
- Ground forces that are participating in space combat as though they were ships do not double the hits they produce.*
    - Ships that are participating in ground combat as though they were ground forces double the hits they produce.*
- A player with *X–89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ* must mandatorily exhaust each planet they make a `bombardment` roll against, even if they produce no hits.
- A player who bombards another player’s coexisting units on a planet they control must still exhaust that planet.
- A war sun must target the same planet with all three of its `bombardment` dice.
    - If a unit may roll additional `bombardment` dice, such as by the *Plasma Scoring* technology, that additional die must target the same planet.
- A player may roll for `bombardment` against a planet that contains no ground forces.

## Plasma Scoring

- Only one additional dice is rolled for each bombardment or space cannon roll, regardless of how many units are using that ability.
    - During an invasion in a system with multiple planets, only one unit will add a dice to its bombardment roll.
    - During an invasion, if ground forces have been committed to multiple planets, *Plasma Scoring* will add an additional die to the space cannon rolls for each planet during the **Space Cannon Defense** step.
- If a player makes separate `Bombardment` rolls against multiple coexisting players on the same planet, *Plasma Scoring* adds one additional die to only one of those rolls.
- A space dock using the `Space Cannon` ability due to the *Experimental Battlestation* action card may roll an additional die from *Plasma Scoring*.

## AI Development Algorithm

- When a player exhausts *AI Development Algorithm* to ignore a prerequisite of a unit upgrade technology that they are researching, they may still use *AI Development Algorithm* itself for meeting a warfare technology (red) prerequisite on that unit upgrade technology in the same manner as any other technology.
    - For example, *AI Development Algorithm* alone may be used to research the Destroyer II unit upgrade.

## Magen Defense Grid (replaced by Ω)

- This card is replaced by the Ω version.
- *Magen Defense Grid* cannot be used if all of a player’s units in combat that had the `Planetary Shield` ability have lost it.

## Magen Defense Grid Ω (replaced by ΩΩ)

- This card is replaced by the ΩΩ version.
- The player with *Magen Defense Grid Ω* chooses which ground force the hit is assigned to.
- The chosen ground force may cancel the hit by using its `Sustain Damage` ability, if present. A different ground force with the `Sustain Damage` ability cannot do so.*
- If the last of a player’s ground forces in a ground combat are destroyed by the effect of *Magen Defense Grid Ω*, that player loses the combat immediately. They will be unable to use any “start of combat” abilities.*
- The hit is produced at the start of ground combat, before any dice are rolled. A ground force destroyed by this hit does not roll.

## Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ

- The player with *Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ* chooses which ground force the hit is assigned to.
- The chosen ground force may cancel the hit by using its `Sustain Damage` ability, if present. A different ground force with the `Sustain Damage` ability cannot do so.*
- If the last of a player’s ground forces in a ground combat are destroyed by the effect of *Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ*, that player loses the combat immediately. They will be unable to use any “start of combat” abilities.*
- *Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ* will place infantry regardless of who activates the system.
- If a player has multiple structures in the system, one infantry is placed with each.
- The infantry must be placed on the same planet as the structure.
    - If the [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player owns *Magen Defense Grid ΩΩ*, and a system containing a [Floating Factory](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Technology#Faction_Technology:~:text=Floating%20Factory) is activated, then the infantry will be placed in the space area.*

## Self–Assembly Routines

- A mech cannot be placed in a space area with *Self–Assembly Routines*, and so cannot be placed in a system that does not contain a planet.
- If a player wishes to place a mech, but there are none left in their reinforcements, they may remove a mech from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and place that instead. The mech will be placed undamaged.

## Duranium Armor

- A player must mandatorily repair a unit with *Duranium Armor* during each round of combat, if possible.
- A player uses *Duranium Armor* only once during each round of combat, at the end of the **Assign Hits** substep. Hits assigned outside of this substep will not allow *Duranium Armor* to repair an additional unit.
- *Duranium Armor* is used even if a player’s opponent produced no hits during a round of combat.
- If a player starts a combat with a damaged unit, they use *Duranium Armor* in the first round of combat.
    - A player may use *Duranium Armor* to repair a unit damaged by `Space Cannon` or `Bombardment` hits that occurred immediately before the combat.
- If a unit is damaged during combat without using its `Sustain Damage` ability, it may be repaired by *Duranium Armor* during the same combat round.
- Only units participating in the combat may be chosen to be repaired.

## Assault Cannon

- A player with *Assault Cannon* must mandatorily resolve its ability if they meet the requirement.
- If a player loses ships before they can resolve the *Assault Cannon* ability, they will be unable to resolve it if they no longer have three or more non–fighter ships.
    - This will most commonly happen when both players in a combat have *Assault Cannon*, and the defender has exactly three non–fighter ships.
    - Examples of other abilities that may remove units at the start of a space combat include the [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player’s [*Dimensional Splicer*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Technology#Faction_Technology:~:text=Dimensional%20Splicer) faction technology ability, the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player’s [`Ambush`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Ambush) faction ability, the [Yin](https://www.tirules2.com/F_yin) player’s [*Impulse Core*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Technology#Faction_Technology:~:text=Impulse%20Core) faction technology ability, as well as abilities such as the *Courageous to the End* action card or the *Reflective Shielding* action card that may trigger off of any of the previous abilities.*
- A player may use an ability to add ships at the start of combat. If this brings the number of non–fighter ships to three or more, the player may then use the ability of *Assault Cannon*.*
    - Each other player may use one ability after that player has gained ships, and before that player has the opportunity to use the *Assault Cannon* ability. This may prevent that player from using the ability, as above.*
    - Examples of abilities that may add units at the start of space combat include a combination of the Mentak player’s [*Sleeper Cell*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Leaders:~:text=Sleeper%20Cell) hero ability and their `Ambush` faction ability, the ability of the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player’s flagship, [*The Alastor*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Flagship:~:text=The%20Alastor), and the [Naaz–Rokha](https://www.tirules2.com/F_naaz_rokha) player’s [*Eidolon*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Naaz-Rokha_Alliance#Mech:~:text=Z-Grav%20Eidolon) mech ability.*
- Effects that cancel hits, such as `Sustain Damage`, cannot be used to prevent ships from being destroyed.

## Sarween Tools

- If units are produced without any unit using its `Production` ability, *Sarween Tools* will not provide a reduction to the cost.

## Scanlink Drone Network

- The units must be on the planet before the **Movement** step of the tactical action.

## Graviton Laser System

- *Graviton Laser System* has no effect during the **Space Cannon Defense** step, as hits can only be assigned to ground forces.
- Hits produced while *Graviton Laser System* is in effect may still be canceled by the `Sustain Damage` ability.
- *Graviton Laser System* applies to every die rolled for the `Space Cannon` roll, including dice rolled by units using another unit’s `Space Cannon`, such as the [Ral Nel](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ral_nel) player’s Linkships.

## Predictive Intelligence

- The ability of *Predictive Intelligence* to cast additional votes cannot be used if it is exhausted.

## Transit Diodes

- When resolving the ability of *Transit Diodes*, that player may remove ground forces from a system containing their command token.*
- The ground forces may be removed from on a planet or from in a space area.*
- The ground forces may be removed from different systems.*
- The ground forces may be placed on different planets.*
- If a mech is damaged when it is removed, it will remain damaged when it is placed.

## Integrated Economy

- The produced units must be paid for.
- There is no limit on the number of units produced, so long as the combined cost does not exceed the planet’s resource value.
- A player explores a newly controlled planet before resolving *Integrated Economy*, so any attachment gained from that exploration affects the planet’s resource value.
- A player may produce ships with *Integrated Economy*, unless another player has ships in the space area of that planet’s system.
- No unit is using its `Production` ability. The *Sarween Tools* technology and similar effects will not apply.
- Effects that let a player produce a unit for less than its cost, such as the [Nomad](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nomad) player’s commander, [Navarch Feng](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nomad#Leaders:~:text=Navarch%20Feng), do not change that unit’s cost.*
