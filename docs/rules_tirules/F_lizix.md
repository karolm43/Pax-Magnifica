## Assimilate

- Any space docks that are placed by this ability during a tactical action may produce units during the **Production** step of that tactical action.
- If the L1Z1X player resolves their `Assimilate` ability, and then plays the *Infiltrate* action card, or vice versa, they will replace zero PDS and space docks with zero PDS and space docks when they resolve the latter ability.*
- If the L1Z1X player would have to place a structure, but there are none of that type left in their reinforcements, they may, but are not required to, remove a structure of that type from any system that does not contain one of their command tokens and place that instead.
- When the L1Z1X player gains control of a planet, they also replace coexisting structures on that planet.

## Harrow

- The `Planetary Shield` ability will prevent the use of the `Harrow` ability on a planet.
- The L1Z1X player cannot use their `Harrow` ability if they are not the active player.
- The L1Z1X player may use their `Harrow` ability at the end of the round in which their last ground force was destroyed. This may cause the combat to end in a draw.
- Every unit in the active system with the `Bombardment` ability may use it against the planet the ground combat is taking place on, regardless of whether that unit bombarded that planet during the **Bombardment** step of the invasion.
    - An Annihilator may bombard when using `Harrow` if that mech is not currently participating in ground combat, even if it has been committed to a different planet, and has been or will be involved in another ground combat during the current invasion.
- The L1Z1X player may use the ability of the *X–89 Bacterial Weapon Ω* or *X–89 Bacterial Weapon ΩΩ* technologies whenever they bombard.

## Inheritance Systems (Technology)

- The two resources for *Inheritance Systems* must be paid in addition to and separately from any other costs paid to research the technology.*
    - For example, if the L1Z1X player is performing the secondary of the *Technology* strategy card, they cannot spend the five resources of [0.0.0] plus one trade good to pay for the costs of both *Technology* and *Inheritance Systems*. Instead, they would have to spend [0.0.0] to pay for the cost of *Technology*, wasting the fifth resource, then spend two additional resources to pay for the cost of *Inheritance Systems*.*
    - The owner of the *Minister of Sciences* law must still spend the two resources for *Inheritance Systems*, as they pay to ignore prerequisites, not to research the technology.*
- *Inheritance Systems* may be used when researching a unit upgrade technology, including the L1Z1X player’s own faction technologies such as [Super Dreadnought](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_L1Z1X_Mindnet#Dreadnought:~:text=Super-Dreadnought) II.
- If *Inheritance Systems* is researched as the first of two technologies researched back to back, such as with the primary ability of the *Technology* strategy card or the *Ixthian Artifact* agenda, it may be used immediately to ignore the prerequisites of the second technology.

## The Helmsman — Dark Space Navigation (Hero)

- Ships in systems with one of the L1Z1X player’s command tokens may move.
- When the L1Z1X player’s ships move outside of a tactical action, they may transport units from systems that contain the L1Z1X player’s command tokens.
- Ships move directly to their destination system. They may only transport units from their origin system.
- Ships moving out of a gravity rift must roll for removal.
    - Ships do not roll for removal if the L1Z1X player owns the *Circlet of the Void* relic.
- Ships cannot move into a supernova, even with *Dark Space Navigation*.
    - Ships may move into a supernova if the L1Z1X player owns the *Circlet of the Void* relic.
    - Ships may travel into an asteroid field only if the L1Z1X player owns the *Antimass Deflectors* technology.
- *Dark Space Navigation* may be used to move ships into the wormhole nexus. If the nexus is inactive, this will cause it to activate.

## Annihilator (Mech)

- An Annihilator may bombard when using the L1Z1X player’s [`Harrow`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_L1Z1X_Mindnet#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Harrow) ability if that mech is not currently participating in ground combat, even if it has been committed to a different planet, and has been or will be involved in another ground combat during the current invasion.

## Fealty Uplink (Breakthrough)

- If the L1Z1X player gains control of an uncontrolled planet, they may choose if they place the infantry before or after they explore the planet.*
- The L1Z1X player must mandatorily place the infantry.*
