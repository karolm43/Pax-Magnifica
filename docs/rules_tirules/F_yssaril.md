## Stall Tactics

- `Stall Tactics` is a faction ability, not an action card, and so cannot be canceled by the *Sabotage* action card.

## Scheming

- `Scheming` applies whenever the Yssaril player draws action cards.
- The Yssaril player will draw only one additional action card, regardless of how many action cards they are instructed to draw.*
- The discarded action card may be any card in the Yssaril player’s hand; it does not have to be one of the cards they just drew.
- No other abilities may resolve until the Yssaril player has discarded the action card.*
- Effects that give the Yssaril player action cards without drawing them, such as *The Codex* relic or the *Reverse Engineer* action card, will not trigger `Scheming`.

## Crafty

- The effect of the *Executive Sanctions* law does not affect the Yssaril player.
- `Crafty` does not prevent the Yssaril player from being made to discard action cards by other effects.

## Spy Net (Promissory Note)

- The player that plays *Spy Net* must look at the Yssaril player’s hand of action cards. They do not have to read or memorize what they look at.*
- A player may receive *Spy Net* in a transaction during their turn. If received at the start of that player’s turn, that player may play it immediately.
- A player who already has *Spy Net* may play it, then receive it again in a transaction, but cannot play it again that turn, as the “start of your turn” timing has passed.

## Mageon Implants (Technology)

- When resolving *Mageon Implants*, the Yssaril player is not drawing a card. As such, [`Scheming`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Yssaril_Tribes#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Scheming) will not trigger.
- The Yssaril player cannot show the other player’s hand of action cards to any other players.*
    - The Yssaril player may claim orally what action cards they saw.

## Transparasteel Plating (Technology)

- Other players may resolve faction abilities, leader abilities or technology abilities during the Yssaril player’s turns.*

## Y’sia Y’ssrila (Flagship)

- If the Yssaril player owns the *Light/Wave Deflector* technology, it will have no additional effect on the *Y’sia Y’ssrila*.*
- If the [Argent](https://www.tirules2.com/F_argent) player owns the [*Aerie Hololattice*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Argent_Flight#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Aerie%20Hololattice) technology, the *Y’sia Y’ssrila* cannot move through systems that contain the Argent player’s structures.

## Ssruu (Agent)

- If the Yssaril player duplicates an agent that allows a unit to roll an additional die, and both the duplicated agent and Ssruu target the same unit, that unit will roll two additional dice.*
- If the Yssaril player duplicates the [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player’s [Captain Mendosa](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Clan_of_Saar#Leaders:~:text=Captain%20Mendosa) agent, they may do so before or after other effects are used to modify any ship’s move value. This includes the Saar player’s Captain Mendosa agent effect.*
- If both the Yssaril player and the [Vuil’raith](https://www.tirules2.com/F_vuilraith) player wish to target the same player with the Vuil’raith [The Stillness of Stars](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Vuil'Raith_Cabal#:~:text=The%20Stillness%20of%20Stars) agent ability, then they will both be able to capture a ship, resolving in initiative order from the active player. Both players will still use the target player’s full commodity value to determine which units they may capture. Should the first player to resolve capture the last of a type of unit from the target player’s reinforcements, the latter player cannot capture a unit of the same type.*
- If the Yssaril player duplicates the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player’s [Suffi An](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Leaders:~:text=Suffi%20An) agent, then the Yssaril player and the player targeted by the Mentak player’s [`Pillage`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Pillage) ability will draw action cards. The Mentak player will not.*
- Duplicating the effect of the [Argent](https://www.tirules2.com/F_argent) player’s [Trillossa Aun Mirik](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Argent_Flight#Leaders:~:text=Trillossa%20Aun%20Mirik) agent, the [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player’s [Emissary Taivra](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Leaders:~:text=Emissary%20Taivra) agent or the [Jol–Nar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_jol_nar) player’s [Doctor Sucaban](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Universities_of_Jol-Nar#Leaders:~:text=Doctor%20Sucaban) agent during the same timing window as that agent is used will have no additional effect.*
- If either the Yssaril player or the [Nomad](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nomad) player resolve the effect of the Nomad player’s [The Thundarian](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nomad#Leaders:~:text=The%20Thundarian) agent, the other will be unable to resolve the same effect immediately. They must wait until dice are rolled again before they may resolve the effect.*

## Deepgloom Executable (Breakthrough)

- The Yssaril player may perform a transaction with another player without being neighbors with that player.*
- The Yssaril player may perform a transaction with another player even if neither player is the active player.*
    - *Deepgloom Executable* may allow the Yssaril player to perform a transaction at a time they usually could not. For example, if the [Mentak](https://www.tirules2.com/F_mentak) player exhausts their agent, [Suffi An](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Leaders:~:text=Suffi%20An), during the strategy phase when another player gains the trade goods on the strategy card they chose, the Yssaril player may allow either of those players to use their [`Scheming`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Yssaril_Tribes#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Scheming) ability, and then perform a transaction with that player.*
- The Yssaril player may allow another player to use one of their faction abilities whenever that ability would be used. That player may use it for the duration of that ability.
