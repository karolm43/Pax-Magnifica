# Sustain Damage

> Źródło: [https://www.tirules2.com/R_sustain_damage](https://www.tirules2.com/R_sustain_damage) — treść reguł © Fantasy Flight Games.
> Zrzut z repo tirules (GPL-3.0). Patrz `SOURCE.md`.

## Rules Reference

Some units have the `Sustain Damage` ability. Immediately before a player assigns hits to their units, that player can use the `Sustain Damage` ability of any of their units in the active system.

- For each `Sustain Damage` ability that a player uses, one hit produced by another player’s units is canceled. Then, each unit using this ability is placed on its side to indicate that it is damaged.
- A damaged unit does not have reduced capabilities and is functionally the same as an undamaged unit, except that it cannot use the `Sustain Damage` ability.
- A damaged unit cannot use the `Sustain Damage` ability until it is repaired during the status phase or by another game effect.
- A unit can use its `Sustain Damage` ability any time a hit is produced against it. This includes hits produced during combat and from unit abilities such as the `Space Cannon` ability.
    - A unit can only use the `Sustain Damage` ability if it is eligible to be hit. For example, a player cannot use a dreadnought’s `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel a hit from `Anti–Fighter Barrage.`
- The `Sustain Damage` ability cannot be used to cancel an effect that directly destroys a unit.
- The Barony of [Letnev](https://www.tirules2.com/F_letnev)’s [*Non–Euclidean Shielding*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Barony_of_Letnev#:~:text=Non-Euclidean%20Shielding) faction technology allows the Letnev player’s units with the `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel up to two hits instead of one.

## Notes

- A ship in the active system may use its `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel a hit produced by a space cannon roll during the **Space Cannon Offense** step.
- A mech committed to a planet may use its `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel a hit produced during the **Space Cannon Defense** step by a unit on that planet.
- Mechs may use their `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel a hit produced by a bombardment roll.
- A non–fighter ship cannot use its `Sustain Damage` ability to cancel a hit produced by an anti–fighter barrage roll, unless an effect such as the *Waylay* action card makes it eligible to be hit.
- A unit may only use its `Sustain Damage` ability once in each timing window, even if it is repaired.
- If multiple ships use their `Sustain Damage` ability, a *Direct Hit* action card may be played for each.
    - When a player uses the `Sustain Damage` ability of one of their ships, they do not have to decide if another of their ships in the same combat will use its `Sustain Damage` ability until after their opponent has used or declined to use a *Direct Hit*.
    - A mech cannot be destroyed by *Direct Hit*, as it is not a ship.
- All damaged units are repaired during the status phase, even if a game effect has caused those units to no longer have the `Sustain Damage` ability.
