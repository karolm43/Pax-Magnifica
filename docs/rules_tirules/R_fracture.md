## Notes

- The Fracture consists of additional systems beyond the regular bounds of the game map.
- When a player gains their breakthrough, they roll a die. On a result of 1 or 10, The Fracture enters play. Other game effects may also bring The Fracture into play.
    - The “0” side of the d10 represents a result of 10.
- When The Fracture enters play, it is placed against the edge of the regular game map.
    - The placement location of The Fracture is arbitrary, and should have no mechanical effect on the game state.
    - All systems in The Fracture are considered to be on the edge of the game board.
    - Systems that The Fracture is touching remain on the edge of the game board.
- While The Fracture is not in play, its systems, such as the one containing Styx, are not legal targets for abilities such as those of the [Yin](https://www.tirules2.com/F_yin) player’s and [L1Z1X](https://www.tirules2.com/F_lizix) player’s heroes.
- If The Fracture enters play as a result of the expedition that also places [Thunder’s Edge](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Thunder's_Edge), Thunder’s Edge is placed first, and then the final ingress token is placed in its system.
- Attachments, such as the *Nano–Forge* relic and the [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) player’s [*Terraform*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Terraform) promissory note, may be attached to planets in The Fracture.
- When The Fracture is brought into play, neutral units are placed on the planets in The Fracture as well as the space area of those planets’ systems, as indicated on the reverse side of The Fracture tiles.
- Frontier tokens are not placed in systems in The Fracture when it is brought into play, as frontier tokens are only placed during setup. However, if The Fracture is in play when the [Empyrean](https://www.tirules2.com/F_empyrean) player resolves their [*Multiverse Shift*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Empyrean#Leaders:~:text=Multiverse%20Shift) hero ability, frontier tokens will be placed in the systems in The Fracture that do not contain a planet.
- When The Fracture is brought into play, several ingress tokens will be placed on the game board. Several systems in The Fracture contain a printed egress. Each ingress is adjacent to each egress, and vice versa. An ingress is not adjacent to an ingress and an egress is not adjacent to an egress.
    - With sufficient movement, a ship could move from a system containing an ingress in the regular game map, into a system containing an egress within The Fracture, and then out to a different system containing an ingress in the regular game map. A ship could also do the reverse to travel from an egress to a different egress via an ingress.
- If The Fracture was brought into play as a result of a player rolling a die upon gaining their breakthrough, they will place ingress tokens according to the synergy on their breakthrough. For each color of technology in their breakthrough’s synergy, they choose three planets with a matching technology specialty, and place an ingress token in that planet’s system.
    - If there are fewer than three planets with a technology specialty of a given color, as many planets as possible are chosen.
    - When the [Vuil’raith](https://www.tirules2.com/F_vuilraith) player’s [*Al’raith Ix Ianovar*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Vuil'Raith_Cabal#:~:text=Al%27raith%20Ix%20Ianovar) breakthrough brings The Fracture into play, ingress tokens are placed this way, as if The Fracture had been rolled for, even though no roll is made.
- If a different game effect places The Fracture into play, or The Fracture enters play because of the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player’s breakthrough (which does not have a synergy), then the player that caused The Fracture to enter play chooses one planet with a technology specialty for each of the four colors of technology, and places ingress tokens in each of those planets’ systems.
- If one system contains two planets with a technology specialty, only one of those planets may be chosen when placing ingress tokens.
- After ingress tokens are placed with either of these two methods, one additional ingress token is placed. If Thunder’s Edge is on the game board, then an ingress token is placed into its system. Otherwise, an ingress token is placed into the Mecatol Rex system.
- A system cannot contain two or more ingress tokens.
- When a player gains control of a planet in The Fracture that is not already controlled by another player, they draw one relic card.
