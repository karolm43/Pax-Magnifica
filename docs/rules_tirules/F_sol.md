## Orbital Drop

- `Orbital Drop` may be used multiple times each round, once for each command token spent from the Sol player’s strategy pool. Each use is a separate action.

## Versatile

- If the Sol player owns the *Hyper Metabolism* technology, they gain four command tokens during the status phase.

## Military Support (Promissory Note)

- A player may receive *Military Support* in a transaction during the Sol player’s turn. If received at the start of the Sol player’s turn, that player may play it immediately.
    - If a player received *Military Support* on a previous turn, they may play it at the start of the Sol player’s turn, and immediately perform a transaction with the Sol player to regain it. However, *Military Support* cannot be played twice in one timing window. The active player cannot play *Military Support* again until the Sol player’s next turn.
- *Military Support* is played before the Sol player may use [*Orbital Drop*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Federation_of_Sol#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Orbital%20Drop).*
- *Military Support* may be played the turn the Sol player passes.
- *Military Support* may be played when the Sol player has no command tokens in their strategy pool.

## Bellum Gloriosum (Breakthrough)

- If the Sol player produces multiple ships simultaneously, the combined capacity is used to determine how many fighters and ground forces do not count toward the `Production` limit.*
- The additional fighters and ground forces are produced simultaneously with any other fighters and ground forces, and the resources paid will be determined by their combined cost.*
    - For example, say that the Sol player has `Production 2`, and wishes to produce a dreadnought and two infantry. They would pay five resources for this, as they would pay a single resource for the pair of infantry.*
- Ships produced with *Bellum Gloriosum* increase the number of fighters and ground forces that may be produced, even if those ships must then be removed to satisfy the fleet pool limit.
