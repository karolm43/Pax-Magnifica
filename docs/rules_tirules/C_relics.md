## Book of Latvinia

- If a player owns all technologies without prerequisites when they draw the *Book of Latvinia*, they will not research any technologies.
    - If a player owns all–but–one technologies without prerequisites when they draw the *Book of Latvinia*, they will research only that one technology.
- A player cannot gain a technology with a prerequisite by ignoring that prerequisite, such as by exhausting a planet with a technology specialty.
- If the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player draws the *Book of Latvinia*, they will gain six command tokens and zero technologies, even if they own all technologies without prerequisites.*
- When a player uses the component action of the *Book of Latvinia*, it is purged, regardless of which outcome is resolved.
- If a player uses the component action of the *Book of Latvinia* when they control planets with each type of technology specialty, they mandatorily gain a victory point; they cannot choose instead to gain the speaker token.

## Circlet of the Void

- The player that owns *Circlet of the Void* may move through or into supernovae and/or asteroid fields.
- The player that owns *Circlet of the Void* may move through nebulae, and their ships in nebulae do not have their movement values reduced.
- The player that owns *Circlet of the Void* still applies the movement bonus to their ships that will move through gravity rifts.
- The player that owns *Circlet of the Void* will still ignore anomaly movement effects and not roll for gravity rifts if *Circlet of the Void* is exhausted.

## The Codex

- The action card discard is shuffled to form a new action card deck as soon as the action card deck has zero cards in it.
- It is public knowledge which action cards a player takes using *The Codex*.

## The Crown of Emphidia

## The Crown of Thalnos

- A player may only reroll combat rolls with *The Crown of Thalnos*. They cannot reroll anti–fighter barrage rolls and similar.
- For each unit that rolls multiple combat dice, the player with *The Crown of Thalnos* should roll those unit’s dice separately from any other combat dice.*
- A reroll caused by any other effect will not cause a unit to be destroyed.*
- Units destroyed by *The Crown of Thalnos* are destroyed at the end of the **Roll Dice** step, before the **Assign Hits** step.
- A unit with the `Sustain Damage` ability cannot use it to avoid being destroyed when it fails to produce a hit with the reroll from *The Crown of Thalnos*.*

## Dominus Orb

- *Dominus Orb* does not increase the distance units may move; they must still be able to reach the active system.

## Dynamis Core

- If a player uses the purge ability of *Dynamis Core*, they will need to return commodities if they now exceed their original commodity value.

## Heart of Ixth

- The “0” side of the d10 represents a result of 10.

## JR–XS455–O

- *JR–XS455–O* is an agent. All rules that apply to agents (and leaders) apply to *JR–XS455–O*.
    - *JR–XS455–O* may be refreshed by the [Nomad](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nomad) player's [*Temporal Command Suite*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nomad#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Temporal%20Command%20Suite) technology.
    - The ability of *JR–XS455–O* may be duplicated by the [Yssaril](https://www.tirules2.com/F_yssaril) player’s agent, Ssruu.

## Lightrail Ordnance

- The space docks may use the `Space Cannon` ability during both the **Space Cannon Offense** and **Space Cannon Defense** steps.*

## Maw of Worlds

- The player does not have to meet the prerequisites of the technologies they acquire using *Maw of Worlds*.*
- The planets exhausted by *Maw of Worlds* will ready at the end of the agenda phase.

## Metali Void Armaments

## Metali Void Shielding

- The affected ship may be destroyed by the *Direct Hit* action card after it uses the `Sustain Damage` ability.*
- A player may play the *Reflective Shielding* action card after it uses the `Sustain Damage` ability via *Metali Void Shielding*.*
- If a player owns *Metali Void Shielding*, and their opponent plays the *Waylay* action card, they may use *Metali Void Shielding* for both the `Anti–Fighter Barrage` and combat rolls in that round of space combat.*
- *Metali Void Shielding* has no effect when the [Argent](https://www.tirules2.com/F_argent) player resolves their [`Raid Formation`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Argent_Flight#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Raid%20Formation) faction ability.*
- If the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player owns *Metali Void Shielding*, their ground forces participating in space combat due to their flagship, [*The Alastor*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Flagship:~:text=The%20Alastor), may be damaged.

## Nano–Forge

- The attached planet will be legendary for effects such as scoring the *Make History* public objective and similar objectives, transferring the *Shard of the Throne* relic, and the [Winnu](https://www.tirules2.com/F_winnu) player's commander, [Rickar Rickani](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Winnu#Leaders:~:text=Rickar%20Rickani). The attached planet also can no longer be chosen by effects that require a non–legendary planet, such as the *Stellar Converter* relic.

## Neuraloop

- A player may use *Neuraloop* to purge *Neuraloop* itself, resolving its ability.
- A player may use *Neuraloop* to purge *Nano–Forge*, even if it is attached to a planet, if they control that planet. That planet loses the increase to its resources and influence values, and is no longer legendary.*
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge another relic that has a purge ability, that purge ability is not resolved.*
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge the *Circlet of the Void*, that player’s units may remain in and move out of any supernovae and/or asteroid fields they are currently in, but cannot move additional units into or through, unless they have another ability that would allow them to do so, such as the *Antimass Deflectors* technology.*
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge the *Dynamis Core*, and they have more commodities than their faction’s printed commodity value, they return any extras to the supply.
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge the *The Obsidian* relic, that player must discard one unscored secret objective if they have a total of four scored or unscored secret objectives. There is no effect if they have four scored secret objectives.*
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge the *Shard of the Throne*, that player loses a victory point.*
- A secret objective revealed by *Neuraloop* becomes a public objective.
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to reveal a secret objective as a public objective, that objective does not count toward the limit of three scored or unscored secret objectives for any player that scores it.*
- A player can score a maximum of one objective of any type during or after each combat. A player cannot score the secret objective revealed by *Neuraloop* and one of their own secret objectives from the same combat.
- If a player uses *Neuraloop* to purge another relic, then that player may use *Neuraloop* again to replace the new objective with yet another objective.*
- The discarded objective is shuffled into its respective deck after the replacement objective is revealed.*
    - This happens before the player with *Neuraloop* chooses if they wish to purge another relic to replace the new objective. As such, it is possible for the player to reveal the original objective after purging two or more relics.*

## The Prophet’s Tears

## Scepter of Emelpar

- The *Scepter of Emelpar* may be used whenever a command token would be spent from a player’s strategy pool. This includes when spending to perform the secondary ability of a strategy card, for faction abilities, and when scoring objectives.*
- If a player has no command tokens in their reinforcements, they must use a command token from their command sheet instead when they use the ability of the *Scepter of Emelpar*.*
- A command token spent from reinforcements using *Scepter of Emelpar* instead of from the strategy pool counts toward objectives that require spending command tokens, such as *Galvanize the People*.
- If the [Muaat](https://www.tirules2.com/F_muaat) player uses the ability of the *Scepter of Emelpar*, they will not gain a trade good from the ability of their commander, Magmus.*

## Shard of the Throne

- When the player that owns the *Shard of the Throne* loses control of a legendary planet or a planet in their home system, they immediately give it to the player who gained control of that planet. No abilities may be resolved before this.*
- If the player that gains *Shard of the Throne* controls no legendary planets or planets in their home system, they cannot lose it.*

## The Silver Flame

- The [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player is unaffected by the negative effect of *The Silver Flame*; if their home system is purged, they may still score public objectives due to their [`Nomadic`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Clan_of_Saar#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Nomadic) faction ability.
- Using *The Silver Flame* always causes The Fracture to enter play, regardless of the result of the roll.
- The “0” side of the d10 represents a result of 10.

## Stellar Converter

- A system that contains a planet destroyed by *Stellar Converter*, and no other planets, is considered to contain no planets.
- A player cannot target a planet they control with *Stellar Converter*.*

## The Triad

- *The Triad* is not considered a planet for other game effects.
    - Units cannot be placed on *The Triad*.*
    - *The Triad* cannot be exhausted by the *Uprising* action card.
    - *The Triad* cannot be elected by an “elect planet” agenda, nor can it be exhausted by agenda effects such as the “For” effect of the *New Constitution* agenda.*
    - Attachments such as the *Nano–Forge* relic or the [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) [*Terraform*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Terraform) promissory note cannot be attached to *The Triad*.
    - *The Triad* does not count toward the planets required to score the *Push Boundaries* public objective, nor the *Establish Hegemony* or *Hoard Raw Materials* secret objectives. In addition, it does not count toward any objectives that require a planet in a specific type of system.
    - *The Triad* does not have a planet trait; it cannot be explored.*
- *The Triad* may be readied by effects that ready planets, such as the *Diplomacy* strategy card.*
    - *The Triad* is also readied at the end of the agenda phase.
- *The Triad* may be exhausted to cast votes.
- For *The Triad*, an Unknown Relic Fragment counts as a fourth type of relic fragment, in addition to Hazardous, Cultural, and Industrial.
- *The Triad* is not exhausted by the *Maw of Worlds* relic.
- The [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player does not gain a trade good from their [`Scavenge`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Clan_of_Saar#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Scavenge) faction ability when they gain *The Triad*.
