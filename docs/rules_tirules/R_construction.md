## Rules Reference
The *Construction* strategy card allows players to place structures on planets they control. This card’s initiative value is “4”.
- During the action phase, if the active player has the *Construction* strategy card, they can perform a strategic action to resolve that card’s primary ability.
- To resolve the primary ability on the *Construction* strategy card, the active player either places 1 structure on a planet they control, or uses the **Production** ability of 1 of their space docks. Then, that player places 1 structure on a planet they control.
    - The two structures can be placed on the same planet or on different planets.
- After the active player resolves the primary ability of the *Construction* strategy card, each other player, beginning with the player to the left of the active player and proceeding clockwise, may spend one command token from their strategy pool to place 1 structure on a planet they control.
    - ⚠️ ZAŁOŻENIE: wersja TE NIE wymaga już wskazania systemu ani położenia żetonu w nim (tekst karty: „place 1 structure on a planet you control”); żeton ze strategy pool jest tylko wydawany.
- When a player places a structure using the *Construction* strategy card, they take that structure from their reinforcements.
    - ⚠️ ZAŁOŻENIE: reguła zastępcza z PoK (brak jednostki w rezerwie → zdjęcie jednostki z dowolnego systemu bez własnego żetonu) obowiązuje dalej; TE jej nie zmienia w tekście karty.
## Notes
- A *structure* is a PDS or a space dock (patrz [Structures](https://www.tirules2.com/R_structures)). Typy struktur brać z assetów jednostek, nie z pamięci.
- ⚠️ ZAŁOŻENIE (przeniesione z PoK; TE tego nie zmienia): maksymalnie 1 space dock i 2 PDS na planetę; struktur nie można położyć na planecie z kartą eksploracji *Demilitarized Zone*.
- The structures can be placed in any systems, regardless of whether the player has a command token in the system or not. ⚠️ ZAŁOŻENIE (przeniesione z PoK).
- The *Production* option of the primary ability follows the normal rules for [Production](https://www.tirules2.com/R_production) (koszt jednostek, limit z wartości Production doku).
