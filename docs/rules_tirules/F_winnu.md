## Blood Ties

## Reclamation

- Placing the structure will occur after the **Production** step. If the Winnu player places a Space Dock, they cannot produce out of it during the same action.

## Acquiescence (replaced by Ω) (Promissory Note)

- This card is replaced by the Ω version.
- If either strategy card had trade goods on it at the start of the strategy phase, those trade goods remain with the player that originally chose the strategy card.
- In a three– or four–player game, the player that played *Acquiescence* chooses which one strategy card from each player are swapped.*

## Acquiescence Ω (Promissory Note)

- If the [Muaat](https://www.tirules2.com/F_muaat) player has their commander, Magmus, unlocked, and they play *Acquiescence Ω*, they will not gain a trade good from their commander ability.*

## Hegemonic Trade Policy (Technology)

- The text of *Hegemonic Trade Policy* is changed to: “Exhaust this card when 1 or more of your units use `Production`; swap the resource and influence values of 1 planet you control during that use of `Production`.”
- Changing a planet’s resource value will affect the `Production` value of a Space Dock on that planet.

## Lazax Gate Folding (Technology)

- While *Lazax Gate Folding* is exhausted, the wormhole passive effect still applies if its conditions are met.
- *Lazax Gate Folding* may cause the Mecatol Rex system to be adjacent to other systems containing an alpha or beta wormhole for all players during the Winnu player’s tactical actions.
    - The Winnu player may become neighbors with other players via *Lazax Gate Folding*.
    - Other players may use the `Deep Space Cannon` ability of their units in systems containing alpha or beta wormholes during the **Space Cannon Offense** step of the Winnu player’s turn if the Mecatol Rex system is the active system.
    - A player may retreat to a system containing an alpha or beta wormhole from the Mecatol Rex system during the Winnu player’s tactical action.
    - The [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player may use their [Dimensional Splicer](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Dimensional%20Splicer) faction technology ability in the Mecatol Rex System during the Winnu player’s tactical action.*
- The action ability of *Lazax Gate Folding* cannot be used if the Winnu player does not control Mecatol Rex.

## Salai Sai Corian (Flagship)

- If the Winnu player’s opponent has only fighters participating in the combat, the *Salai Sai Corian* will roll no dice.
- During a combat against the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player in a system containing each player’s flagships, the *Salai Sai Corian* will count the Nekro player’s infantry participating in the combat when determining how many dice to roll.

## Rickar Rickani (Commander)

- If Rickar Rickani is unlocked via a combat in the Mecatol Rex system, then the +2 will apply to combat rolls during that combat.*
- If a system meets two (or more) of the conditions listed, Rickar Rickani will only apply +2 to combat rolls, not +4 (or +6).

## Mathis Mathinus — Imperial Seal (Hero)

- A player chosen to perform the secondary ability must still spend a command token from their strategy pool to do so, except for the *Leadership* strategy card.
    - If the Winnu player resolves the *Trade* strategy card, the players they choose with its primary ability must resolve the secondary ability without spending a command token. Other players chosen with *Imperial Seal* may choose whether to resolve the secondary ability, but must spend a command token to do so.
- [*Acquiescence Ω*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Winnu#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Acquiescence) cannot be triggered by this ability, as using *Imperial Seal* is a component action, not a strategic action.

## Reclaimer (Mech)

- If the Winnu player places a Reclaimer with the *Imperial Arms Vault* ability of [Hope’s End](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/Hope's_End), it cannot use its ability to place a structure that turn, as that happens at the end of the turn, after the tactical action has been resolved.
- Placing the structure will occur after the **Production** step. If the Winnu player places a Space Dock, they cannot produce out of it during the same action.
- If the Winnu player gains control of a planet with multiple Reclaimers, they may place a structure for each one, if they may do so without exceeding the maximum number of that structure allowed on a planet.*
- The Reclaimer need only be on the planet at the end of the tactical action the Winnu player gained it, not when the Winnu player gains control of it. This can occur from effects such as the *Integrated Economy* technology, or some exploration cards.*

## Imperator (Breakthrough)

- The Winnu player applies the +1 to their combat rolls even if it is the Winnu player’s own *Support for the Throne* in their opponent’s play area.*
- If their opponent receives a *Support for the Throne* during a combat, the Winnu player will apply +1 to the remaining combat rolls during that combat.*
