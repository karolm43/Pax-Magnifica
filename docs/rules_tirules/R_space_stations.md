## Rules Reference

There currently does not exist a Living Rules Reference section regarding space stations.

## Notes

- Several systems contain space stations.
- A player gains control of a space station when they are the only player with ships in that space station’s system.
    - If another player moves ships into that system, they will not gain control of that space station unless they win the resulting space combat.
    - If the player who controls the space station moves their ships out of the system, they retain control of that space station.
- A player does not lose control of a space station if a neutral ship is placed in its system, as there is no neutral player.
- A player whose ships are in a space station’s system with only neutral units gains control of that space station before resolving combat against those neutral units, as there is no neutral player.
- Each space station has a corresponding planet card. The space station may be exhausted for resources or influence as though it were a planet. The space station will be readied during the status phase as though it were a planet, or by other game effects that can ready planets.
    - Space stations are also readied at the end of the agenda phase.
- A player may exhaust a space station at any time to convert any commodities that they have into trade goods.
- For each space station a player controls, their commodity value is increased by one.
    - Players may place a space station commodity token in the commodity area of their faction sheet as a reminder of their increased commodity value.
- A player who controls one or more space stations may resolve transactions with other players who control one or more space stations.
    - Players who resolve transactions in this manner need not be neighbors.
- A player can exhaust space stations to cast votes. However, space stations are not planets, so they cannot be elected.
- Space stations are not planets, so attachments such as the *Nano–Forge* relic and the [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) player’s [*Terraform*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Terraform) promissory note cannot be attached to them.
- A space station is not a planet; controlling one does not prevent a player from being eliminated.
- Structures and ground forces cannot be committed to or placed on a space station.
- Space Stations do not count as planets for the purpose of scoring objectives, and a player may still score public objectives even if another player controls a space station in their home system.
- If a system tile contains a space station, but no planets, then a frontier token will be placed in that system during game setup.
