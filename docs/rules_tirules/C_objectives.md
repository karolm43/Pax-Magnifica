## Achieve Supremacy

- The flagship or war sun may be in the home system of an eliminated player.

## Adapt New Strategies

- A *Valefar Assimilator* technology will count if it is currently copying another player’s faction technology.

## Amass Wealth

- The influence, resources and trade goods must be spent during the status phase. Any that were spent during the action phase will have no effect.
- Trade goods may be spent as influence or resources when scoring *Amass Wealth*.

## Become a Legend

- The number of units in the system is irrelevant, if there is at least one.
- A ship, ground force, or structure will all qualify for *Become a Legend*.*
    - If one player has a ground force or structure on a planet in a qualifying system, and another player has a ship in the same system, both players may count the system toward the requirements of *Become a Legend*.*
    - A player may have ships in the system containing a legendary planet or Mecatol Rex without controlling the planet for that system to count toward the requirements of *Become a Legend*.*
- If Mirage is placed in an anomaly, or the *Nano–Forge* relic is attached to a planet in an anomaly, that system will only count as one system toward the requirements of *Become a Legend*.*
- A system containing a [Vuil’raith](https://www.tirules2.com/F_vuilraith) [Dimensional Tear](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Vuil'Raith_Cabal#:~:text=Dimensional%20Tear) is an anomaly.
    - If a Vuil’raith player’s Dimensional Tear is in a system containing another anomaly, a legendary planet, or Mecatol Rex, that system will only count as one system toward the requirements of *Become a Legend*.*

## Become a Martyr

- The lost planet may be in any player’s home system. It does not have to be a planet in the home system of the player scoring *Become a Martyr*.
    - The lost planet may be in the home system of an eliminated player.
- If the lost planet is the last planet a player controls, that player will be eliminated before they score *Become a Martyr*, if they meet the other requirements for elimination.
- If the player with the *Shard of the Throne* relic loses their home system, they will lose the *Shard of the Throne* card and the associated victory point before they may score *Become a Martyr*.
- A player who loses their home system due to *The Silver Flame* relic may score *Become a Martyr*.
- The attacker will have the opportunity to score a secret objective before the defender has the opportunity to score *Become a Martyr*.*

## Become the Gatekeeper

- A player with a ship in the wormhole nexus, or in any other system with both an alpha and beta wormhole, qualifies for *Become the Gatekeeper*.*
- Any ground forces or structures are irrelevant.*
- The [Creuss](https://www.tirules2.com/F_creuss) player’s [Quantum Entanglement](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ghosts_of_Creuss#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Quantum%20Entanglement) faction ability only makes alpha and beta wormhole systems adjacent; a player still needs ships in both a system containing an alpha wormhole and a system containing a beta wormhole.

## Betray a Friend

- Only promissory notes that specifically instruct a player to place them in their play area count for *Betray a Friend*. Another player having a player’s *Trade Agreement* promissory note does not count.
- The [Ul](https://www.tirules2.com/F_ul) player’s [*Terraform*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Titans_of_Ul#Faction_Promissory_Note:~:text=Terraform) promissory note counts for *Betray a Friend*; when it is attached to a planet, it counts as being in that planet owner’s play area.
- The defender cannot score *Betray a Friend*.*

- Units that are temporarily ships for the duration of a space combat, such as those made so by the [Nekro](https://www.tirules2.com/F_nekro) player’s flagship, [*The Alastor*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nekro_Virus#Flagship:~:text=The%20Alastor), or the [Naaz–Rokha](https://www.tirules2.com/F_naaz_rokha) player’s Z–Grav Eidolon mechs, may count toward scoring *Demonstrate Your Power*.*

However, fleet pool limits are also checked before scoring.*
    - The Mentak player cannot use the ability of their [*Salvage Operations*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mentak_Coalition#Faction_Technologies:~:text=Salvage%20Operations) technology until after the window in which to score *Demonstrate Your Power*.*

## Destroy Their Greatest Ship

- *Destroy Their Greatest Ship* is scored as soon as the war sun or flagship is destroyed; it does not require waiting until the end of the combat.
    - A player does not have to win a combat to score *Destroy Their Greatest Ship*.
    - A player may still only score one objective during or after each combat.
- If a player destroys their own flagship or war sun during combat, such as via the effect of *The Crown of Thalnos* relic, their opponent cannot score *Destroy Their Greatest Ship*.*

## Dictate Policy

- If there are three laws in play at the start of the agenda phase, a player may score *Dictate Policy* immediately.*
- *Dictate Policy* is scored after the third law resolves. As such, the *Deadly Plot* action card will prevent any player from scoring *Dictate Policy*.*
- *Dictate Policy* is scored before any rider action cards are resolved.*
- If the [*Political Data Nexus*](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Xxcha_Kingdom#Leaders:~:text=Political%20Data%20Nexus) [Xxcha](https://www.tirules2.com/F_xxcha) hero ability puts a third law in play during the action phase, *Dictate Policy* cannot be scored until the agenda phase.

## Spark a Rebellion

- A player that currently outright has the most victory points cannot score *Spark a Rebellion*.
- If multiple players are tied for the most victory points, *Spark a Rebellion* may be scored by winning a combat against any of them.
    - *Spark a Rebellion* may be scored by winning a combat against a player with zero victory points if all players have zero victory points.
- A player who wins a combat against the owner of the Shard of the Throne law, who has the most victory points, may both score *Spark a Rebellion* and gain Shard of the Throne.

## Stake Your Claim

- A player coexisting on a planet may score *Stake Your Claim* using that planet, even if it is the only planet in its system, as the planet is also controlled by another player.
- The player who controls a planet cannot use it to score *Stake Your Claim* because another player is coexisting on it, as the controlling player does not count a coexisting player as also controlling that planet.
- A planet that contains neutral infantry is not controlled by another player, so it cannot be used to score *Stake Your Claim*.*

- This card is replaced by the Ω version.

- If a player still has fighters in a system after assigning all hits produced during the **Space Cannon Offense** step, *Turn Their Fleets to Dust Ω* may be scored.*
- If a system contains only fighters, then *Turn Their Fleets to Dust Ω* cannot be scored by destroying them.*
- Scoring *Turn Their Fleets to Dust Ω* will not prevent that player from scoring a secret objective in the **Space Combat** step during this tactical action.
