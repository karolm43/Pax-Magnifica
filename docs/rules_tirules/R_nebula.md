# Nebula

## Rules Reference

A nebula is an anomaly that affects movement and combat.

- A ship can only move into a nebula if it is the active system.
    - A ship cannot move through a nebula. That is, a ship cannot move into and out of a nebula during the same movement.
- A ship that begins the **Movement** step of a tactical action in a nebula treats its move value as “1” for the duration of that step.
    - Other abilities and effects can increase this number.
- If a space combat occurs in a nebula, the defender applies +1 to each combat roll of their ships during that combat.

## Notes

- The movement restrictions of anomalies are intended to apply to all units, not only ships.
- Units are intended to be able to move into a nebula even when it is not the active system, but not through it.
- Ships can retreat into a nebula, as a nebula is intended to no longer need to be the active system for units to move into it.
- Movement bonuses, such as those from the *Gravity Drive* technology or the *Flank Speed* action card, are added to the move value of 1.
- The defender’s +1 applies only to combat rolls during space combat; like other [combat roll modifiers](https://www.tirules2.com/R_modifiers), it does not modify anti–fighter barrage, bombardment or space cannon rolls.
- The following effects change how a nebula affects movement:
    - The [Empyrean](https://www.tirules2.com/F_empyrean) player’s ships are not affected by nebulae when moving, due to their [`Voidborn`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Empyrean#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Voidborn) faction ability.*
    - The owner of the *Circlet of the Void* relic ignores the movement effects of nebulae.
    - The *Nav Suite* action card allows ships to move through a nebula, and ships moving out of a nebula keep their normal move value.*
    - The [Saar](https://www.tirules2.com/F_saar) player’s agent, [Captain Mendosa](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Clan_of_Saar#Leaders:~:text=Captain%20Mendosa), is treated as a bonus and overrides the nebula’s move value of 1.
    - If a [Vuil’raith](https://www.tirules2.com/F_vuilraith) Dimensional Tear is in a nebula, its +1 is applied after the nebula sets each ship’s move value to 1.*
