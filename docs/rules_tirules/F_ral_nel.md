## Survival Instinct

## Miniaturization

- Structures will be placed at the end of the tactical action. This will be after the production step, and so any space docks placed cannot use their `Production` ability during the same action.
- If the Ral Nel player’s ships leave a system or are destroyed, their structures in the space area remain there until they are blockaded, transported, or placed onto planets.

## Nano–Link Permit (Promissory Note)

- The moved structures may use their unit abilities during the tactical action that they are moved.*
- The structures are moved directly to the planet; they are not transported.*

## Nanomachines (Technology)

- When the Ral Nel player exhausts *Nanomachines*, they perform only one of the three component actions listed on the card.*
- If the Ral Nel player has no PDS in their reinforcements when performing the first ability, they may remove one from a system that does not contain one of their command tokens to place it on a planet they control.
- The Ral Nel player may perform the second ability even if they have no damaged units, or even if they have no units with the `Sustain Damage` ability on the game board.*
- The Ral Nel player must have at least one action card in their hand to perform the third ability.*
- When performing the third ability, the Ral Nel player chooses which card they will discard before seeing which card they will draw.*

## Linkship (Unit)

- Linkships cannot fire `Space Cannon` against adjacent units, even if the Ral Nel player has PDS II.
- If a space dock gains `Space Cannon` from the *Experimental Battlestation* action card, each Linkship II in the system may use that `Space Cannon` ability.
- Each Linkship II rolls one space cannon die, regardless of how many PDS are in its system.

## Last Dispatch (Flagship)

- The Ral Nel player chooses which ship is destroyed.
- A unit with `Sustain Damage` cannot be destroyed, even if it is damaged.
    - If a unit loses the `Sustain Damage` ability, such as a war sun by the effect of the *Publicize Weapon Schematics* agenda, then that unit may be destroyed with the ability of the *Last Dispatch*.
- If this ability destroys the last of the other player’s ships in the system, the Ral Nel player’s ships still retreat and the ability resolves.
- If the Ral Nel player plays the *Skilled Retreat* action card, they cannot resolve the ability of the *Last Dispatch*.
    - *Skilled Retreat* is not a retreat.

## Kan Kip Rel (Agent)

- The action card that the Ral Nel player gives to another player must be one of the two that they drew, and not another action card from their hand.*
- The ability of Kan Kip Rel resolves fully before the action card hand limit is checked.
- If the [Yssaril](https://www.tirules2.com/F_yssaril) player uses this ability, they draw three action cards instead of two due to their [`Scheming`](https://twilight-imperium.fandom.com/wiki/The_Yssaril_Tribes#Faction_Abilities:~:text=Scheming) faction ability. They discard one card from those drawn or from their hand, then take one of the drawn cards and give another to another player, keeping the third if it was not discarded.

## Watchful Ojz (Commander)

- Watchful Ojz can only be used if the Ral Nel player has a valid retreat path for the moved ships (separate from the system the commander allows them to retreat to).
- The destination system may contain a planet controlled by another player, which may also have that player’s ground forces on it.*
- Watchful Ojz is a “when” ability. It resolves before the *Intercept* action card is played and resolved.

## Director Nel — Signal Intrusion (Hero)

- *Signal Intrusion* may be triggered by the Ral Nel player themself passing.*
- The Ral Nel player may take any number of turns after unpassing, before they pass again, which will end the action phase.*
- When the Ral Nel player passes again, they may score the *Prove Endurance* secret objective.*
- Purging Director Nel does not prevent the active player from scoring the *Prove Endurance* objective. The active player scores *Prove Endurance* when they pass, then the Ral Nel player purges their hero.

## Alarum (Mech)

- Ground forces may only be moved from adjacent systems, not from within the active system.
- If an Alarum moves another Alarum into the system, the newly moved Alarum cannot use its ability to move additional ground forces.

## Data Skimmer (Breakthrough)

- An action card will be placed on *Data Skimmer* if it is discarded after being played or canceled, if it is discarded due to hand size limits, or if it is discarded for another game effect.*
- Action cards discarded by the Ral Nel player are placed in the action card discard pile, and not on *Data Skimmer*.*
- Action cards on *Data Skimmer* are not in the discard pile.
- If the Ral Nel player is eliminated, *Data Skimmer* is returned to the game box, and all action cards on it are immediately discarded.
